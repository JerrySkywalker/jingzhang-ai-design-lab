# Contributing

This repository is an open design laboratory. Contributions are welcome when they improve the evidence, completeness, falsifiability, or urban-design quality of the work.

## Useful contributions

- corrections to official-requirement interpretation;
- primary-source discovery with provenance and reuse terms;
- benchmark comparisons based on inspectable submissions;
- critiques of candidate concepts;
- urban-design, architecture, transport, landscape, municipal, operations, AI, robotics, or systems-engineering analysis;
- reproducible calculations and clearly licensed diagrams.

## Contribution rules

1. Distinguish facts, derivations, assumptions and concepts.
2. Link primary sources where possible.
3. Do not submit private, restricted, personal, or uncleared spatial data.
4. Do not imply official approval or implementation certainty.
5. Do not copy another participant's work without compatible licensing and attribution.
6. Keep the distinction between this design lab and the future formal submission repository explicit.

Substantial conceptual changes should explain the trade-off being changed and update the decision log when appropriate.
