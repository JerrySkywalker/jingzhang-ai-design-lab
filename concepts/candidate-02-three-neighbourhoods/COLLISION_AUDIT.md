## Word substitution test

Replacing “neighbourhood” with “living yard,” “local unit,” “belonging community” or “complete district” does not create a new proposition. Candidate 02 earns a distinct position only if all of the following are visible in maps and programme:

1. three independently complete catchments rather than repeated nodes on a corridor;
2. different spatial and economic models for Making, Learning and Metropolitan neighbourhoods;
3. a many-to-many federation rather than a north-to-south user journey or production chain;
4. the heritage park as a shared common but not the only public-space organiser;
5. ordinary daily services and non-talent users changing land use, ground floors and phasing.

## Abandoned direction

An early Candidate 02 direction used a cross-weave/cross-streets structure. It was rejected before Candidate numbering because Jingzhang Cross Streets, Jingzhang Crossings, Jingzhang Mending Belt and Local City Units already occupy this design territory with greater specificity. Renaming it would have failed the collision test.

## Current decision

Candidate 02 is worth retaining for comparison, not yet for selection. It offers a clear anti-corridor hypothesis and a complete-city test, but its conceptual territory is crowded. It should be killed if verified site work cannot produce three materially different neighbourhood forms or if its federation cannot be distinguished from existing “stay and belong” proposals.
