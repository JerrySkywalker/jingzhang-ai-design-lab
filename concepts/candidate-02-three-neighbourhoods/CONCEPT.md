# Candidate 02 — 京张三邻 / Three Neighbourhoods Jingzhang

**Status:** Under Evaluation

**Version:** architecture exploration v0.1

**Not the selected proposal. Not a formal submission.**

## Evidence contract

- **FACT:** The official working brief defines three scales of approximately 43.6 km², 11.4 km² and 368.4 ha, and names Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community and Dazhongsi as the three key areas.
- **FACT:** The canonical repository still reports exact official polygons and statutory planning controls as missing. The current polygons are provisional and are not redlines, parcel boundaries or an implementation basis.
- **ASSUMPTION:** The three key areas are embedded in materially different urban contexts and can be developed as complete neighbourhoods. Existing population, use, ownership and facility baselines remain unverified.
- **CONCEPT:** All spatial structures, project families and operating mechanisms in this candidate are propositions for later professional and public deepening.

## A. Core theme

### Chinese working name

**京张三邻**

“三邻” means three complete urban neighbourhoods that are close enough to collaborate but complete enough not to depend on a ceremonial corridor for daily life.

### English working name

**Three Neighbourhoods Jingzhang**

### One-sentence proposition

把一条容易被做成九公里展示走廊的 AI 创新带，重组为三座能够独立工作、居住、学习、照护、交往和休息的完整创新邻区，并以京张遗产公园、轨道交通和三区两翼服务网络维持邻区之间的协作。

### First-principles design judgment

**A world-class innovation ecosystem is first a set of complete urban neighbourhoods, not a sequence of specialised campuses attached to a branded line.**

The key urban question is not how many innovation nodes can be placed along the railway. It is whether a researcher, service worker, resident, child, older person or visitor can complete an ordinary day within each key area while still reaching the wider innovation network.

This leads to four design rules:

1. each key area must contain a credible daily-life mix, not only its official specialist function;
2. the three areas collaborate as parallel neighbourhood economies rather than as a single linear production pipeline;
3. the Jing-Zhang heritage park is a shared cultural and ecological common, not the only organising spine;
4. AI services are embedded in ordinary neighbourhood institutions and retain non-AI access.

### Why this belongs to Jing-Zhang

This is not a generic “15-minute AI district” for four reasons.

- **FACT:** The task already identifies three differentiated areas and two external wings. The candidate reverses their usual hierarchy: the three areas become full urban places, while the belt becomes the connection among them.
- **FACT:** The brief requires the Jing-Zhang heritage park, higher-education and research relationships, AI industry, transit, community life and three distinct detailed-design areas to be handled together.
- **CONCEPT:** A long north–south heritage geography can otherwise encourage a thin corridor with repeated nodes. “Three Neighbourhoods” deliberately accepts local difference and makes the park a shared inheritance between places.
- **ASSUMPTION:** Existing campuses, communities, commercial streets and station areas provide enough reusable fabric to support three different neighbourhood models. This must be tested against official geometry, building inventory and field observation.

### Fundamental difference from Candidate 01

| Dimension | Candidate 01 — Re-Embodied Jingzhang | Candidate 02 — Three Neighbourhoods |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First question | How can heritage infrastructure acquire new public and embodied functions? | Can each key area function as a complete piece of city before it performs a specialist AI role? |
| Primary form | One adaptive civic spine, three engines, two networks, distributed stations | Three complete neighbourhoods, one shared heritage common, two external service wings |
| Innovation logic | A visible research–test–service–feedback metabolism | Three parallel local ecosystems that exchange people, knowledge and services |
| Public realm | Continuous civic infrastructure spine | A distinct civic centre and five-minute public-space web in each neighbourhood |

Candidate 02 fails if its three neighbourhoods are only Candidate 01’s three cores with extra housing and amenities.

## B. Three-scale proposition

### 43.6 km² — a federation of innovation neighbourhoods

The coordinated research area is organised as a federation, not a corridor hierarchy:

- Zhongzhiyuan neighbourhood connects full-stack research and engineering to a complete working and living environment;
- AI Origin neighbourhood connects universities, research, entrepreneurship, learning and intergenerational daily life;
- Dazhongsi neighbourhood connects metropolitan arrival, enterprise services, culture, commerce and ordinary urban life;
- Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing supplies professional services, capital, IP, recruitment and international links;
- Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing supplies public-life settings, service feedback and carefully governed trials;
- wider Haidian and regional partners connect through research, standards, talent, supply chains and public problem briefs without being represented as committed partners.

The three neighbourhoods should be able to originate projects independently. Collaboration is many-to-many: a team need not pass through all three areas in a fixed order.

### 11.4 km² — three neighbourhoods, one common, two wings

The diagrammable structure is:

## **三邻 · 一园 · 两翼 · 多联**

- **三邻:** three complete innovation neighbourhoods, each with a civic centre, mixed daily functions, a local walking web and a different urban form.
- **一园:** the Jing-Zhang heritage park as shared cultural memory, blue-green public common and inter-neighbourhood route; it does not absorb every activity.
- **两翼:** west/east service interfaces corresponding to technology-service and scenario-empowerment relationships.
- **多联:** existing transit, walking, cycling, service and ecological connections, to be located only after verified base data and fieldwork.

This structure can be drawn as three overlapping local catchments linked by transit and the common park, rather than three beads on a dominant line.

### 368.4 ha — three complete but unequal prototypes

Each key area has the same completeness test but a different primary identity:

1. **Zhongzhiyuan — Making Neighbourhood:** research, engineering, testing, repair, learning, food, rest and worker services.
2. **AI Origin — Learning Neighbourhood:** university–city exchange, entrepreneurship, housing-support interfaces, care, public learning and local services.
3. **Dazhongsi — Metropolitan Neighbourhood:** arrival, business exchange, culture, commerce, events, repair and everyday station-area life.

“Complete” does not mean identical land-use ratios. It means that no area treats ordinary life as an externality.

## Complete-design capability

Candidate 02 is intended to cover:

- three spatial scales and the three-areas/two-wings framework;
- innovation ecosystem and regional collaboration;
- land use, mixed functions and conceptual development controls;
- retain, renovate, reuse, selectively add and only conditionally replace;
- buildings, ground floors, urban form and character;
- rail, street, walking, cycling, parking, curb and logistics;
- municipal, public-service and new infrastructure;
- Jing-Zhang heritage park, blue-green links and local public spaces;
- all three key areas at differentiated detailed-design depth;
- at least 10 AI scenarios, 3 tests and 5 personas;
- culture, landmarks, visual identity, events and long-term operations;
- project list, phases, evidence gaps and kill criteria.

## Current value and current danger

The candidate’s value is urban clarity: it asks whether the project creates three real places rather than one branded strip. Its danger is familiarity. “Complete neighbourhood,” “talent retention,” “living yard” and “local urban unit” are already present in strong submissions. Candidate 02 should continue only if it can demonstrate a sharper, site-specific difference through verified facility gaps, local urban-form prototypes and a non-linear collaboration model.
