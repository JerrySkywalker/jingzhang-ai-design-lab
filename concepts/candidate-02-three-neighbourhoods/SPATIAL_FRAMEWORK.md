# Candidate 02 Spatial Framework v0.1

This is a conceptual spatial framework. Exact boundaries, street alignments, parcels, buildings, controls and infrastructure capacities remain pending authoritative data.

## 1. The 43.6 km² federation

### World-class AI ecosystem

The ecosystem is organised around three complete neighbourhoods plus shared regional services.

| Resource | Federation mechanism | Spatial expression | Evidence gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Universities and research | joint problem studios, visiting teams and shared review | learning rooms, review halls and routes between campus edges and neighbourhood centres | verified institutional interfaces and access conditions |
| Enterprises | distributed team locations and shared test/service access | adaptable workspaces in all three neighbourhoods, with more controlled facilities in Zhongzhiyuan | enterprise and building inventory |
| Capital and professional services | periodic clinics and permanent service desks through the technology-service wing | Dazhongsi and AI Origin enterprise-service frontages, plus distributed appointments | no partner or funding commitments assumed |
| Talent | arrival, work, learning, care and long-term daily support | different dwelling-support, food, health, recreation and social interfaces in every neighbourhood | population, housing and service baseline |
| Compute and data | shared service access rather than a citywide visible device network | controlled back-of-house infrastructure and public help desks | capacity, security and operator unknown |
| Scenarios | neighbourhood problem briefs and bounded testing | local service sites and controlled test rooms | permissions, insurance and operating responsibility |

The federation connects to wider Haidian through academic, enterprise and service networks; to Beijing innovation areas through research and test partnerships; and to regional networks through standards, supply chains and events. These are **CONCEPT** interfaces, not existing agreements.

### Three areas and two wings

The two wings do not terminate in one central spine. They serve each neighbourhood differently:

- Zhongguancun Technology Service Wing supplies specialised services to all three neighbourhoods through scheduled and permanent interfaces.
- Xiaoyuehe Scenario Empowerment Wing supplies public problems, observation and feedback, while protecting ordinary residents from compulsory testing.
- Cross-neighbourhood projects can originate in any area and call on either wing.

## 2. The 11.4 km² structure

## **三邻 · 一园 · 两翼 · 多联**

### Three neighbourhoods

Each neighbourhood has four nested spatial layers:

1. **Civic centre:** an ordinary public anchor combining service, learning, meeting and rest.
2. **Five-minute web:** a walkable local network connecting daily services, public space, transit interfaces and work/learning locations.
3. **Productive edge:** controlled or serviceable spaces for research, enterprise, logistics and testing.
4. **Quiet interior:** housing, education, care, ecological or other sensitive uses protected from event and logistics pressure.

These layers are relational design rules, not mapped parcels.

### One shared heritage common

The Jing-Zhang heritage park performs four shared roles:

- public memory and interpretation;
- inter-neighbourhood walking and cycling;
- ecological continuity and climate comfort;
- selected events and encounters that benefit from a common setting.

It is not required to host every AI service, every landmark or the main daily centre of each neighbourhood. This prevents public life from being thinned into a single strip.

### Two wing interfaces

Wing interfaces are placed at neighbourhood centres or productive edges according to the service:

- legal, IP, finance, recruitment and international support should be easy to find but need not occupy the park;
- scenario-opening, community problem briefs and evaluation should happen near the affected users and spaces;
- compute, secure data and heavy service functions stay in controlled buildings or service edges.

### Multiple links

Five link families must be mapped later:

- rail and public-transport links between neighbourhoods;
- ordinary walking and cycling links within each neighbourhood;
- verified crossings to the heritage common;
- blue-green links to existing water and green systems;
- service/logistics links that avoid primary civic spaces.

No new bridge, tunnel, road redline or rail alignment is claimed.

## 3. Land-use strategy

### Neighbourhood completeness test

Every neighbourhood must show a credible relationship among:

- research, enterprise or innovation work;
- public services and learning;
- food, retail and non-consumption rest;
- housing-support and care interfaces;
- culture and recreation;
- green/open space;
- transit and logistics.

This is not a fixed percentage formula. Once official land use and building data are available, a professional team should define catchments, quantify deficits and test alternatives.

### Distribution logic

- Zhongzhiyuan may carry the highest share of controlled R&D, engineering and test space, but it must also support ordinary worker life and public learning.
- AI Origin may carry the strongest mix of learning, entrepreneurship, community service and housing-support functions.
- Dazhongsi may carry the strongest metropolitan exchange, enterprise service, culture, commerce and evening activity.
- Sensitive housing, schools, medical/care functions and habitat areas receive low-disturbance edges in all three.

## 4. Building and urban-form strategy

### Retain and adapt first

Conceptual sequence:

**survey → retain useful fabric → repair access and ground floors → adapt/reuse → add reversible or infill space → consider replacement only after professional proof**

No existing building is assigned a final action in v0.1.

### Massing direction

- form local centres through coherent street walls, courtyards and visible entrances rather than isolated towers;
- concentrate activity near verified transit and service centres while protecting quiet interiors;
- step massing and activity down toward heritage, ecological and residential edges;
- use adaptable lower floors and shared rooms to support changing innovation uses;
- treat roofs, servicing and loading as part of urban character, not residual engineering space.

Heights, FAR, density and exact setbacks remain unknown.

## 5. Public-space system

Candidate 02 uses a **polycentric public realm**:

- one civic centre in each neighbourhood;
- a network of smaller courtyards, gardens, streets, station forecourts and indoor public rooms;
- the heritage park as a shared common;
- ordinary services distributed so that public life does not depend on events or consumption.

Each civic centre must work on a normal weekday with AI and events switched off.

## 6. Blue-green system

The blue-green system combines:

- the verified heritage-park landscape;
- local shade and rainwater management within each five-minute web;
- connections to Qinghe, Xiaoyuehe and other existing systems only after authoritative or cleared mapping;
- quiet habitat and low-light zones separated from intensive events;
- visible maintenance access and seasonal management.

The candidate does not assume hydrological connections or engineering capacity from names alone.

## 7. Mobility, parking and logistics

### People

- public transport first for inter-neighbourhood movement;
- walking and accessibility first within neighbourhoods;
- continuous, legible cycling connections with secure parking;
- safe station-to-neighbourhood arrival sequences;
- paper/static wayfinding and staffed help remain available.

### Vehicles and parking

- reduce unnecessary through-traffic in civic centres where verified feasible;
- manage pick-up, drop-off and deliveries by place and time;
- locate shared parking and loading at productive/service edges where later traffic evidence supports it;
- never place automated-vehicle spectacle ahead of pedestrian continuity.

### Logistics

- Zhongzhiyuan needs the strongest separation between public and technical logistics;
- AI Origin needs small-scale servicing that does not disrupt quiet residential and learning uses;
- Dazhongsi needs tightly managed curb, event and commercial deliveries around transit.

## 8. AI scenario spatial system

AI is organised by neighbourhood need, not by a dedicated citywide embodied network:

**ordinary task → responsible local institution → physical service point → minimum data → human path → bounded AI enhancement → review and withdrawal**

Scenario points cluster at:

- civic centres for public service and learning;
- productive edges for controlled tests;
- transit interfaces for arrival and access;
- heritage/public-space locations only when the service adds public value without displacing ordinary use.

## 9. Urban-renewal logic

The candidate is advanced through missing-function projects rather than a single megaproject:

1. diagnose each neighbourhood’s daily-life and innovation gaps;
2. repair walking, access, public-service and ground-floor discontinuities;
3. reuse rooms and buildings to close the most important gaps;
4. test local operations over different times and user groups;
5. add permanent construction only where reuse and service changes are insufficient;
6. compare all three neighbourhoods annually so specialisation does not create new dependence or exclusion.

## 10. Drawing test

A future diagram passes only if it shows:

- three spatially complete catchments, not three dots;
- different centres and urban-form types;
- the park as shared common but not sole organiser;
- transit, local walking, wing-service and ecological relationships as different line types;
- sensitive interiors and service edges;
- provisional/unknown geometry clearly separated from design intent.
