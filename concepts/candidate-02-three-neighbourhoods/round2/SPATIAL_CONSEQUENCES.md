# Candidate 02 Round-2 Spatial Consequences

## Required structural rewrite

Until H3 is evidenced, the 11.4 km² plan should not draw three complete-neighbourhood circles. The defensible working structure is:

```text
one continuous heritage / public / blue-green corridor
+ three official strategic and detailed-design projects
+ cross-corridor seams and station interfaces
+ N ordinary-day units whose boundaries are evidence-gated
+ a bounded specialist-service federation
```

This is a major rewrite of Candidate 02, not a cosmetic correction.

## 43.6 km² relationship

At the research scale, completeness is not self-sufficiency. Universities, labs, enterprises, capital, compute, specialist health/education and global exchange remain regional networks. The belt's units should expose which dependencies are local, shared along the belt or regional. No arrow means a partnership or service exists.

Federation has spatial meaning only when specialist capacities are intentionally unequal and accessible, while ordinary daily essentials are locally evidenced. Three official projects can remain high-value nodes even if the number of ordinary units is two, four or more.

## 11.4 km² structure

### Primary bone

The heritage-park/public corridor provides continuous walking, cycling, culture, shade/refuge and blue-green continuity, subject to real evidence. It is not itself a substitute for local services and must not become a forced commute between essential needs.

### Cross-corridor seams

Candidate units should cross rather than merely face the corridor where real routes, public interfaces and uses support it. Barriers, roads, tracks, campuses, waterways and private edges must be mapped before a seam is claimed.

### Unit boundary test

Each proposed unit boundary must contain or explicitly fail the same eight-persona/seven-state contract. A unit is redrawn, merged or subdivided when ordinary chains cross its edge systematically. Unit count follows results rather than branding.

### Federation layer

Only specialist or legitimately scale-dependent services may be shared. Every shared service records access mode, hours, capacity hypothesis, non-digital route, closed-facility fallback, labour and responsible operator. Federation is many-to-many when at least two-way exchanges occur without making one unit the permanent supplier and the others dependants.

## Land use and buildings

- Do not infer mix from colour percentages or proximity; map physical places and eligibility/hours.
- Put affordable food, toilets, refuge, human help and worker support into accessible ground floors before iconic innovation venues.
- Housing/care claims require official and survey evidence; do not add fictional residents to pass the contract.
- Retain/adapt/new-build decisions remain building-survey dependent.
- Specialist labs and enterprise spaces may concentrate, but their service labour and public edges must be designed.
- If basic completeness requires major demolition or unknown policy changes, redraw the unit rather than manufacture a new town.

## Mobility

- test actual walk, wheelchair, cycle and transit chains across weekday/evening/weekend and weather states;
- distinguish straight-line distance from route/time/access;
- treat station access and Dazhongsi geometry as blocked by evidence;
- provide ordinary fixed information and staffed fallback when digital navigation fails;
- separate event flows, logistics and worker access from everyday refuge and accessible routes.

## Public and blue-green space

Civic rooms are useful only if they are public, open at the relevant time, non-commercial, non-app-gated and not the only facility serving multiple needs. Redundant small rooms/refuges may be more robust than one centre.

The corridor's living systems come before catchment branding. Unit lines cannot fragment habitat, drainage or shade responsibility. No ecological performance is claimed without survey evidence.

## Phasing

1. **Evidence phase:** official/cleared geometry, facility/hours register, route/field audit and contract run for H2/H3/H4+/corridor.
2. **Repair phase:** reversible improvements to missing daily chains—hours, staffed help, toilets, refuge, worker access, crossings—without declaring permanent unit boundaries.
3. **Unit decision:** professional team selects or rejects the unit count and draws land/building/public/mobility consequences.
4. **Federation pilot:** share one specialist service with a local fallback and one-facility-down exercise.
5. **Long-term renewal:** only after ordinary-day evidence and controls are stable.

## What must be drawn

- hypothesis map comparing H2/H3/H4+/corridor on the same base;
- eight-persona service-chain maps with hours and external dependencies;
- weekday/evening/weekend/weather/event/failure overlays;
- ground-floor and worker/logistics sections;
- local-versus-federated service matrix;
- blue-green/canopy/refuge and barrier map;
- three official key-area plans that do not pretend their boundaries are catchments.
