# Candidate 02 — Three Key Areas Stress Test

## Rule

The three official areas remain mandatory detailed-design subjects. This test does not assume they are the boundaries of three complete neighbourhoods. Each must face a different ordinary-day failure and demonstrate a different spatial response.

## Zhongzhiyuan — workday-enclave stress

### Official/contextual role

Full-stack autonomous innovation, standards/safety governance and a garden-style innovation district are official directions. They do not prove housing or daily services.

### Failure question

Can researchers, service workers, maintenance workers, disabled users and visitors complete arrival, affordable food, toilets, shelter/refuge, help and worker-support chains in evenings, weekends and bad weather?

### Required spatial consequences

- public/worker ground-floor route outside controlled labs;
- affordable food and toilets with evidenced hours;
- non-commercial all-weather refuge and staffed/non-digital orientation;
- service access, storage and worker rest that do not cross the civic route;
- controlled tests remain separate from ordinary public space;
- garden claims yield to canopy/soil/drainage evidence.

### Key uncertainty

Whether any daily unit includes nearby residential/care uses or whether Zhongzhiyuan is properly a specialized anchor within a larger cross-corridor unit.

## AI Origin — campus-access stress

### Official/contextual role

Near-campus innovation, transfer, residential life and campus–park links are official directions. Campus resources cannot be counted as public without access and hours evidence.

### Failure question

Can students, residents/families, older adults, disabled users, service workers and visitors complete ordinary chains without campus credentials, an app or participation in an innovation programme?

### Required spatial consequences

- genuinely public civic/learning ground floors on ordinary routes;
- non-account help, toilets, refuge and easy-read orientation;
- childcare/basic-care claims only where evidenced or explicitly external;
- everyday crossing and public-space repair before programme branding;
- small shared learning/work rooms that remain useful without AI.

### Key uncertainty

Real public/private boundaries, residential/service baseline and campus–park crossings.

## Dazhongsi — event/commercial/TOD stress

### Official/contextual role

AI-native business, urban industry, station-city integration and four-quadrant walking are official directions. Exact station/key-area relationships are not established by current provisional geometry.

### Failure question

Do ordinary residents/workers/visitors retain access, food, toilets, refuge, help and service logistics during evenings, weekends, events, one-facility closure and digital outage?

### Required spatial consequences

- continuous accessible station/street route once geometry is verified;
- non-commercial refuge and public toilets not displaced by events;
- affordable and non-digital service alongside premium/AI commerce;
- back-of-house logistics, waste, maintenance and worker support;
- event overlay with ordinary-day space explicitly protected;
- staffed fallback and fixed orientation.

### Key uncertainty

Authoritative boundary, station quadrants, road crossings, ownership, operating hours and actual programme.
