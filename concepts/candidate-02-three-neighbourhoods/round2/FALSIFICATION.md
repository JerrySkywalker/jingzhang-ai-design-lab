# Candidate 02 Round-2 Falsification

## Genericity test

**Result: FAIL in its Round-1 form.**

Replacing Jing-Zhang with “three AI parks” leaves the formula—complete daily life, civic room, short walking web, mixed use, many-to-many federation—substantially intact. The official three-area tasks make the contexts different, but C02 previously promoted that required difference into evidence of three complete neighbourhoods.

Survival requires the heritage/public corridor, actual cross-corridor barriers, real service chains and distinct building/operating conditions to determine the units. These inputs are not yet available.

## Repeatability test

The inherited area designs risk repeating:

```text
civic centre
+ five-minute web
+ mixed use
+ ordinary-day programme
```

with different names. Round 2 replaces this repetition with area-specific failure questions:

- Zhongzhiyuan: can a research/production district support shift workers, daily services and public refuge outside work hours?
- AI Origin: can campus/innovation interfaces be genuinely public and support residents/families without assuming campus access?
- Dazhongsi: can an urban/station district protect ordinary services, workers and refuge from events, commercial pressure and logistics?

If these produce the same plan or all depend on a remote shared civic facility, C02 fails.

## AI-removal test

Removing all AI leaves a potentially valuable complete-city programme; that is allowed. But the AI innovation-belt identity then needs a non-decorative mechanism:

- daily service deficits become research/test questions only after the physical service baseline exists;
- each unit can host different research, adoption and public-use relationships;
- shared specialist infrastructure and knowledge move across units, but ordinary toilets, refuge, food, help and maintenance are not exported;
- AI failure is one of seven operating states in the completeness contract.

This still collides with human-city and everyday-life competitors. AI is not yet a distinct urban-form generator for C02.

## Industry test

Local completeness may consume programme and attention without strengthening full-stack AI. A surviving federation must distinguish:

- **local daily base:** services required by people and maintenance in each actual unit;
- **specialized innovation capacity:** research, controlled validation, enterprise growth, adoption and exchange that may be unequal and shared;
- **regional dependencies:** universities, capital, compute and specialist facilities that should not be falsely localized.

No unit must duplicate every lab or business service. Conversely, industry ambition cannot justify removing housing/care/food/refuge evidence from workers' and residents' ordinary days.

## Federation test

Round-1 arrows do not prove federation. A real many-to-many federation would change:

- location and capacity of redundant local services;
- which specialist ground floors are shared and what happens when they close;
- cross-unit walking/cycle/transit routes and accessible/non-digital transfer;
- opening hours, staffing, maintenance and event calendars;
- mutual-aid rules and bounded fallback capacity;
- phasing, so no unit waits years for another unit's basic service.

The synthetic experiment shows a `federation_dependent_unit` fails the local contract when its daily services are assigned to an external shared hub. Thus “shared” is not automatically complete.

## Strongest kill argument

The number three was derived from the taskbook, not from urban-life evidence, and almost every visible ingredient is occupied. Current geometry cannot support catchment work. Without field/service/professional proof, the concept is an attractive renaming of three required key areas and should not receive full professional design budget.
