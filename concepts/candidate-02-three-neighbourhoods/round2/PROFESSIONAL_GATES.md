# Candidate 02 — Professional and Human Gates

## CAN_BE_DONE_BY_AGENT_NOW

- maintain the exact proposal/Issue collision record;
- run and improve the synthetic completeness contract;
- compare H2/H3/H4+/corridor under one explicit logic when cleared data appear;
- record assumptions, failures, external dependencies and required drawings;
- generate field-observation templates that avoid personal data.

## CAN_BE_DONE_BY_OWNER

Core/high-value contribution:

- formalize service-dependency and failure-mode logic;
- test federation as a resilient network rather than branding;
- design structured, bounded observation protocols and evidence schemas;
- model one-facility, digital and shared-service failure without inventing availability;
- build reproducible comparison tooling for unit hypotheses.

Supporting contribution:

- personally observe selected public routes/services at ordinary times without inferring population or community preference;
- implement machine-readable facilities/hours/chain registers after sources are cleared;
- help professional teams keep AI trials bounded and reversible.

Weak fit:

- independently defining neighbourhood boundaries, land use, building renewal, landscape/ecology, social need or community consent.

## REQUIRES_FIELD_OBSERVATION

- weekday/evening/weekend and rain/heat route conditions;
- whether apparent public places are accessible, staffed and usable without purchase/account;
- actual service-worker/maintenance routes, rest, storage, waste and loading;
- one bounded sample per hypothesis before generalization;
- observation is not a population survey or a promise of service availability.

## REQUIRES_URBAN_DESIGN_PROFESSIONAL

- define and compare unit boundaries from morphology, use, public realm and service chains;
- translate failures into land, ground-floor, massing, renewal and phasing choices;
- make the three official areas different without assuming they are catchments;
- determine whether the evidence supports two, three, four-plus or corridor-plus-units.

## REQUIRES_TRANSPORT

- accessible walking/wheelchair/cycle/transit times and barriers;
- station/key-area relationships, especially Dazhongsi;
- evening/weekend service, cross-unit transfer and failed-facility fallback;
- event, curb, delivery, waste and maintenance effects.

## REQUIRES_LANDSCAPE

- living assets, soil, drainage, canopy, habitat and seasonal comfort baseline;
- continuous corridor responsibilities across unit lines;
- route-level shade/shelter/refuge and event recovery.

## REQUIRES_BUILDING_SURVEY

- actual uses, public/private access, hours, structure, adaptability and service space;
- whether ground floors can support daily services without major demolition;
- retain/adapt/new-build decisions and worker facilities.

## REQUIRES_OFFICIAL_DATA

- authoritative overall/key-area geometry and controls;
- roads, station, public-space, blue-green, heritage and management layers;
- facilities, population/employment/visitor baselines and operating data where legitimately public/cleared;
- provisional geometry remains non-authorizing.

## REQUIRES_COMMUNITY_PROCESS

- real ordinary-day needs, exclusions and unwanted interventions;
- whose “completeness” is being defined and by whom;
- acceptable external dependencies and mutual-aid expectations;
- family, older/disabled people, service workers and existing residents/workers must not be represented by synthetic personas alone.

## Minimal next professional evidence

Before full design, commission one short, shared-base test comparing H2/H3/H4+/corridor: cleared geometry/facility register, bounded multi-time route observation, accessibility/transport review and an urban designer's consequence sketch. If H3 does not clearly outperform, retire “three neighbourhoods” rather than fund its illustration.
