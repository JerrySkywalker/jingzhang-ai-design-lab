## NON_NEGOTIABLE_SPATIAL_CHANGES

- Map shade/shelter/refuge along chains, not only at three centres.
- Add maintenance/service-worker spaces and logistics to every area brief.
- Preserve continuous ecological/drainage responsibility across any unit line.
- Build redundancy for basic services before shared/federated specialist services.
- Protect ordinary public space during events and technology failure.

## EVIDENCE_GAPS_NOT_DESIGN_LICENSES

- official/cleared unit and key-area geometry;
- living assets, soil, canopy, drainage and habitat baseline;
- actual facilities, access eligibility, hours and responsible operators;
- seasonal and accessible routes;
- service/logistics/event operations and fallback capacity.
