# Candidate 02 — Why Exactly Three?

## Result

The Round-1 inference `three key areas = three complete neighbourhoods` is not supported. Official evidence supports three differentiated strategic/detail-design objects; it does not establish three independent ordinary-day catchments.

C02_KILL_PRESSURE=CRITICAL

## H3 — three independent but connected urban units

### EVIDENCE_FOR_THREE

- `FACT:` the official announcement requires three areas and gives different north/middle/south design tasks and approximate areas;
- `FACT:` the taskbook assigns different innovation roles to Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin and Dazhongsi;
- `FACT:` official public narrative describes three areas and two wings along a north-south heritage-park setting;
- `CONTEXTUAL/DERIVED:` public OSM station points suggest a north-to-south sequence, but do not define catchments.

This evidence justifies three detailed projects or anchors. It does not prove that each contains a complete daily service chain.

### EVIDENCE_AGAINST_THREE

- no official population, employment, facilities, hours, service capacity or catchment evidence;
- no authoritative overall/key-area polygons;
- official-repository Issues raise material provisional-geometry warnings for the park relationship and Dazhongsi location;
- north-south continuity and east-west connection are stronger official structural requirements than neighbourhood independence;
- the north-middle distance pattern may not align with equal or even comparable everyday units;
- mature competitors already map three prescribed areas to three courts/yards/rooms/stations;
- a nine-local-unit competitor shows that finer-grained H4+ is plausible.

### UNKNOWN

- which physical services and users belong inside each potential unit;
- real walking/wheelchair/transit reach at relevant times and weather;
- whether key-area boundaries overlap daily catchments or cut across them;
- local capacity under event, digital outage or one-facility failure;
- whether a shared/federated service is actually reachable and open.

### WHAT_DATA_WOULD_DECIDE

- cleared geometry and facility/hours register;
- seven-state `NEIGHBOURHOOD_COMPLETENESS_CONTRACT` run for eight roles;
- route audits and professional transport/public-realm analysis;
- identical evaluation of H2/H3/H4+/corridor, with failed roles/states exposed rather than averaged;
- a drawn consequence test for land, ground floor, public space, mobility, service labour and phasing.

## H2 — two urban units

H2 remains plausible. A large northern innovation/service unit and a closer central-southern urban unit are one possible reading, but choosing that merge from straight-line station distances would be speculation. H2 must be evaluated with the same contract rather than used as a convenient fallback.

H2_STATUS=PLAUSIBLE_UNTESTED

## H4+ — more local units

The 11.4 km² range could contain multiple smaller cross-corridor daily units, while the official three areas remain strategic projects. Open PR #1455 already explores nine cross-line local units. Competition precedent is not site proof, but it defeats the claim that three is self-evident.

H4_PLUS_STATUS=PLAUSIBLE_UNTESTED

## Corridor hypothesis

Official language, the heritage-park brief and public station context make a continuous corridor the strongest current baseline. A corridor can connect strategic anchors and many local units without declaring itself the provider of all daily services.

CORRIDOR_STATUS=BEST_SUPPORTED_BASELINE_NOT_COMPLETE_ANSWER

## Consequence

Candidate 02 cannot advance under its existing title as a proven “three neighbourhood” plan. It may survive only as an evidence-gated polycentric hypothesis:

```text
continuous heritage/public corridor
+ three prescribed strategic projects
+ N ordinary-day units determined by evidence
+ bounded many-to-many federation
```

If the Owner requires the number three to remain a first principle without new evidence, C02 should be killed.
