# C01 → C02: AI / System Distinctiveness Review

## Gate

A proposal for an AI innovation belt must identify a spatial/operating consequence that ordinary mixed-use, fifteen-minute or community planning does not already supply. AI may be removable from basic public service, but research, validation, production and accountable adoption must still form a distinctive, testable system.

## Verdict

C02 currently fails this gate. Its strongest urban qualities survive AI removal, while its AI ecosystem remains a programme overlay and its federation remains unproved institutional language.

| Attack | Evidence-based finding | Required correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI as optional app | The Round-1 plan can work as a complete-community proposal with AI labels removed | define which specialist research/test/adoption spaces and operations change because of AI, without weakening the daily base |
| World-class ambition | Local completeness does not connect convincingly to full-stack research, scaling or global exchange | show unequal specialist roles, external networks and access/transfer mechanisms |
| Fifteen-minute city collision | Civic rooms, mixed use and short webs are crowded generic territory | unit boundaries must emerge from evidence; distinctiveness cannot be a radius or service checklist |
| Testing/infrastructure edge | C02 lacks a hard gate from public problem to controlled validation and accountable adoption | add bounded test/learning path, operator/exit and spatial containment without importing C01 wholesale |
| Production/enterprise growth | Ordinary life may dominate land/programme without explaining 0→1, growth and adoption space | identify which building types and shared specialist services support each stage |
| Federation as words | Many-to-many arrows have no capacity, hours, failure or physical-interface consequences | test one real specialist exchange plus local fallback and show its ground-floor/mobility/labour effects |
| AI failure | Digital outage is now in the contract, but no real facilities have been tested | run the contract on cleared site/service evidence |

C02 can survive only if evidence-gated units coexist with a specific, unequal innovation system; adding robots, agents or dashboards would not cure the failure.
