# Candidate 02 Culture, Brand and Operations v0.1

## 1. Cultural proposition

Candidate 02 connects three cultures through the idea of **neighbourhood-scale making and belonging**.

### Jing-Zhang railway culture

The railway is read as a system that connected places while giving stations and settlements distinct local identities. This is a **CONCEPT** interpretation; all historical claims and site-specific stories require archival verification.

### Zhongguancun innovation culture

Innovation is represented by repeated local acts: learning, discussion, repair, starting small, forming teams and turning knowledge into use. It is not represented only by corporate milestones.

### AI new culture

AI becomes part of civic competence: residents and workers can understand, use, question and refuse services in ordinary neighbourhood institutions.

### Narrative arc

**A railway connected destinations. An innovation city must make each destination worth living in.**

## 2. Naming system

- Belt/federation: **京张三邻 / Three Neighbourhoods Jingzhang**
- Zhongzhiyuan: **造研邻 / Making Neighbourhood**
- AI Origin: **共学邻 / Learning Neighbourhood**
- Dazhongsi: **都会邻 / Metropolitan Neighbourhood**
- Shared park/common: **京张共园 / Jing-Zhang Common Park** as a working descriptor, not a proposed official renaming.

Names remain provisional and require public comprehension, translation and trademark review.

## 3. Logo and visual-identity motif

### Motif

Three unequal open shapes surround a shared blank field:

- the three shapes express distinct neighbourhoods;
- the blank field expresses the shared heritage common;
- small gaps show transit and civic links;
- no rail-to-circuit-board icon is used.

### Visual system

- one shared neutral heritage palette;
- one accent colour for each neighbourhood;
- diagrams use catchments and local webs, not three glowing dots on a line;
- wayfinding keeps official street and transit information primary;
- no enterprise logos, portraits or uncleared typefaces.

## 4. Three landmark directions

Landmarks are civic places with ordinary functions, not isolated objects.

1. **Zhongzhiyuan Making Commons:** public review, training, food/rest and visible but bounded research interfaces.
2. **AI Origin Learning House:** public learning, service, neighbourhood meetings and small-team support.
3. **Dazhongsi Metropolitan Exchange Hall:** arrival help, enterprise/cultural exchange, repair and everyday station-area services.

Each landmark must:

- remain useful without AI;
- have an operator and maintenance space;
- be accessible without purchase or registration for at least a defined public portion;
- avoid claims of approved location or architecture;
- provide a quiet or non-event mode.

## 5. Heritage and innovation route

The heritage common links the three landmarks, but visitors are encouraged to enter each neighbourhood rather than complete only a linear “pilgrimage.” A future route may include:

- verified railway history;
- neighbourhood change and living memory;
- public contributions to innovation, maintenance and accessibility;
- spaces where failed or withdrawn ideas are explained;
- static, bilingual, accessible interpretation.

## 6. Annual activity architecture

### Three Neighbourhoods Week

Each neighbourhood hosts one ordinary-day open programme and one shared federation forum. The event evaluates neighbourhood completeness rather than maximising attendance.

### Open Making Days

Zhongzhiyuan opens selected review/training interfaces while secure work remains protected.

### Origin Learning Season

AI Origin hosts courses, peer review, community problem framing and family-accessible learning.

### Dazhongsi Exchange Nights

Dazhongsi hosts bounded evening enterprise, culture and repair programmes while preserving transit and residential function.

### Common Park Day

Heritage, ecology, walking and neighbourhood histories are presented without turning the park into an AI fairground.

All events are **CONCEPT** suggestions, not confirmed programmes.

## 7. Long-term operations

### Neighbourhood stewards

Each neighbourhood needs a responsible operating coalition or lead organisation able to:

- maintain public rooms and ordinary services;
- coordinate events and logistics;
- publish access hours and complaint routes;
- manage bounded AI scenarios;
- measure who is excluded or displaced;
- stop programmes that undermine daily life.

No current organisation is assumed to accept this role.

### Federation table

A periodic federation table exchanges:

- unmet service needs;
- space and facility availability;
- research/test needs;
- lessons from failures and withdrawals;
- public-space and access maintenance issues;
- regional collaboration requests.

It is not a citywide data command centre.

### Conversion pathways

- learner → mentor or team member;
- research result → bounded test;
- local problem → supported project;
- visiting team → longer collaboration;
- tested service → neighbourhood adoption, relocation or withdrawal;
- maintenance lesson → public standard or training.

## 8. Brand risks

- “three neighbourhoods” sounds generic without real local form;
- names imply official administrative boundaries;
- the identity becomes three colours applied to the same design;
- public events replace daily service investment;
- a global-talent narrative erases existing residents and workers;
- the park is rebranded despite already having an established public identity.
