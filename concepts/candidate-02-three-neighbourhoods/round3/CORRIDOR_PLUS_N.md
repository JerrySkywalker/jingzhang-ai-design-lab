# Corridor+N — Continuous Jing-Zhang Backbone plus Evidence-Gated Units

## Hypothesis

```text
continuous heritage / public / blue-green backbone
+ transverse city seams
+ three official unequal strategic projects
+ N ordinary-day units determined later by networks, barriers, services and field evidence
+ bounded local/shared/regional service federation
```

## Evidence fit

`BEST_SUPPORTED_WORKING_ARCHITECTURE`, not a final unit solution.

- It directly respects the official Jing-Zhang heritage/public-space and north–south continuity task.
- It preserves all three required detailed projects without turning their provisional polygons into catchments.
- It makes east–west stitching and institutional/barrier analysis explicit.
- It allows H2/H3/H4+ to be decided later from the same contract.
- It separates universal public/living-systems continuity from local daily completeness and regional specialist networks.
- It remains honest that N is unknown.

## Spatial/operational consequences

### Backbone

Walking/wheeling, cycling where appropriate, heritage interpretation, shade/refuge, blue-green continuity and non-digital orientation. It is not a substitute for local services and cannot force long trips for basic needs.

### N local units

Boundaries follow verified route relationships, barriers, ground-floor/public-service clusters, transit thresholds and public-space continuity. Each must expose failed roles/states and external dependencies.

### Three strategic projects

- Zhongzhiyuan: research/production and shift/service-worker daily-life test;
- AI Origin: campus-city/public access, learning and resident/family service test;
- Dazhongsi: typological metropolitan arrival, commerce, event/logistics and ordinary public-life test until geometry is resolved.

### Federation

Basic toilets, refuge, affordable food, human help, maintenance and accessible everyday chains cannot be exported. Labs, testing, enterprise growth services, global exchange, capital and specialist facilities may be unequal/shared if access and fallback are evidenced.
