## Same-base rule

H2, H3, H4+ and Corridor+N use exactly the same committed evidence:

- official text for the three scales, areas, three required key projects and their strategic roles;
- repository provisional polygons, shown only as provisional;
- the same 2026-08-12 bounded OSM snapshot and source limitations;
- the same Issue #846 and #1029 geometry warnings;

- the same collision set, including City Completeness, Everyday Jing-Zhang and three ideal-unit works.

No hypothesis gets a different facility list, boundary or map extent. Contextual POI counts describe only mapped objects intersecting provisional rectangles; they do not measure completeness, hours, capacity, access, catchments or the correct location.

## Unit definition

An urban unit is not a circle or prescribed project polygon. It is a hypothesis that:

1. contains connected ordinary-day service chains for the required roles/states;
2. has physically accessible route relationships across its internal barriers;
3. relates coherently to transit and public-space structure;
4. is not divided by institution/campus/private/service barriers that its definition ignores;
5. exposes external dependencies rather than counting them as local;
6. can retain ordinary-day redundancy and maintenance/service labour;
7. can host specialised innovation capacity without exporting ordinary life;
8. does not fragment heritage or living-systems continuity.

The current common base lacks official units, service hours/capacity, access eligibility and route audits. Therefore it cannot certify any exact count.

## Comparison scale

Scores below use an ordinal evidence-fit scale, not an optimisation result:

- `0` contradicted or unusable;
- `1` weak/high-risk;
- `2` plausible but materially unproved;
- `3` best-supported working hypothesis.

No hypothesis can score above 3 because authoritative polygons, service/hours, route/access, population/employment and field evidence are missing.

## H3 support test

### Evidence for H3

- official sources require three differently named key projects with unequal strategic roles and approximate areas;
- the three provisional rectangles are ordered north/middle/south;
- the taskbook expects all three to receive detailed design.

### Evidence against H3

- official sources never call them independent complete neighbourhoods or catchments;
- the rectangles are not station/road anchored and the Dazhongsi rectangle is materially displaced from its named contextual anchor;
- contextual intersection counts are highly unequal and semantically incomplete: `PROV-KEY-001` has 1 mapped public-service and 2 selected commercial objects, `PROV-KEY-002` 7/2 plus 5 innovation objects, and `PROV-KEY-003` zero mapped public/commercial objects in the selected snapshot; these differences may be data/location artefacts and cannot prove completeness;
- official text foregrounds a continuous Jing-Zhang public/heritage structure and cross-corridor stitching, not three self-contained catchments;
- more/fewer units remain plausible because station, street, campus and service patterns are finer than the three project labels;
- direct competitors already claim three complete cores/ideal units; uniqueness cannot rescue missing evidence.

### Judgment

`H3_NOT_SUPPORTED`. This is stronger than `UNRESOLVED` because the candidate identity requires a positive inference—“three prescribed projects are exactly three complete neighbourhoods”—and current evidence explicitly shows those polygons are not catchment evidence. Missing data does not merely prevent confidence; it removes the only offered derivation of “three”.

This does not establish H2, H4+ or a particular N. It establishes that a final unit count must be downstream of professional/field evidence.

## Competition consequence

C02 no longer owns:

- city completeness / ordinary-first AI-optional;
- Everyday Jing-Zhang / staffed/non-digital service;
- exactly three flagship ideal units;
- one line + three stations/rooms/cores;
- daily service, home-work relay, day/night or civic service networks.

The ordinary-day contract remains valuable as a **review instrument** precisely because it is stricter and candidate-agnostic. That instrument has already changed C01 routes, rooms, labour, refuge and fallback. It does not need C02’s false spatial identity to survive.

## What would reverse the H3 judgment

All of the following, assessed against H2/H4+/Corridor+N on the same base:

1. authoritative overall and key-area polygons plus a defensible proposed unit boundary set;
2. cleared facility/service register with hours, capacity, public eligibility and responsible operators;
3. accessible route/time and barrier audit across weekday/evening/weekend/weather states;
4. field observation of ground-floor interfaces, service labour, logistics, refuge and institutional edges;
5. professional urban-design/transport comparison showing three catchments reduce external dependencies and severance better than alternatives.

Because this is more than one small missing datum and C01 has passed its conceptual admission, C02 should not consume a full professional candidate-development budget as an independent candidate. Its tools and key-area critiques transfer to the next phase.
