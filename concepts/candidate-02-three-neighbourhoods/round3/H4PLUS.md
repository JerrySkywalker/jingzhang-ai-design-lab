# H4+ — Four or More Local Units

## Hypothesis

The overall range contains multiple smaller network-defined ordinary-day units that cross or meet the corridor; the three official key projects act as strategic anchors rather than unit boundaries.

## Evidence fit

`PLAUSIBLE_NOT_RESOLVED`.

- Multiple contextual transit/street/service/research clusters and institutional edges make finer units plausible.
- Smaller units could expose rather than hide missing local food/help/refuge/worker-service chains.
- They could better respect barriers and different evening/weekend conditions.
- The snapshot cannot establish public access, service hours/capacity, sidewalks, crossings or institutional permeability.
- Too many units could fragment heritage/public-space coherence, duplicate governance and over-localise specialised innovation capacity.

## Spatial consequence if later supported

- several local repair/service/public-room projects rather than three comprehensive centres;
- shared corridor and specialist federation remain cross-unit;
- unit boundaries follow verified networks/barriers, not circles;
- the three key projects receive detailed design without becoming the only daily centres;
- phasing can repair the worst chain failures before fixing a final number.

## Kill test

H4+ fails if smaller units lack enough independent ordinary-day function, over-duplicate specialist capacity or fragment the shared heritage/living-systems spine.
