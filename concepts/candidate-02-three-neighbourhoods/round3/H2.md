# H2 — Two Major Urban Units

## Hypothesis

Two larger daily/innovation units organise the overall range, potentially a northern research/production ecology and a central–southern urban/learning/metropolitan ecology, while the three official key projects remain distinct interventions.

## Evidence fit

`PLAUSIBLE_NOT_SUPPORTED`.

- It avoids assuming that each official project is a catchment.
- It may reduce duplicated specialist capacity and express a north versus central/south contrast.
- It risks creating units too large for accessible ordinary-day chains and hiding multiple campus/street/service barriers.
- The Dazhongsi provisional mismatch makes any central–south merge especially speculative.
- No population, service-hour, accessible-route or institutional-access evidence supports the merge.

## Spatial consequence if later supported

- two main public/service constellations rather than three copied centres;
- three key projects remain unequal anchors within them;
- stronger cross-corridor connections and local subunits still needed;
- basic daily services cannot be exported across the entire large unit;
- logistics and specialised innovation could be coordinated at the two-unit scale.

## Kill test

H2 fails if the ordinary-day contract shows repeated cross-unit travel, inaccessible internal barriers, missing night/weekend redundancy or service chains finer than two large units.
