# H3 — Exactly Three Complete Neighbourhoods

## Hypothesis

Each official key project is an independently complete but unequal innovation neighbourhood, connected through a federation.

## Evidence fit

`NOT_SUPPORTED`.

Official evidence proves three strategic/detail-design projects, their approximate areas, north–south order and different AI roles. It does not prove independent complete neighbourhoods, ordinary-day catchments, boundaries, service chains or federation.

The common base weakens direct mapping:

- all three rectangles are provisional and not station/road anchored;
- the Dazhongsi rectangle is about 2.26 km from the contextual Dazhongsi station point;
- contextual POI intersections are unequal and cannot establish service completeness;
- multiple transit/street/research/service objects suggest finer relationships than one unit per key rectangle;
- no service hours/capacity/access or seven-state route evidence exists.

## Spatial consequence if forced despite evidence

It would repeat three mixed-use/civic-room/service-web packages, count external services as local, or invent programme/population to make each pass. That is precisely the failure this sprint was designed to prevent.
