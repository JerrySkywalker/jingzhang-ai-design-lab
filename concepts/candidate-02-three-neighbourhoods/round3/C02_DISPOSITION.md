## Why KILL rather than HOLD

Round 2 held C02 for one bounded “why exactly three?” test. Round 3 supplied a shared contextual base and direct competition refresh. The test did not produce positive H3 evidence:

- official three areas are projects/roles, not catchments;
- exact polygons and service/route evidence remain absent;
- provisional locations are not station/road anchored and Dazhongsi materially conflicts with its name;
- H2/H4+ remain plausible;
- Corridor+N fits official continuity with fewer unsupported commitments;
- direct competitors already occupy completeness/everyday/three-unit territory.

Continuing C02 would either preserve “three” without evidence or rename Corridor+N as C02. The former is bad urban reasoning; the latter is a different concept and should not hide the kill.

## What is salvaged

1. the strict ordinary-day completeness contract;
2. eight-persona/seven-state service-chain review;
3. local/shared/regional dependency ledger;
4. worker/maintenance/night/weekend/non-digital checks;
5. Corridor+N as a neutral professional comparison frame;
6. key-area-specific questions for production, campus-city and metropolitan-adoption conditions.

These already improve C01 and can review any future Candidate 04. They are not grounds to keep C02 alive.

## Site specificity

C02 in its original form remains `GENERIC`: “three complete innovation neighbourhoods” transfers to any three AI parks. The salvaged Corridor+N frame is `PARTLY_PORTABLE` because the Jing-Zhang heritage/public backbone and three official projects structure it, but N and the actual units are not yet site-derived.

## Candidate-04 consequence

C02’s death does not itself trigger Candidate 04 because C01 passed conceptual spatial admission. Candidate 04 should trigger only if C01 fails professional admission or the Owner rejects the major city-first rewrite.

## Reversal gate

The Owner could reopen a three-unit concept only after authoritative/cleared boundaries, service/hours/capacity, accessible network/barrier evidence, field observation and professional H2/H3/H4+/Corridor+N comparison positively support H3. That would be a new evidence state, not continued polishing.
