## Word substitution test

Candidate 03 is not distinct if “habitat mosaic” can be replaced with “habitat commons,” “season line,” “forest rail” or “ground first” without changing:

- figure-ground and land-use decisions;
- protect/repair/connect/add priorities;
- building-edge and selective-subtraction decisions;
- four types of cross-mesh;
- the three unequal habitat matrices;
- maintenance, professional authority and non-AI baselines.

The proposed difference is therefore structural: a non-linear living-system mosaic governs the urban plan, while the heritage line is only its seed corridor. That claim is unproven until mapped against verified ecology, buildings, streets and operations.

## Current decision

Candidate 03 remains useful as an architecture-exploration alternative because it most clearly challenges Candidate 01's technology/infrastructure-first tendency and Candidate 02's neighbourhood-completeness priority. It is not conceptually unoccupied. It should be killed if qualified landscape/ecology/hydrology work cannot turn the mosaic into a legible, site-specific urban structure or if Habitat Commons/Season Line/Forest Rail already do the same work better.
