# Candidate 03 — 京张生境拼图 / Jingzhang Habitat Mosaic

**Status:** Under Evaluation

**Version:** architecture exploration v0.1

**Not the selected proposal. Not a formal submission.**

## Evidence contract

- **FACT:** The official working brief defines study scales of approximately 43.6 km², 11.4 km² and 368.4 ha, and requires coordinated treatment of the Jing-Zhang heritage park, three key areas, industry, public life, blue-green systems, transport, AI scenarios and implementation.
- **FACT:** The canonical repository does not yet provide authoritative polygons or statutory FAR, height, density, green-ratio and setback controls. Current geometry is provisional.
- **FACT:** The official source register identifies planning, ecological, transport, heritage and other baseline gaps. Available open map data is contextual evidence, not a statutory source.
- **ASSUMPTION:** Existing and potential soil, canopy, runoff, habitat, microclimate and open-space conditions can form a connected urban living system. Their actual condition and ecological value are unverified.
- **CONCEPT:** The following habitat matrices, ecological cross-meshes, stepping stones, programmes and operations are design propositions.

## A. Core theme

### Chinese working name

**京张生境拼图**

“生境” includes the conditions that let human and non-human life persist: soil, water, shade, vegetation, habitat continuity, quiet, seasonal comfort and stewardship. “拼图” rejects one universal corridor solution; different urban pieces form a legible whole through their ecological relationships.

### English working name

**Jingzhang Habitat Mosaic**

### One-sentence proposition

让土壤、水、树冠、生境连续性与季节舒适度先于技术展示和单一轴线决定用地、建筑边界、交通与更新次序，把京张铁路遗产转化为一条生境种子带，并由三片不同的城市生境基质和横向生态联系共同形成可生活、可学习、可生产的创新城区。

### First-principles design judgment

**The innovation district should first secure the living systems on which urban life depends; buildings, programmes and AI should then fit those systems rather than compensate for their failure.**

This produces six rules:

1. existing soils, mature trees, drainage paths, habitat patches and comfortable public places are investigated before new objects are placed;
2. the heritage park is a seed corridor and cultural record, not the entire green system;
3. each key area develops a different habitat matrix tied to its urban condition;
4. cross-connections for shade, rainfall, habitat and public health are as important as the north–south heritage geography;
5. reuse and selective subtraction precede large new construction;
6. every AI service has a non-AI maintenance baseline, a named steward and a stop condition.

### Why this belongs to Jing-Zhang

Candidate 03 is not a generic “smart eco-park.”

- **FACT:** The task combines a long railway-heritage landscape with dense and diverse innovation districts, three key areas and two wider wings. It therefore contains a real tension between linear heritage identity and non-linear urban/ecological needs.
- **CONCEPT:** The former railway geography becomes a seed bank of memory, material reuse, succession and movement. Its value lies not only in continuity but in its ability to propagate into blocks, campuses, streets, roofs and courtyards.
- **CONCEPT:** Zhongguancun innovation culture is reinterpreted as open experimentation with urban living systems: observe, compare, maintain and revise rather than simply install technology.
- **ASSUMPTION:** The three key areas contain sufficiently different density, building, public-space and exposure conditions to support productive, learning and metropolitan habitat prototypes. This requires authoritative geometry and field/ecological verification.

## B. Three-scale proposition

### 43.6 km² — a living-systems innovation landscape

The wider research range is treated as an ecological and institutional relationship field:

- three key areas host different urban-habitat and public-life prototypes;
- the Technology Service Wing can connect climate, landscape, construction, digital and enterprise services without being reduced to a “green-tech” cluster;
- the Scenario Empowerment Wing can provide everyday settings for learning from public-space and service outcomes, subject to consent and governance;
- universities, institutes and companies can contribute research and training;
- capital and professional services can support maintenance, reuse and responsible enterprise;
- talent and residents encounter a district whose ecological quality is ordinary infrastructure, not an amenity reserved for campuses;
- compute, data and AI support a limited number of public-interest observations and tests rather than becoming the spatial metaphor;
- wider Haidian connections are represented as potential networks, not confirmed partnerships.

### 11.4 km² — seed corridor, three matrices, four cross-meshes, many stepping stones

The diagrammable structure is:

## **一条遗产种子带 · 三片生境基质 · 四类生态横脉 · 多级踏脚石**

- **一条遗产种子带:** the Jing-Zhang heritage park carries railway memory, reused material, seasonal planting, walking and habitat succession.
- **三片生境基质:** productive habitat at Zhongzhiyuan, learning habitat at AI Origin and metropolitan climate habitat at Dazhongsi.
- **四类生态横脉:** shade/walking, rainfall/soil, habitat/biodiversity and public-health/refuge connections cross streets and blocks.
- **多级踏脚石:** verified courtyards, roofs, campus edges, street pockets, rain gardens and small public rooms close gaps in the living system.

The structure is not a decorative green overlay. It changes which land is kept permeable, which edges open, which streets receive shade and crossings, where buildings are retained or selectively removed, where additions may sit and which projects occur first.

### 368.4 ha — three habitat prototypes

1. **Zhongzhiyuan — Productive Habitat District:** research and engineering coexist with soil, water, shade, repair, outdoor work and controlled testing.
2. **AI Origin — Learning Habitat Community:** university–city exchange, public learning, care and intergenerational life organise a cultivated and observed civic landscape.
3. **Dazhongsi — Metropolitan Climate Foyer:** dense station-area life is made walkable through shade, rain management, clean-air and quiet refuge, public thresholds and ecological stepping stones.

Exact placement is withheld until official geometry is available.

## Innovation proposition

The candidate does not make ecology a specialist industry island. It uses living-system quality as the common ground for a broad AI ecosystem:

- fundamental research and computation remain possible;
- construction, landscape, mobility, health, maintenance, culture and public service create applied problems;
- small companies gain shared test and demonstration access under bounded rules;
- universities and communities can participate in public learning without becoming data sources by default;
- observed outcomes feed maintenance and design revision;
- the physical district remains useful when a digital system is offline or retired.

## Complete-design capability

Candidate 03 is capable of covering:

- all three spatial scales and the three-areas/two-wings relationship;
- industry, education, research, enterprise, capital, talent, compute, data and scenario links;
- land use, buildings, renewal and conceptual urban form;
- rail, street, walking, cycling, parking, curb and logistics;
- municipal and new infrastructure;
- heritage, blue-green systems, public space and all three key areas;
- at least 10 AI scenarios, 3 controlled tests and 5 user groups;
- railway, Zhongguancun and new AI culture;
- landmarks, visual identity, events and long-term stewardship;
- projects, phasing, risks, evidence gaps and kill criteria.

## Current value and current danger

The value is a strong city-making priority: living systems determine form and investment rather than decorate them. It creates the clearest break from Candidate 01's technical-infrastructure emphasis.

The danger is substantial collision. Strong existing proposals already address seasonal climate infrastructure, habitat commons, shade, forest rail and ground-first public value. Candidate 03 is worth further comparison only if landscape, ecology, hydrology and urban design expertise can produce a credible non-linear spatial system that is more than a renamed green corridor.
