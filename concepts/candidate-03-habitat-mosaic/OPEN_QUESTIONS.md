# Candidate 03 Open Questions and Kill Criteria

## Official and scientific evidence gaps

1. What are the authoritative overall and key-area polygons?
2. Where is the Dazhongsi key area relative to actual transit and urban conditions?
3. Does the official scope intersect the mapped Jing-Zhang heritage park, and where?
4. What planning, heritage, ecological, water, utility, transport and development controls apply?
5. What are the verified soil, contamination, drainage, topography, canopy, habitat, heat, wind and air-quality conditions?
6. Which buildings, courtyards, roofs, edges and public rooms are structurally and operationally suitable?
7. Which current public spaces work well, for whom, at what times and in which seasons?
8. Who owns, operates and maintains the relevant spaces and systems?
9. Which ecological information is safe and appropriate to publish?

## Design questions

1. Can the non-linear mosaic be drawn clearly without inventing ecological facts?
2. What makes each of the three habitat matrices a genuine urban-form type?
3. Which existing living assets should be protected before any new green project?
4. Where do the four cross-meshes conflict with streets, utilities, ownership, freight or emergency access?
5. Does the heritage seed corridor retain railway character or become a generic landscape?
6. Can dense Dazhongsi conditions support meaningful living-system improvements without a spectacle canopy or implausible roof greening?
7. How much productive/public permeability is safe at Zhongzhiyuan?
8. Can AI Origin support public learning without extracting data or over-programming quiet space?
9. Which projects improve daily life without any AI component?
10. What maintenance capacity exists across ordinary, severe-weather, event and failure states?
11. Does landscape-first priority weaken the broader world-class AI innovation ecosystem?
12. What does Candidate 03 contribute beyond Habitat Commons, Season Line, Shade the Cloud, Forest Rail and Ground First?

## Owner decisions required

- Is the team willing to bring landscape, ecology, hydrology and public-health expertise into the core design process?
- Is a candidate with weaker direct Owner technical fit but stronger ecological city-making worth carrying?
- How much collision with current ecological/climate proposals is acceptable?
- Should the concept stop if professional evidence produces disconnected local projects rather than a coherent mosaic?
- Which physical improvements deserve funding before any AI test?
- Can the Owner accept that multi-agent and embodied-intelligence work may remain peripheral?

## Kill criteria

Candidate 03 should be stopped or fundamentally reconstituted if any of these conditions persists:

1. Habitat Commons, Season Line, Forest Rail, Ground First or another high-quality proposal already expresses the same spatial logic more credibly;
2. the 11.4 km² plan is only a green line plus scattered patches, not a clear non-linear structure;
3. the three key areas repeat the same garden, canopy and sensor package;
4. “living systems first” does not change land use, building edges, mobility, public space or phasing;
5. removal of AI reveals only generic landscape language rather than a strong urban design;
6. addition of AI creates unnecessary sensing, spectacle, false precision or operational fragility;
7. the Jing-Zhang railway relationship is decorative or interchangeable with any linear brownfield park;
8. ecological or climate claims cannot be supported by qualified evidence and modelling;
9. the concept ignores dense urban functions, ordinary commerce, service workers or metropolitan intensity;
10. implementation depends on unknown authority, widespread demolition, infeasible roof greening or unmaintainable construction;
11. no accountable long-term steward or maintenance path can be identified;
12. public access and ecological protection cannot be reconciled;
13. professional review concludes that the mosaic fragments rather than connects urban life;
14. the broader AI innovation ecosystem is reduced to a narrow environmental-technology theme.

## Evidence that would justify continuation

- an authoritative, multidisciplinary living-system and urban-condition atlas;
- a legible non-linear plan whose connections follow evidence;
- three materially different matrix prototypes;
- concrete retain/repair/reuse/subtract/add decisions;
- feasible maintenance and stewardship responsibilities;
- physical/manual value before digital enhancement;
- a collision review demonstrating structural, not lexical, difference.
