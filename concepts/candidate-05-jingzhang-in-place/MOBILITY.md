# Mobility

Mobility begins with unequal thresholds and reliable ordinary routes, not a branded north–south AI track.

## Three threshold systems

### Qinghe regional arrival

Treat the existing multimodal hub as the gateway. Field-audit the relationship among high-speed/suburban rail, metro, bus, taxi, bicycle, walking, Five-Ring access and Zhongzhiyuan context. Candidate actions focus on last-kilometre continuity, readable transfers, refuge/help and separation from equipment/service movement; they do not duplicate the hub.

### Wudaokou–Qinghua East public-side campus access

Connect existing station exits, public streets, park and verified gates without routing the city through campuses. Design two states at Qinghua East Road West: present Line 15 terminal conditions and future 13×15 interchange demand. Preserve space for walking, cycling, waiting, accessible routes and local servicing without assuming final exits.

### Dazhongsi four-quadrant station city

Use the operating 12×13 interchange as fact and the official four-quadrant task as a design question. Audit station exits, crossroads, grade-separated conditions, bicycle interfaces, local streets, buses and accessible routes. Different thresholds may require underpass repair, surface priority, bridge/forecourt interface or staffed alternative; the candidate does not assert which solution fits an unsurveyed node.

## Network hierarchy

- ordinary public route: continuous, anonymous, step-free where required, signed and AI-independent;
- local walking/cycling: connects daily services, park/water and public-side rooms;
- controlled access: timed/institutional/test entry never counted as permanent public continuity;
- service/emergency: audited separately, no conflict hidden in public/blue-green drawings;
- event/AI operation: time-bounded and removable; ordinary route has priority;
- failure route: human help, physical signs and verified alternative when lift/facility/digital layer is down.

## Ordinary-Day mobility gate

Test eight user groups—resident, child/caregiver, older person, disabled person, student/researcher, enterprise/independent worker, maintenance/delivery worker and visitor—through:

`weekday`, `evening`, `weekend`, `heat/rain`, `event`, `facility down`, `digital down`.

A failed state changes a route, rest/refuge location, staffed entry, timetable or project phase. It may not be logged only as risk.

## Parking, loading and servicing

Current capacity/flows are unknown. Formal design requires a field/traffic audit before moving or adding car/bicycle/loading areas. First responses should be operational/reversible—timed access, marked handover, bicycle management, conflict-free emergency route—before new permanent infrastructure.

## AI boundary

AI may assist bounded demand/conflict forecasts and accessible navigation, but no individual tracking or app dependency is required. Physical routes, signs, staff/manual help and emergency systems remain authoritative. AI is not the mobility concept.

## Stop conditions

- campus opening or station exit is unverified;
- accessible alternative fails;
- public route conflicts with secure test/equipment or emergency access;
- proposed crossing needs unverified engineering/right-of-way;
- event/AI operation reduces ordinary passage below the professionally accepted standard.
