# Urban Renewal and Buildings

## Rule: status is not action

No source category automatically authorizes a building action. `Old community`, `former factory`, `low-efficiency`, `unsafe`, `heritage-adjacent`, OSM footprint and provisional land-use label are area/context evidence only until a professional building/tenant/title survey supports a decision.

## Building evidence matrix

Before a candidate building receives `ADAPT`, record:

- current lawful use and operating hours;
- owner/manager, tenant/business and consent state;
- structure, fire/life safety and load;
- heritage status/control requirements;
- accessibility and public/controlled entrances;
- floor depth, grid, daylight/ventilation and service core;
- power, data, water, drainage, equipment and emergency capacity;
- public-space, loading, waste, noise/light and neighbour interface;
- displacement/affordability effect;
- reversible reuse and exit condition.

Unverified items keep the action at `SURVEY_REQUIRED`.

## Action types

### Retain

Protect a functioning use/building and improve only its surrounding route, maintenance or public interface. Retain is active urban design, not doing nothing.

### Repair

Address accessibility, envelope, safety, drainage, service, public-ground or neighbour interfaces without changing the core use. Prioritise repairs that serve ordinary users with AI off.

### Open edge

Create a public-side room, forecourt, doorstep or timed interface without claiming the secure interior. Requires owner/operator consent, access/security design, staffing and a closure fallback that does not break the city route.

### Subdivide/reconnect

Introduce verified public/service passages or smaller carriers only when legal access, fire, title and user continuity support it. It is not a universal superblock-cutting rule.

### Adapt/infill

Reversible fit-out or carefully located infill follows demonstrated programme, structural/service and public-space need. The new use must remain reusable if the AI programme ends.

### Replacement

Not part of the automatic patchwork. It requires structural/heritage evidence, alternatives analysis, tenant/business continuity, planning/title and public procedures, independent review and explicit public benefit. The candidate does not name a real building for demolition.

## Three building families

- north controlled carrier: eligible deeper shell with service/safety separation, public observation outside and ordinary reuse after AI;
- middle public/controlled room: small street/park-side carrier with reversible partition, ordinary learning/community baseline and secure project state;
- south mixed urban carrier: accessible active ground, stacked ordinary work/service/adoption rooms and compact service interface.

These are types, not confirmed buildings.

## Continuity and anti-displacement contract

Every renewal package must state:

- existing resident/business/staff groups and how they are consulted accessibly;
- no involuntary displacement claim without a legal process and disclosed remedy;
- affordability/tenure indicator and who can verify it;
- temporary-operation/relocation path during works;
- benefit allocation to existing users, not only incoming AI firms;
- stop condition when continuity or accessibility worsens;
- public reporting and appeal route.

Candidate 05 cannot promise a rent, unit count or relocation policy without formal evidence/authority; it makes these missing contracts admission dependencies.

## Building metrics for formal development

Do not set baselines before survey. Define methods for:

- retained footprint/use share;
- verified adaptable carrier count/area;
- accessible public entrance/route continuity;
- reversible fit-out share;
- existing resident/business continuity;
- affordable workspace/community-room availability;
- occupied hours with ordinary/non-digital access;
- maintenance responsibility and unresolved defect closure;
- AI-specific space removed/reused after scenario exit.
