# Blue-Green and Public Realm

Blue-green systems are existing projects, maintenance regimes and living processes—not Candidate 05's brand.

## Project-interface rule

Separate and label:

- `OPEN / AS BUILT`;
- `APPROVED / PROCURED / IN DELIVERY`;
- `WORKS COMPLETE / PUBLIC OPEN STATUS UNKNOWN`;
- `CONTEXTUAL OSM`;
- `SITE PERFORMANCE UNKNOWN`;
- `CONCEPTUAL C05 INTERFACE`.

The project-interface register identifies where Candidate 05 completes an edge, crossing, ordinary facility or operating relationship without redrawing Phase 1, Qinghe/Xiaoyue programmes or later works.

## System roles

- Jing-Zhang heritage park: existing public/heritage ground and north–south relationship, with unequal opening/project states;
- Qinghe: regional river/gateway context with active improvement programme;
- Xiaoyue River: ecological/flood/maintenance project corridor and institution/community relationship;
- area courts/forecourts: public-side thresholds for station, campus and compound contexts;
- doorstep spaces: food/rest/care, toilets, shade/rain refuge and ordinary learning/work near daily routes.

## Living Systems Gate

Every patch records:

| Gate | Required evidence/question | Spatial consequence |
|---|---|---|
| soil/root zone | current soil, trees, rooting and compaction | change footprint, loading, path or phase; no device/root conflict |
| drainage | project scope, levels, outlets/capacity and maintenance | protect flood/maintenance corridor; avoid invented hydraulic claim |
| canopy/shade/refuge | measured seasonal exposure and user needs | retain/add shade or refuge before programmed display space |
| habitat | documented/field conditions | protect/create only evidence-led link; no speculative species claim |
| maintenance | actor, access, seasonal work and budget category | keep service access and recovery space visible |
| noise/light | residents, heritage, park hours and controlled operations | limit location/hours/brightness; provide quiet route/edge |
| seasonal operation | heat, rain, freeze, leaf litter, events | change material, shelter, closing and recovery plan |

AI sensors/controls never substitute for soil, drainage, shade, manual maintenance or safe offline operation.

## Public-space hierarchy and rights

1. anonymous passage and free rest;
2. accessible toilets, seating, shade/rain refuge and human help;
3. ordinary community/learning/work and food/care interfaces;
4. scheduled culture/innovation programmes;
5. controlled AI testing/display only where it cannot displace levels 1–3.

Existing users retain priority over event spectacle. No facial recognition or personalised eligibility is needed for ordinary ground.

## Three area applications

- north: coordinate river/compound/arrival systems and separate secure test/service from public water/green routes;
- middle: extend interfaces around delivered park and approved public-space works; keep permanent routes outside campuses;
- south: link station/grade thresholds to small courts, quiet residential edges and heritage review rather than a new linear green identity.

## Unknowns that block detailed design

Tree/canopy inventory, soil/root health, site heat, water quality, flood levels, drainage capacity/outfalls, noise/light, current project limits/opening, maintenance agreements and heritage-control bands all require current professional evidence.
