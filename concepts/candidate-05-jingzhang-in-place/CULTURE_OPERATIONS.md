# Culture and Operations

## Cultural direction

The railway is evidence of successive working layers: a historic transport project, underground/parallel infrastructure, heritage objects, delivered park sections, daily routes and future city interfaces. It is not an AI track metaphor and does not justify one-spine branding.

`京张续城 / Jing-Zhang In Place` tells a simple public story: continue the existing city carefully, make unequal places work together, and let new innovation uses earn space without erasing what is already here.

## Three cultural expressions

- north—river/arrival/working landscape: interpret Qinghe/rail relationships and current innovation work through verified public-side programmes, not a new tech monument;
- middle—park/station/campus public edge: treat Qinghuayuan Station, delivered park and scheduled institutional learning as distinct layers with quiet/residential respect;
- south—grade/renewal/rail learning: connect verified railway/AI research and heritage through scheduled learning and ordinary city routes, not a pilgrimage axis.

Heritage use requires source, copyright, protection and professional approval. No unverified heritage boundary, institutional partnership or event is claimed.

## Public naming hierarchy

- candidate identity: `京张续城 / Jing-Zhang In Place` (working only);
- spatial language: `water–compound threshold`, `public-side campus threshold`, `grade-separated station-city field`;
- action language: `retain`, `repair`, `open edge`, `reconnect`, `adapt/infill`, each paired with evidence status;
- AI programmes: descriptive task names, never a district-level OS or agent-city brand.

## Operating model

Operations are organised by project/interface package, not one omniscient district platform.

| Operating layer | Responsible role types | Required record |
|---|---|---|
| ordinary public route/space | public-space, transport, property and maintenance roles | opening, inspection, accessibility, complaint, outage and recovery |
| controlled institution edge | institution/operator, public-interface staff, security/safety and community liaison | schedule, access, closure fallback, public-route independence |
| renewal pilot | coordinator, owner/manager, tenants/businesses, residents, professional reviewers | evidence, consent, works, continuity, stop/go and exit |
| AI task space | task owner, qualified human reviewer, safety/privacy and facility operator | state/TTL, resource, supervision, incident, reset and removal/reuse |
| event/culture | organizer, venue/public-space manager, accessibility, safety and rights roles | permission, capacity, noise/light, accessibility, cancellation and clean-up |

No named entity is assigned a role until verified consent/authority exists.

## Annual/seasonal programme family

Candidate 05 can support a modest rotating programme—open-building/heritage learning, public-side research exchange, renewal walkshops, bounded validation observation and local business/community events—but it does not require one new global festival. Programmes use existing/verified spaces, respect quiet/maintenance and have cancellation/ordinary-reuse paths.

## Ordinary operation

- fixed opening information and physical signs exist without an app;
- public passage, toilets/rest, food/care and human help remain available during AI shutdown;
- controlled rooms can close independently;
- heat/rain, evening/weekend, event, facility-down and digital-down staffing/routes are explicit;
- maintenance, cleaning, loading, waste and emergency responsibilities are visible;
- existing residents/businesses have an accessible complaint/appeal and continuity record.

## Brand risk

Before formal lock, search current titles and conduct plain-language testing. If `In Place` or `续城` is confused with Rooted/retention peers or suggests freezing change, rename it. The spatial proposition cannot be altered to defend the name.
