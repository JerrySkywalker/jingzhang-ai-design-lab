## First-principles proposition

Jing-Zhang is not an empty AI line. It is an existing city of unequal working layers: delivered and in-progress public projects, controlled university edges, active innovation carriers, mixed communities and businesses, regional and metropolitan stations, rivers, heritage and grade-separated infrastructure. The design task is to make those layers work together without erasing their difference or redrawing projects already under way.

Candidate 05 therefore begins with evidence status and urban fabric, not AI technology or a railway metaphor:

```text
existing / delivered / controlled / unknown condition
→ public and service relationship
→ retain / repair / edge / reconnect / adapt / infill action
→ implementation evidence and consent gate
→ complete ordinary city
→ bounded AI programme / fit-out / operating consequence
```

Replacement is never one of the automatic actions. It enters only after a separate statutory, structural, heritage, tenant/business and public-interest proof.

## Name

`京张续城 / Jing-Zhang In Place` is a working candidate name, not a registered brand or Owner-locked submission title.

- `续城` describes continuing an existing city through successive working layers, not reassembling it or re-embodying it.
- `In Place` signals adaptation rooted in actual projects and interfaces, without claiming everything must remain physically unchanged.
- the name avoids AI jargon and does not make heritage, ecology, verification or evidence the public brand.

If collision or public-language testing finds the name too close to current “rooted/existing city” peers, the concept remains valid and the name must change. Renaming may not alter the proposition.

## AI double test

`AI_OFF_CITY=PASS`: removing all AI leaves a coherent heritage/park, university-edge, transit, mixed-use, renewal, blue-green and ordinary-life city.

`AI_MATTERS=PASS_CONDITIONAL`: AI materially changes three selected environments—controlled validation fit-outs, secure project/data interfaces, and adoption/compliance operating rooms. Those effects must be proved with Task-to-Space packets and may produce `NO NEW AI SPACE REQUIRED`. If they become optional tenant branding, Candidate 05 is killed.

## Non-negotiable required changes

1. Build the formal status–action plan from actual site/project evidence; no generic colour-by-action diagram.
2. Carry three Task-to-Space packets through state, TTL, minimum resource, ordinary sufficiency, spatial consequence/no-build, offline mode and removal/reuse.
3. Add resident/business continuity, affordability/anti-displacement, accessible consultation and benefit-allocation contracts.
4. Attach actor, evidence, consent, stop/go metric and exit/reuse rule to each pilot.
5. Preserve exact-geometry, title, building-condition, capacity and engineering unknowns until professional evidence exists.

## Hard kill conditions

- the primary geometry becomes a line/three nodes, generic retain/open/infill palette or repeated cross-street module;
- three key areas become validation/incubation/market stages or the same renewal kit ×3;
- campus interiors become assumed public routes;
- building conditions or parcel actions are invented;
- AI is optional analysis, app, display or tenant label;
- community continuity is brand language without enforceable gates;
- implementation relies on unverified megaprojects, universal opening or large demolition;
- the concept becomes a near duplicate of a current mature proposal.
