# Implementation

Implementation is a sequence of evidence and consent gates, not a promised government programme.

## G0 — align and survey

- replace/refine official geometry when released;
- reconcile open, approved, in-delivery and unknown park/water/station/public-space projects;
- audit public/controlled access, buildings, tenants/businesses, title/consent, transport, servicing and living systems;
- create project-interface, assumption and evidence registers;
- establish accessible resident/business/worker/institution consultation;
- select no pilot until a responsible actor set and stop authority are credible.

Output: evidence/status atlas and `NO ACTION / SURVEY / PILOT-ELIGIBLE` classification, not a demolition plan.

## G1 — no-regret interfaces and reversible pilots

At most one initial pilot in each area, only with verified carrier/consent:

- north: controlled validation carrier/interface without public-route harm;
- middle: public-side ordinary/controlled adaptable room outside campus dependence;
- south: accessible public-ground/adoption-service interface coordinated with renewal/station conditions.

Low-regret public repair—signage, accessibility, shade/refuge or project-interface correction—may proceed conceptually when it serves AI-off ordinary life and has professional approval.

## G2 — selected adaptive reuse

Advance only after independent structure, fire, heritage, accessibility, service, privacy/safety and resident/business continuity review. Reversible elements and ordinary reuse are mandatory. Publish what evidence changed the action.

## G3 — measured infill

Infill addresses a documented programme/public-space/service gap that cannot be met by ordinary or adapted space. It requires statutory controls, title, infrastructure, living-systems and public-interest review. No generic density target is set.

## Replacement gate

Replacement is never a default phase. It requires evidence that retain/repair/adapt alternatives cannot safely/publicly meet the need, plus the complete legal/public process and continuity remedy. Candidate 05 identifies no real demolition.

## Stop/go indicators

Each pilot defines observable methods and future baselines for:

- ordinary/accessibility route continuity;
- public-space availability during all operating states;
- existing resident/business/user continuity and affordability;
- controlled/public boundary integrity and incidents;
- maintenance responsibility and issue closure;
- task success, human review and stale-state/TTL rejection;
- AI-off/manual service availability;
- living-systems harm/maintenance load;
- removable/reusable fit-out at exit;
- project duplication/conflict findings.

No numeric target is invented before a baseline and accountable operator exist.

## Funding/procurement boundary

Potential categories include public-space/infrastructure renewal, property retrofit, operator/tenant fit-out and programme operation. Candidate 05 does not claim a budget, funder, procurement route, public–private vehicle or approved project. Each package must identify public costs/benefits separately from commercial revenue and cannot finance public obligations by assumed future high rents.
