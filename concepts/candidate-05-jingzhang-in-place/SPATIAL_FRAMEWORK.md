# Spatial Framework

## 43.6 km² — asset-and-interface framework

The regional diagram is a matrix of existing assets and public interfaces, not a flow arrow or one innovation axis.

| Relationship class | Spatial question | Candidate response | Evidence ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| university/research edges | where can public exchange occur without compromising controlled interiors? | public-side rooms, forecourts and scheduled programmes at verified gates | no permanent opening or partnership assumed |
| innovation carriers | which existing carriers can support public, project or controlled work? | survey and classify access/fit-out/service needs before programme assignment | no vacancy, capacity or reuse claim without survey |
| enterprise/professional/capital | how can adoption services enter an ordinary mixed city? | verified street-facing rooms and mixed-use carriers, not a single south headquarters | no firm migration or operator commitment |
| regional/metro gateways | how do unequal arrivals meet public city networks? | Qinghe regional, middle campus/transit and Dazhongsi metropolitan threshold families | no precise catchment/flow claim |
| heritage/park/water projects | what is built, approved, in delivery or unknown? | project-interface register and non-duplicating connection packages | source status never upgraded |
| community/talent life | how do innovation and existing life share services/space? | ordinary routes, food/care/rest, affordable work/community interfaces and continuity gates | no demographic or tenure quantity invented |

West/east wings remain relationship contexts rather than two invented construction belts. Public, professional, compute/data and daily-life services enter through verified institutions/operators only after consent.

## 11.4 km² — status × action patchwork

### Evidence status

| Status | Meaning | Design permission |
|---|---|---|
| `BUILT_OPERATING` | publicly evidenced as open/operating | retain and improve interface; do not redraw as future project |
| `APPROVED_OR_IN_DELIVERY` | approved/procured/reported works with stated scope | coordinate and phase; do not claim current opening/performance |
| `CONTROLLED_ACCESS` | institutional/secure entry is timed, gated or role-limited | keep public network outside; add only verified interface programmes |
| `SURVEY_ONLY_UNKNOWN` | geometry/use/condition/title/access not sufficiently evidenced | no parcel/building action; survey and show typology only |
| `VERIFIED_ADAPTABLE` | future professional survey establishes safe/consented adaptability | reversible retrofit, subdivision or fit-out may proceed |

### Conditional action families

`RETAIN`, `REPAIR`, `OPEN EDGE`, `SUBDIVIDE/RECONNECT`, `ADAPT/INFILL`. An action needs a source/status, section, public/service consequence, actor/consent path, stop/go indicator and reuse/exit path. `REPLACE` is outside this automatic palette and requires a separate legal/public-interest gate.

### Connection families

1. ordinary public street/park route—anonymous, step-free, AI-independent;
2. grade-separated threshold—underpass/bridge/station interfaces, professionally checked;
3. controlled institutional threshold—public-side room plus scheduled/verified access;
4. transit/water/heritage interface—coordinates existing project, maintenance and protection systems.

No one connection family is repeated as the total brand.

### Public-space hierarchy

- regional: Qinghe/Xiaoyue/heritage-park and gateway relationships;
- area: station forecourts, campus-edge courts, compound/water interfaces;
- local: doorstep rest, food/care, learning/work, toilets, shade/rain refuge;
- controlled: secure test/project rooms physically separated from ordinary passage;
- service: loading, repair, waste and emergency access audited as a parallel subsystem.

## Differentiated fabrics

- north has deeper compounds, water/project interfaces and regional arrival;
- middle has fine-grain public-side rooms, controlled edges, delivered park and current/future transit;
- south has compact mixed urban fronts, interchange/grade conditions and heterogeneous renewal types.

The formal design must show at least one plan and one section for each fabric, at consistent comparison scales.

## Geometry discipline

Current polygons remain provisional and the south key rectangle conflicts with contextual Dazhongsi anchoring. Formal migration starts with topology/typology and required flags. Exact organizer geometry triggers full recomputation of land use, buildings, roads, public/green space, constraints, phases, metrics and all derived figures.

## Required spatial proof before Owner lock

- evidence/status layer for delivered, planned, controlled and unknown conditions;
- four connection-family sections;
- three same-scale key-area context/design panels;
- status→action→pilot trigger matrix;
- overlay showing public/blue-green and servicing do not silently conflict;
- AI-off drawing and three AI task-to-space deltas.
