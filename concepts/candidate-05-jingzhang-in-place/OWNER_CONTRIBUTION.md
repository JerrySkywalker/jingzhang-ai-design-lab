## Strong contribution areas

- Task-to-Space requirements and ordinary-space sufficiency/no-build decisions;
- AI state, uncertainty, TTL, authorization and stale-state rejection;
- minimum information/resource bundles and bounded infrastructure needs;
- human review, safety, privacy, offline/manual fallback and removal/reuse;
- scenario operating architecture, measurable stop/go gates and evidence ledgers;
- reproducible geometry/metrics/figure pipeline and formal validation.

## Areas not substituted by Owner expertise

- first-principles urban design and spatial composition;
- transport/interchange/accessibility engineering;
- architecture, structure, fire, services and adaptive-reuse feasibility;
- landscape, soil, drainage, hydrology, ecology and maintenance;
- heritage conservation and archaeological/protection controls;
- statutory planning, title, leases, procurement, finance and approval;
- resident/business engagement, accessibility co-design and anti-displacement policy;
- bilingual public communication and graphic/board production.

## Guardrail

Owner systems expertise may make Candidate 05 safer and more falsifiable, but may not turn it into Urban OS, Agent city, evidence/verification branding, task-to-space total concept, embodied/robot city or a service-station city.
