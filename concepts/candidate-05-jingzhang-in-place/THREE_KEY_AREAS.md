# Three Key Areas

Exact key-area redlines are unavailable. These are differentiated context/design typologies, not parcel plans.

## 1. Zhongzhiyuan — water–compound–regional-arrival mosaic

### Position

Northern full-stack innovation context connected to the operating Qinghe regional multimodal gateway, Five-Ring relationship and differentiated Qinghe/Xiaoyue/heritage interfaces.

### Spatial structure

Several compound, water/green and arrival fields linked by ordinary public routes and controlled service/test thresholds. No ceremonial gate and no generic garden campus.

### Land/function

Retain existing lawful uses. Introduce controlled validation, project/service and public-learning functions only in professionally verified carriers. Daily services and public space remain independent of secure programmes.

### Buildings/renewal

Survey structure, fire, power/data, access, current users, heritage, title/consent and service separation. Adapt eligible deep/large shells reversibly; do not invent laboratories, factories or vacancy.

### Public realm

Coordinate river/park projects, public observation, worker/visitor amenities, shade/rain refuge and heritage interfaces. Do not create a conflicting new river section.

### Mobility/logistics

Distinguish regional passengers, local walking/cycling, workers/visitors, equipment/service and emergency routes. Last-kilometre conditions require field audit.

### AI

Bounded validation may require secure perimeter, safety observer, clean/reset state, equipment access and reversible fit-out. It never occupies the ordinary river/park route.

### Ordinary life

Food, toilets, rest, public water/park access, care/referral, worker refuge and manual assistance remain available with AI off and on weekends/heat/rain.

### Implementation

Reconcile current water/park projects; survey candidate carriers; select one consented reversible fit-out; open only after safety/operating review; scale on measured use and zero public-route harm.

### Risk

Unknown carrier, load, title, project status and traffic/service conflict. Stop if no verified carrier or ordinary/public route separation exists.

## 2. AI Origin — public-side campus threshold field

### Position

Existing Wudaokou/AI Origin innovation context between the delivered heritage park, controlled campuses, current Line 13/15 access and future Qinghua East Road West interchange.

### Spatial structure

Fine-grain public-side street/park rooms and courts linked by permanent city routes outside campus gates. Controlled institutional thresholds and scheduled programmes sit behind clear boundaries.

### Land/function

Learning, transfer, small project rooms, community/talent services, food/care and existing business continuity. Do not invent a new innovation centre or universal shared campus.

### Buildings/renewal

Survey street/park-side floors and small courts. Use reversible public/controlled partitions and ordinary multi-use rooms; no blanket wall removal, tenancy assumption or building-specific efficiency label.

### Public realm

Complete interfaces around Phase 1 and the approved seven-node public-space project. Preserve anonymous passage and ordinary rest outside programmed rooms.

### Mobility/logistics

Connect Wudaokou and current Qinghua East access now; preserve adaptable bicycle, walking, waiting and service capacity for the future interchange. Campus interiors are never public shortcuts.

### AI

Secure project/data work may change access, acoustic, power/data, booking and human-review states in one half of an otherwise ordinary learning/work/community carrier.

### Ordinary life

Residents, students, independent workers, caregivers, older/disabled users and visitors can access passage, learning/community, toilets/rest and manual help without AI or institutional enrollment.

### Implementation

Map current public/project/gate status; enter institutional and tenant dialogue; pilot one public-side adaptable room at a consented carrier; measure public availability, affordability, controlled-use integrity and neighbour impact.

### Risk

Opening times, title, carrier availability and future station status are time-sensitive. Stop or relocate the programme if it depends on an unverified campus opening.

## 3. Dazhongsi — grade-separated station-city renewal field

### Position

Operating 12×13 interchange, official four-quadrant task, mixed urban fronts, grade separation, heritage context and a documented heterogeneous renewal unit.

### Spatial structure

Multiple station/underpass/bridge/local-street sections and compact fabric patches; no station-centric megaproject and no use of the provisional key rectangle as a parcel plan.

### Land/function

Ordinary mixed city plus verified public/customer/professional/adoption rooms. Business/resident continuity and public service precede new enterprise branding.

### Buildings/renewal

Area-level old/unsafe/low-efficiency labels never transfer to a building. Survey every candidate; prioritise ground-floor access repair and small adaptive reuse; infill only after demonstrated need; replacement follows separate procedure.

### Public realm

Continuous accessible routes from real station exits to all quadrants, bicycle interfaces, rest/toilets, small courts and quiet residential edges. Exact engineering waits for professional data.

### Mobility/logistics

Separate interchange passenger, local walking/cycling, service/loading, emergency and event states. Different grade conditions receive different solutions.

### AI

Staffed adoption/compliance rooms and bounded urban-use demonstrations alter selected room programme, supervision and operating time. The public route and human service remain complete when AI is removed.

### Ordinary life

Step-free movement, non-digital help, food/care, quiet/night protection, delivery/maintenance worker access and facility/digital-down routes are mandatory.

### Implementation

Join the documented renewal coordination process as a conceptual project package; audit crossings/buildings/users; pilot one reversible public-ground interface; scale only after access, continuity and maintenance responsibility are verified.

### Risk

Exact polygon, parcel condition, station surface performance, title and engineering capacity are unknown. Stop any action that presumes demolition, universal connectivity or station development rights.

## Non-repetition test

The north is organised by water/compound/region, the middle by public/controlled campus-park thresholds, and the south by grade-separated interchange/urban renewal. If formal drawings show the same court, gate, AI room or connection module three times, Candidate 05 fails.
