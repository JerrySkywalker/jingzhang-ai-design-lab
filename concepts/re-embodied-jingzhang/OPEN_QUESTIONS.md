# Open Questions / Kill Criteria

## Official-data gaps

- exact official site and three key-area polygons;
- current regulatory controls and building-height/FAR conditions;
- verified existing-building inventory and ownership constraints;
- road redlines, station interfaces and detailed accessibility conditions;
- municipal capacity and protected/environmental constraints.

## Urban-design questions

- Is “一脊·三核·两网·多站” spatially stronger than a conventional one-belt/three-core diagram, or merely renamed?
- Can the heritage spine remain primarily a human public space while supporting visible AI functions?
- What building reuse typologies make “re-embodiment” physically legible?
- Which east–west stitches are actually needed after field/data verification?
- How should the three key areas differ architecturally and publicly, not only programmatically?

## Technical questions

- Which 3–4 scenarios deserve quantitative requirement-inversion work?
- What is the smallest credible re-embodiment station program?
- Which robot/service functions are mature enough for a 2026–2030 conceptual pilot?
- Can shared charging/maintenance/tool interfaces be described without assuming nonexistent standards?
- What public information should every AI service disclose on site?

## Cultural/brand questions

- Is “再具身” understandable and attractive to non-technical reviewers?
- Does it connect naturally to Jing-Zhang engineering culture, or require too much explanation?
- Can the visual identity avoid overused rail/electronic-circuit imagery?
- What Chinese/English naming system has international communication value?

## Competitive kill criteria

Candidate 01 should be rejected if a benchmark sweep finds an existing proposal that already combines the same four-layer re-embodiment idea with equal or better full urban-design execution.

## Comparison requirement

Before selection, develop at least:

- Candidate 02 — a complete concept with a substantially different spatial/cultural mother idea;
- Candidate 03 — another complete concept with different implementation and AI logic.

The owner should select only after a requirement-by-requirement comparison.
