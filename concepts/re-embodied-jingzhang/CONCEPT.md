# Candidate 01 — 再具身京张 / Re-Embodied Jingzhang

**Status:** Under Evaluation  
**Version:** concept v0.1  
**Not the selected final proposal.**

## One-sentence proposition

把百年京张从“被保存的铁路遗产”转化为一条能够持续承载文化、生态、创新、公共生活与具身 AI 服务的**可重构城市公共基础设施脊柱**，并以此组织 43.6 km² 创新生态、11.4 km² 完整城市设计和三个重点区域的差异化更新。

Working English proposition:

> Re-embody a century-old infrastructure corridor as an adaptive civic spine where heritage, urban renewal, public life and embodied intelligence continuously acquire new physical functions.

## Why “re-embodiment”

The term currently carries four linked meanings.

### 1. Heritage re-embodiment

The railway is not treated as a decorative background for AI. Its linear continuity, station logic, engineering culture and public memory are translated into a contemporary civic framework: heritage interpretation, blue-green continuity, slow mobility, public life, innovation exchange and visible AI experimentation.

### 2. Urban-fabric re-embodiment

The proposal should prefer **retain → renovate → reuse → add function → selectively rebuild** over tabula-rasa redevelopment. Existing research, education, residential, commercial and public-service fabrics are reorganized so that AI-era production and daily life coexist.

### 3. AI re-embodiment

AI should become spatially legible through public-service interfaces, edge compute, low-speed robots, intelligent mobility, adaptive facilities, test/validation spaces and human-supervised operations — not remain only in apps, dashboards and slogans.

### 4. Governance re-embodiment

Urban design is treated as a living loop:

`design → pilot → observe → evaluate → adapt / rollback → scale`

The physical city therefore contains places and interfaces for testing, maintenance, public feedback, human fallback and reconfiguration.

## Complete-design requirement

This concept is only viable if the re-embodiment logic changes decisions across all major urban-design systems:

- innovation ecosystem and industry;
- land use and urban renewal;
- buildings and ground-floor interfaces;
- mobility and logistics;
- municipal/new infrastructure;
- blue-green and public-space networks;
- the three key areas;
- AI scenarios and personas;
- Jing-Zhang / Zhongguancun / AI cultural narrative;
- phasing and long-term operations.

If it remains merely a robotics/service-station idea, Candidate 01 fails.

## T-shaped differentiation

Horizontal layer: complete urban-design response.

Vertical technical depth:

1. embodied-AI public infrastructure;
2. shared re-embodiment stations for heterogeneous physical agents;
3. minimum-sufficient sensing / Know-Enough requirement inversion;
4. robust resource/task scheduling;
5. normal–degraded–fallback–human-takeover operation.

## Falsification tests

Reject or fundamentally rewrite Candidate 01 if any of the following remains true after the next design pass:

- the same spatial plan would work unchanged if all embodied-AI ideas were deleted;
- the concept cannot produce distinct, credible designs for all three key areas;
- “re-embodiment” becomes only a metaphor without land-use/building/public-space consequences;
- the public realm is subordinated to robots rather than people;
- the proposal requires permanent over-sensing or proprietary infrastructure;
- the heritage narrative becomes superficial technology decoration;
- a strong existing submission already implements the same complete design more convincingly.
