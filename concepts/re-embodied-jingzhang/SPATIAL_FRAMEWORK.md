# Spatial Framework v0.1

## Three scales

### 43.6 km² — regional innovation metabolism

Purpose: connect research, engineering, testing, entrepreneurship, finance/services, urban application and public feedback into one visible AI innovation metabolism.

Provisional value-chain logic:

`research & talent → engineering & compute → prototype/test → enterprise/service → urban scenario → public feedback → new research`

The official three areas and two wings remain the organizing framework rather than being replaced by a new invented geography.

### 11.4 km² — complete urban-design body

Working spatial structure:

## **一脊 · 三核 · 两网 · 多站**

### 一脊 — 京张再具身公共脊 / Re-Embodied Civic Spine

A continuous composite corridor combining:

- Jing-Zhang heritage narrative;
- blue-green and slow-mobility continuity;
- public-life sequence;
- innovation/exhibition interfaces;
- distributed AI-service/test nodes;
- wayfinding and recognition system.

The spine should not become a technology exhibition strip. Daily public use remains primary.

### 三核 — differentiated urban engines

1. **众智园：自主创新与具身验证核** — R&D, engineering, pilot/test, shared technical infrastructure and embodied-AI service support.
2. **北京 AI 原点社区：人才生活与开放创新核** — mixed work/life, community services, learning, entrepreneurship, third places and public AI experience.
3. **大钟寺：站城交换与产业服务核** — transit-oriented exchange, enterprise services, commercial/public activity, conference/exhibition and international interface.

### 两网

**Human Civic Network**

Heritage + blue-green + walking/cycling + accessible public spaces + community/public-service interfaces.

**Embodied Intelligence Network**

Edge compute + public AI interfaces + test/validation zones + low-speed robot routes where appropriate + charging/service/recovery points + operational data contracts.

The second network is subordinate to the safety, dignity and accessibility requirements of the first.

### 多站

“站” is a reusable urban component, not necessarily a railway station:

- re-embodiment/service station;
- public AI service station;
- developer/innovation exchange station;
- heritage interpretation station;
- human fallback/service recovery station;
- event and temporary-use service station.

## Cross-section principle

Where the spine meets neighborhoods, prefer layered interfaces:

`quiet/private frontage ← local street ← mixed public edge ← civic spine ← mixed public edge ← local street ← quiet/private frontage`

High-activity AI display/test uses should cluster at suitable public or innovation interfaces, not penetrate sensitive residential/education/medical edges by default.

## Urban-form principle

Without official planning controls, do not freeze numeric FAR or height. Conceptually:

- preserve and reuse viable fabric where possible;
- concentrate higher activity near existing transit and innovation nodes when later verified feasible;
- step down massing toward heritage/blue-green/public-space edges;
- activate ground floors on civic interfaces;
- protect quiet residential/service interiors;
- use modular/convertible ground and first-floor space where future AI/innovation uses may change rapidly.

All numeric controls remain future official-data dependent.
