# Three Key Areas — Detailed-Design Hypotheses v0.1

All three polygons and detailed siting remain provisional until authoritative spatial data is available. The purpose of this file is to define **different urban-design logics**, not final parcel decisions.

## 1. 众智园 AI 自主创新加速区

### Positioning

**“造出来、测明白、修得好”** — the north-end engine for AI engineering, validation and embodied-system support.

### Spatial structure

- innovation-production clusters linked by a walkable research/public-space network;
- controlled test interfaces separated from ordinary pedestrian conflict zones;
- shared loading/service edges rather than unmanaged robot/logistics occupation of civic space;
- blue-green buffers and comfortable daily public spaces between technical facilities.

### Building and renewal logic

- prioritize reuse/adaptation of suitable R&D/industrial/office stock;
- create flexible lab–office–pilot interfaces;
- reserve conceptual shared technical service space for maintenance, charging, tool/module exchange and safe recovery;
- make public-facing ground floors legible where research/test activities are suitable for exhibition or community learning.

### Mobility

- transit/walk/cycle first for people;
- time/space-separated low-speed robot logistics where needed;
- shared curb/loading management;
- no assumption of new road redlines or autonomous-vehicle priority.

### Public realm

Research courtyards, shaded walking loops, public demo terraces and accessible learning interfaces should prevent the area from becoming a closed industrial campus.

### Signature AI element

**Re-Embodiment Depot / 再具身总站（concept）** — shared service/maintenance/test infrastructure for heterogeneous embodied agents, combined with a public observability gallery explaining safety, repair and human takeover.

### Main risk

Over-technologizing the district and converting public space into a robotics proving ground. Human daily use must remain the first design constraint.

## 2. 北京 AI 原点社区

### Positioning

**“AI 进入日常，但人仍能退出 AI”** — talent life, learning, community service and open innovation.

### Spatial structure

- mixed work/life/learning blocks;
- a chain of third places connecting community, entrepreneurship and heritage/public space;
- quiet interiors for housing, education and care;
- active public edges for community services, learning and small-scale innovation.

### Building and renewal logic

- reuse ground/first floors for shared learning, maker, community and public-service spaces;
- preserve residential viability rather than turning the community into an exhibition park;
- enable reversible small interventions before large redevelopment;
- increase mixed-use convenience through verified existing-building opportunities rather than assumed demolition.

### Mobility

Walkability, accessibility and station/community connection are primary. Robot/service trials are limited to clearly governed low-speed roles.

### Public realm

Study gardens, intergenerational community spaces, evening-safe walking routes and public-service nodes should support students, researchers, families, service workers and older residents.

### Signature AI element

**AI Origin Commons / 原点公共客厅** — a human-first civic interface where residents can access AI-assisted public services, learn what data is used, select non-AI alternatives and participate in scenario evaluation.

### Main risk

Gentrification-by-AI branding and digital exclusion. Affordable daily life, ordinary services and human channels must be protected.

## 3. 大钟寺 AI 产业集聚区

### Positioning

**“把创新交换变成城市生活”** — enterprise service, transit exchange, commercial/public interface and international communication.

### Spatial structure

- station-area walking continuity and legible arrival sequence;
- enterprise-service and exhibition/conference interfaces near high-accessibility nodes;
- public plazas and evening uses connected to the civic spine;
- service/logistics edges separated from primary pedestrian public space.

### Building and renewal logic

- adaptable commercial/office/event floors;
- visible innovation-service frontages;
- selective renewal of underused interfaces when later verified;
- conceptual massing should strengthen public-space enclosure without asserting statutory height/FAR.

### Mobility

Prioritize transit transfer, walking continuity, cycling and time-managed curb functions. Autonomous/robotic service should solve last-100-meter or operations problems rather than add spectacle.

### Public realm

A gateway sequence linking transit, public square, enterprise/event functions and the Jing-Zhang cultural spine.

### Signature AI element

**Jingzhang Future Exchange / 京张未来换乘厅（concept）** — not merely a transport hall, but a public interface for enterprises, developers, events and AI-enabled city experiences with explicit human service fallback.

### Main risk

Becoming a generic TOD + AI showroom. Heritage narrative, everyday public use and enterprise service must be spatially integrated rather than branded separately.
