# C01 V0.3 Rewrite Decisions

## Rewrite thesis

The proposal will no longer begin with robots, Agents or a re-embodiment station. It begins with the city problem:

> Jing-Zhang needs a continuous, ordinary, climate-capable public ground connecting unequal innovation and living conditions. New AI services are admitted only when a public task proves a physical support gap, and that support must remain bounded, recoverable and reusable.

The kernel therefore appears late in the argument as a consequence of city design, not its substitute.

## Spatial thesis

```text
ordinary public continuity spine
+ transverse city connections
+ conventional service-edge audit/repair
+ selectively embedded distributed cells
+ few specialised backends
```

No new robot land use, universal station, continuous robot lane or central coordination brain is proposed.

## What changes from earlier C01

| Earlier tendency | V0.3 correction |
|---|---|
| broad “re-embodiment” vocabulary | one narrow task→resource→space chain |
| same station language in three areas | three non-identical sections: backend / civic front / adoption edge |
| technology appears as corridor identity | ordinary public/blue-green/mobility structure appears first |
| sharing assumed beneficial | seven profiles show sharing often loses; dedicated/no-build remains valid |
| public space hosts technology | public space hosts ordinary life and accountability only; technical work back-of-house |
| fallback described operationally | human room, route, safe holding, shutdown and recovery are drawn programme |
| “minimum sensing” as ethics slogan | reproducible error/TTL/privacy inversion chooses a minimum bundle |
| station as permanent landmark | universal station deleted; removable interfaces and ordinary-use conversion |

## Required narrative order

1. city and site problem;

## Name pressure test

| Option | Benefit | Risk | When admissible |
|---|---|---|---|
| `KEEP CURRENT NAME` — 再具身京张 / Re-Embodied Jingzhang | continuity with Candidate 01 and Owner’s technical thesis | over-technical, difficult for the public, easily confused with reassembly/reuse and implies robots are the city subject | only if public testing shows comprehension and the city-first subtitle dominates |
| `RENAME TOTAL CONCEPT` | lets ordinary city/public ground lead; reduces collision with reassembly and robotics | may lose the memorable technical kernel and accumulated research identity | if professional/Owner review finds the current name frames the wrong design object |
| `KEEP RE-EMBODIMENT AS SUBSYSTEM` | preserves the narrow method while allowing a city-first total concept name | requires disciplined two-level brand architecture | if the urban concept survives independently and the subsystem is described as service architecture, not the whole city |

No final name is selected in this run. `KEEP RE-EMBODIMENT AS SUBSYSTEM` is the strongest non-binding architecture hypothesis, but final naming remains an Owner/Chief Architect decision.
