## Required section changes

1. Place technical logistics on hardened/back sides, not the landscape/public spine.
2. Draw drainage/canopy/refuge as prior spatial zones.
3. Move technical waiting indoors and cap/refuse peak demand.
4. Separate contaminated cleaning/waste from public/green drainage.
5. Preserve ordinary maintenance labour and access before adding technical maintenance.
6. Make temporary/event modules close first under heat, rain, poor air or ecological conflict.
