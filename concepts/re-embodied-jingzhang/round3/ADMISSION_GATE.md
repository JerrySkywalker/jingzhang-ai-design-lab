```text
C01_SPATIAL_ADMISSION=PASS
```

C01 passes the narrow question “does the kernel force urban spatial choices?” It does **not** pass parcel placement, building feasibility, transport design, landscape performance, official-boundary or implementation gates. ## Kernel admitted for testing

```text
public task
→ required observable state
→ tolerated uncertainty / TTL
→ minimum sensing / compute / energy / tool / maintenance bundle
→ compatible embodied platform
→ fully shareable / time-shareable / isolated-share / non-shareable decision
```

The kernel is one chain. Removing inversion, compatibility, allocation/isolation or physical consequence invalidates the claim.

## Unavoidable spatial consequences

The kernel changes the city only in bounded places:

1. **No new AI land-use category.** Cells embed in lawful mixed-use, research, civic, building-service or transport-support space. A robotics parcel fails the low-demand counterfactual.
2. **A front–controlled middle–back sequence.** Public accountability and human help face the city; supervised handoff/safe holding sits behind; noisy maintenance, energy, stores, wash and technical logistics remain back-of-house.
3. **Multiple failure domains.** A shared programme cannot occupy one fire/power/network domain. Independent shutdown and manual recovery routes are spatial requirements.
4. **Compatibility-separated rooms and flows.** Human-contact, clean payload and hazardous maintenance do not share generic storage, wash or waste routes.
5. **A managed service edge, not a robot lane.** Arrival/recovery uses conventional service streets/courts and time windows; the heritage promenade remains pedestrian/cycle/public-life space.
6. **Demand-gated reversibility.** Ordinary loading, maintenance, civic-room and operations uses must occupy the space if embodied demand is 80% lower.
7. **Staff presence and welfare.** Operator overload is a hard stop. The programme therefore needs a real supervision/work/welfare room, not a remote dashboard fiction.

These changes are small compared with the total city. That is a strength only if the proposal stops branding every street as infrastructure.

## Counterfactual admission

### AI off

Walking, shade, drainage, ordinary civic rooms, loading, maintenance, visitor help and public service continue. The controlled test layer closes; screens become static notices; rooms revert to named ordinary uses. `PASS`.

### 80% lower embodied adoption

Dedicated/shared build decisions revert to existing service rooms and one reversible pilot. The distributed network is not built. `PASS`, provided phasing remains demand-gated.

### Peak demand

The model rejects the assumption that sharing saves capacity. Event/coincident peaks require dedicated or capped provision; public realm cannot absorb overflow queues. `PASS_FALSIFICATION`.

### One shared cell fails

The paired cell and manual service continue selectively; the failed cell is isolated and recovered from a service route. A single mega-hub fails. `PASS_DISTRIBUTED / FAIL_UNIVERSAL`.

### Rain/heat

Technical queuing cannot occupy shade, refuge or drainage. Cells use hardened service space; public waiting retains shelter and non-digital help. `REQUIRES_SECTION_CHANGE`, incorporated in the three section prototypes.

### Event and maintenance

Event modules are temporary; event command remains non-shareable. Maintenance access, waste, cleaning and staff welfare are drawn as permanent ordinary service requirements. `PASS_CONCEPTUAL`.

## What is ordinary urban back-of-house

Loading bays, building operations rooms, dry stores, staff rooms, conventional low-voltage rooms, wash-down space and ordinary maintenance access are not new AI infrastructure. C01 may ask that these be adaptable, correctly separated and capable of hosting removable modules. It may not relabel normal good urban servicing as innovation.

The genuinely kernel-specific additions are limited to:

- task-derived compatibility/segregation schedule;
- removable standardized module interfaces where demand is verified;
- physically independent failure cells where sharing is used;
- safe recovery/incident closure position;
- public accountability/help interface tied to current service state;
- task-specific reset, hygiene and isolation rules.

## Kill conditions carried forward

1. A professional section cannot fit independent cells and recovery without taking canopy, drainage, refuge, active frontage or safe pedestrian space.
2. Observed demand does not stagger or platforms lack stable compatible interfaces; dedicated/ordinary service space is sufficient.
3. The public accountability front is private, app-gated or has no qualified human service.
4. Three key-area sections collapse into the same bay-and-screen type.
5. C01 continues to lead with Agent/robot/fallback/charging vocabulary rather than city/site problems.
6. The candidate cannot explain why the railway/public-space structure changes phasing and interface design beyond branding.
7. Building/fire/energy/transport/landscape evidence requires a new campus-scale depot or robot-only land use.

## Admission disposition

The narrow kernel survived because it generated **different build/no-build decisions**, rejected its own universal station, separated resources that cannot be shared, and changed sections under failure/low-demand conditions. It now warrants a city-design rewrite and professional section review. It does not warrant a formal fork or a final winner decision.
