## Section corrections already incorporated

- Zhongzhiyuan section ends in a protected drainage/canopy edge and places logistics on the hardened side.
- AI Origin separates public shade/refuge from the light service cell and keeps the quiet edge free of heavy backend.
- Dazhongsi makes the sheltered public arrival primary and lets temporary technical staging close under heat/rain/event overload.
- The heritage/public spine is S0: no routine logistics, charging, broken-device holding or robot lane.

## Hard refusal

If a task requires loss of functioning soil, canopy, drainage, habitat continuity or public refuge before its public benefit is demonstrated, the task is refused or moved to an existing hardened backend. “AI optimisation later” is not mitigation.
