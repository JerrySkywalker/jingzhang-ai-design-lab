## Changes to the candidate

- **Land use:** no robot/AI land-use category; ordinary city functions remain the primary allocation.
- **Public rooms:** AI Origin and Dazhongsi require real staffed civic/help functions, not display pavilions.
- **Routes:** public/accessible/late routes are independent of technical logistics and digital wayfinding.
- **Building interfaces:** staff welfare, ordinary operations, toilets/refuge leads, loading/waste and human help are explicit.
- **Service cells:** queue and operating-hours caps; cells close before public routes or refuge are compromised.
- **Fallback:** non-AI service is a staffed spatial programme, not a paragraph in software governance.
- **Phasing:** Phase 0 repairs ordinary-day gaps before any cell pilot.
