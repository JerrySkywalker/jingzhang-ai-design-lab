# C01 Round-3 Spatial Programme

## City-first premise

The Jing-Zhang task is first an incomplete urban-ground problem: a heritage/public-space line must connect unequal research, learning, residential, service and metropolitan conditions while protecting ordinary access, climate refuge and long-term maintenance. AI infrastructure enters only where a public task exposes a physical support gap that ordinary urban service space cannot meet.

## 43.6 km² coordination scale

The regional structure is not a device network. It is a **task and compatibility federation** connecting universities, research, firms, capital/service institutions, talent, compute/data governance and public test opportunities through bounded packets:

- AI Origin frames/rejects public tasks and knowledge-transfer needs;
- Zhongzhiyuan verifies compatibility, isolation and technical recovery where a controlled backend is justified;
- Dazhongsi tests adoption, service labour and metropolitan/enterprise interfaces typologically;
- the Zhongguancun service wing supplies legal, IP, investment-readiness and enterprise services without promising programmes or funding;
- the Xiaoyuehe scenario wing supplies public/maintenance/environmental questions without turning residents or ecology into test subjects;
- wider Haidian/Beijing networks exchange methods, standards and failure evidence, not unverified live data or assumed institutional commitments.

Physical machines do not need to travel along the full corridor. The shared regional object is a requirements/compatibility method; heavy support remains selective and local.

## 11.4 km² overall design structure

### Primary skeleton

```text
public continuity spine
    + transverse ordinary-city connections
    + conventional service-edge network
    + distributed/adaptable building cells only where admitted
```

1. **Public continuity spine:** Jing-Zhang heritage/public realm, walking, cycling, shade, refuge, culture and non-digital navigation. It carries no routine robot logistics.
2. **Transverse ordinary-city connections:** connect neighbourhoods, campuses/research institutions, public services and transit leads. Exact routes require field and transport verification.
3. **Conventional service-edge network:** existing streets, courts and loading conditions are audited for goods, waste, maintenance, accessible pick-up and emergency access before any automated service.
4. **Selective cell network:** existing service rooms/bays first; reversible fit-out second; new building only after demand, compatibility and failure-domain evidence.

### Land use and functional layout

No new land-use type is proposed. Programme sits within confirmed mixed-use, research/innovation, civic/community, commercial/transport-support or building-service space. Provisional geometry and OSM building footprints cannot establish current use or availability.

### Public-space system

Public space has three priorities in order: ordinary free use and access; living-systems performance; accountable optional innovation. Public-facing AI content is limited to human help, service state, responsible operator, stop/appeal and occasional bounded learning. Queueing, charging, repair, storage, calibration and incident recovery remain off the public spine.

### Blue-green system

Soil, canopy, shade, drainage, habitat and refuge are prior constraints. Technical cells use hardened/back-of-house space; no sensor or service slab may be justified by saying AI will later optimise the loss. Landscape survey decides whether any crossing or cabinet is admissible.

### Mobility and logistics

- walking/wheeling and cycling continuity are physically legible with AI off;
- public transport and conventional delivery remain the baseline;
- embodied services use capped time windows at conventional service edges;
- no continuous robot lane, underground robot passage or exclusive curb network;
- broken equipment has a human recovery route;
- ordinary goods, waste, staff access and emergency operations are not displaced.

### Urban renewal logic

```text
survey ordinary value
→ repair public ground and service access
→ adapt an existing room/bay
→ run one removable cell
→ test a second isolated cell only if sharing evidence exists
→ selectively embed or remove
```

The first permanent investment is good ordinary-city servicing and public realm, not a technology network.

## Three-area programme split

| Area | Urban role | Admissible kernel-specific programme | Ordinary fallback/use | Excluded programme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zhongzhiyuan | productive R&D/testing/repair edge | two independent controlled cells, segregated stores/wash/isolation, general maintenance and specialist backend, public accountability frontage | ordinary municipal/building maintenance, repair/training and logistics | spectacle on park, universal platform hub, uncontrolled public test loop |
| AI Origin | civic learning/task definition/accountability | accessible task clinic, human service, small module checkout, public failure/change archive, light back-of-house | civic room, learning, project review, public help | heavy repair, battery depot, compulsory digital intake |
| Dazhongsi typology | metropolitan arrival/adoption/service-labour edge | managed short-stay edge, staffed help, temporary service holding, small recovery, event overlay | ordinary loading, accessible pick-up, visitor/worker support | exact station/quadrant claim, heavy repair, permanent device queue |

## Phasing

### Phase 0 — evidence and ordinary repair

Field protocol, service-chain observation, route/service-edge audit, building survey, fire/energy/logistics review, public-task packets and ordinary-day/living-systems fixes. Build no network.

### Phase 1 — one reversible cell

Use one existing service room/bay and one conventional edge. Limit compatible task families, cap hours and keep human alternatives. One cell is a pilot, not critical infrastructure.

### Phase 2 — isolation test

Only after observed stagger/compatibility, add a second independent cell. Test power/network/cell/contamination/operator failures and 80% lower demand.

### Phase 3 — selective embedding or removal

Professional teams compare ordinary, dedicated and distributed-shared provision by area. Expansion follows evidence; otherwise modules leave and rooms retain ordinary uses.

## Drawing boundary

The common contextual base supports a relationship diagram and typological sections. It cannot place cells on parcels, certify street access, identify reusable buildings or resolve Dazhongsi. Those are explicit professional admission tasks.
