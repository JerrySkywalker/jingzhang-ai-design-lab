# Implementation and Operations v0.1

The final competition proposal must not claim confirmed government schedules, budgets or approvals. This file defines a conceptual implementation logic.

## Phase 0 — Evidence and spatial correction

Before hard design commitments:

- obtain/refresh authoritative boundaries and controls when available;
- validate existing buildings, transit interfaces, public spaces and constraints;
- conduct limited structured field observation where useful;
- refresh benchmark and Issue/PR review;
- replace provisional assumptions and recalculate affected geometry/metrics.

## Phase 1 — Low-regret civic improvements

Candidate project families:

- walking/accessibility discontinuity repair;
- wayfinding and heritage interpretation;
- reversible public-space furniture/shade/power/data interfaces;
- community/public-service pilots with human fallback;
- limited research/test interfaces in suitable existing spaces;
- open operational dashboards that explain rather than surveil.

## Phase 2 — Reuse-led urban renewal and shared infrastructure

- adaptive reuse of suitable innovation/community/enterprise-service buildings;
- shared lab/pilot/service interfaces;
- first re-embodiment service hub;
- improved station-to-neighborhood/public-space connections;
- expanded public-space and blue-green continuity where verified feasible;
- formalized operator/maintenance contracts for AI scenarios.

## Phase 3 — Networked embodied-city operations

Only after evidence from earlier phases:

- multiple interoperable re-embodiment/service nodes;
- expanded low-speed embodied services where public benefit is demonstrated;
- shared task/resource scheduling across nodes;
- larger international events and test/validation programs;
- long-term public evaluation and periodic redesign.

## Annual activity architecture — working concept

- **Re-Embodied City Week:** public exhibition of what moved from prototype to safe urban use;
- **Open Agent Design Sprint:** developers, residents and professional teams test urban-design/public-service ideas;
- **Jing-Zhang Engineering & AI Forum:** connects railway engineering heritage to contemporary systems engineering;
- **Embodied AI Public Challenge:** controlled demonstrations focused on safety, maintenance, accessibility and public benefit rather than speed spectacle;
- **Community Review Days:** publish scenario performance, failures and redesign decisions.

All names are working concepts and require later brand review.
