# C02 → C01: Ordinary-Day Completeness Review

Review basis: `experiments/c02-ordinary-day-completeness/NEIGHBOURHOOD_COMPLETENESS_CONTRACT.md`.

## Verdict

C01 has a stronger technical failure model than an ordinary-day city model. It does not currently demonstrate that any area supports eight roles across seven states. Shared infrastructure must be subordinated to, and tested inside, ordinary urban service chains.

| Attack | Evidence-based finding | Required correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technology display | Round-1 public observability/gallery language can turn daily space into a demonstration | show public help/accountability only; keep tests bounded and back-of-house |
| Single spine | A continuous technical/heritage spine may require users to travel for basic needs | count belt services as external until local route/time evidence exists |
| Daily life | Scenario cards do not prove housing, food, childcare, health, toilets or refuge | publish persona-specific physical chains; do not infer availability from land use |
| Ordinary services | AI/robot services receive more detail than fixed, staffed and low-cost alternatives | draw ordinary service first and AI-on as an optional branch |
| Non-AI state | C01 now requires convertible cells, but whole daily chains remain untested | run all three area briefs with digital/shared-system unavailable |
| Existing residents/workers | Current users are represented by generic personas, not evidence | field/community gates; do not claim local need or consent |
| Night/weekend | Technical pilots and innovation venues may operate on narrow schedules | test evening/weekend opening, lighting, staffing, transit and refuge |
| Accessibility | Sensing/fallback cannot repair inaccessible geometry or restricted access | accessible route/building review before assistive technology |
| Service workers | C01 recognizes technicians but under-specifies food, toilet, rest and shifts | add worker chains and welfare space to every cell/area |
| Maintenance labour | Prototype models resource units, not labour conditions or travel | record staffing, qualifications, changeover, manual recovery, storage and welfare |

## Gate result

ORDINARY_DAY_COMPLETENESS_GATE=FAIL_NOT_SITE_TESTED

C01 need not become three complete neighbourhoods. It must demonstrate that its technical system neither hides nor worsens ordinary-city deficits.
