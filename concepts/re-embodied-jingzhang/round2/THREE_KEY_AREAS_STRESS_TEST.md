# Candidate 01 — Three Key Areas Stress Test

## Test rule

Each key area must alter the shared-infrastructure chain in a different way. Repeating a branded station with different façade language is a kill condition. The exact boundaries, buildings, access and land controls are unresolved; the following are functional/spatial hypotheses for later professional testing.

## Zhongzhiyuan — controlled back end

### Required role

Translate heterogeneous platform differences into inspectable compatibility evidence. It is the only key area allowed to consider heavier calibration, quarantine, repair and controlled test space.

### Spatial structure

- a service-side technical court linked to adaptable indoor bays;
- separated clean/general, human-contact and hazardous-tool rooms;
- controlled test loop or court that never borrows the public promenade as an unapproved test lane;
- public observation/accountability edge separated acoustically and physically from work zones;
- two independent shutdown zones and a manual recovery route.

### Ordinary-day value if demand is 80% lower

The same shells serve municipal equipment inspection, cycle/accessibility-device repair, public-space maintenance training, maker work and general logistics. The controlled court returns to ordinary service-yard use. Removable robotic modules leave.

### Failure test

A bay fire, energy isolation or software stop cannot close the public route or the second cell. Platform recovery is by trained people. If the site cannot accommodate separation and recovery without consuming green/public space, the heavy programme is rejected or moved indoors elsewhere.

### Kill trigger

The area is killed as the technical back end if building/fire/service access evidence shows that safe segregation requires a new permanent campus-scale depot or extensive public-realm sacrifice.

## AI Origin — civic requirements and accountability front end

### Required role

Convert daily public/worker tasks into requirements before a device is chosen, preserve negative evidence and decide which tasks should not be automated. This is the Owner's task-inversion method made public, not a repair station.

### Spatial structure

- an accessible requirements clinic and task-scripting room in an existing public-facing ground floor;
- a small checkout/return point for low-risk removable modules;
- a staffed non-digital service and complaint desk;
- a public failure/repair archive and scheduled review room;
- a direct ordinary walking route that remains valid when the clinic is closed.

### Ordinary-day value if demand is 80% lower

Rooms operate as community meeting, accessible service-design, student/project review and public help spaces. The archive becomes an accountable public-service change log. There is no stranded heavy plant.

### Failure test

Digital intake failure switches to paper and face-to-face task scripts; failure of a test platform has no effect on daily access. Participation remains voluntary and does not infer population needs without community process.

### Kill trigger

If there is no lawful, genuinely public and non-app-gated interface, this role cannot be claimed through a campus or private innovation venue.

## Dazhongsi — operational adoption edge

### Required role

Test whether a proven service survives ordinary commercial/transport rhythms, human handover, evening/weekend operation and operator maintenance. It is a light turnaround and accountability interface, not a showroom and not a second technical depot.

### Spatial structure

- a managed short-stay service edge outside the pedestrian-priority route;
- a small staffed recovery/help room and secure temporary holding position;
- conventional delivery and accessible-service alternatives alongside any automation;
- clear separation between passenger/visitor movement, queues, goods, waste and failed equipment;
- published operator, hours, complaint and removal path.

### Ordinary-day value if demand is 80% lower

The edge serves ordinary loading and accessible pick-up; the room becomes staffed visitor/service-worker support and general building operations. No device-specific streetscape remains.

### Failure test

Automation stops without closing retail, transit access or walking. Broken equipment is removed to a technical cell rather than repaired in public. Because the official geometry is provisional, the exact curb, station or quadrant arrangement is `BLOCKED_BY_EVIDENCE`.

### Kill trigger

If transport, property or operating evidence cannot provide a managed service edge without worsening walking, cycling, loading or refuge, this adoption role must be redesigned or removed.

## Cross-area flow

```text
AI Origin: define / reject public task
          ↓ bounded requirement packet
Zhongzhiyuan: verify compatible platform + segregated resource bundle
          ↓ limited release with operator and recovery plan
Dazhongsi: test adoption in ordinary operations
          ↓ failure, labour and public feedback
AI Origin: revise requirement or stop
```

This is a learning flow, not permission to centralize personal data. Physical machines do not need to travel through all three areas, and the heritage park is not a logistics conveyor.
