# Candidate 01 Round-2 Spatial Consequences

## Translation rule

The prototype succeeds only if its compatibility, peak-timing and failure-domain findings change the urban design. The required spatial type is not a citywide robotic network. It is a small number of adaptable, isolatable service cells connected to ordinary building backs and managed street edges while the heritage park remains an unencumbered public realm.

All site locations and dimensions remain `ASSUMPTION` or `CONCEPT`; authoritative geometry, statutory controls, building survey and demand calibration are absent.

## Land use

No new independent land-use category is proposed. A robotics-only parcel would be brittle under the 80%-low-demand counterfactual. Support space should be embedded within lawful mixed-use, research, public-service, transport-support or building-service areas after professional confirmation.

The plan must show three layers:

1. **public continuity layer:** heritage, walking, cycling, shade, refuge and non-digital public service;
2. **managed service-edge layer:** time-windowed arrival, safe recovery and light exchange outside pedestrian-priority space;
3. **adaptable building layer:** indoor storage, supervision, calibration, general maintenance and utilities.

Permanent technical footprint is subordinate to soil, canopy, drainage, emergency access and daily civic use.

## Buildings

An admissible cell has distinct front, middle and back conditions:

- **public front:** operator identity, current system state, help/complaint route and AI-off alternative; no compulsory registration;
- **controlled middle:** quarantine/safe-recovery position, supervised handover and bounded test interface;
- **back-of-house:** general bay, separately locked incompatible tools, replaceable energy interface, parts/storage, ventilation/fire separation subject to professional design, technician and supervisor work/welfare space.

Preference order is existing service space → reversible fit-out → adaptable addition → new stand-alone building only after demonstrated demand. Device-specific towers, underground robotic passages and permanent embedded tracks are rejected.

## Street and curb

- Locate arrival and recovery on managed service streets or building courts, not on the main heritage promenade.
- Use time windows and small holding capacity; do not reserve continuous curb before demand is evidenced.
- Keep pedestrian and cycle priority geometrically legible without relying on AI negotiation.
- Provide a non-automated towing/recovery route and human access to every cell.
- Do not place charging, tool exchange, queueing or broken equipment within refuge, tree-rooting or drainage zones.
- Preserve emergency and ordinary goods access; platform choreography is never a substitute for a safe section.

## Public space

Visible interfaces should be limited to accountability and learning: responsible operator, service state, safe stop, public help, failure record and occasional bounded demonstration. Maintenance machinery, batteries, noisy calibration, tool storage, personal or commercially sensitive data and routine queuing belong back-of-house.

Public space must work when every technical screen is dark. Demonstrations are guests in the public realm, not its organizing purpose.

## Blue-green system

The service system cannot claim a right to continuous park frontage. No hub or slab may be placed before existing trees, rooting zones, infiltration, drainage paths, habitat continuity and seasonal refuge are surveyed. Environmental sensing may reveal a problem but cannot compensate for lost canopy, sealed soil or a missing drainage route.

Cells should use already hardened service space where feasible; routes across green space are a last resort requiring landscape, drainage, arboricultural and accessibility review.

## Phasing and anti-ruin strategy

### Phase 0 — evidence before construction

- observe and script a small set of real public/maintenance tasks;
- derive state, uncertainty, TTL and human duty requirements;
- inspect candidate existing indoor service spaces and managed edges;
- publish compatibility and non-shareability matrices;
- run tabletop failure and 80%-low-demand tests.

### Phase 1 — reversible single-cell pilot

Use an existing or leased indoor bay with removable modules, one managed service edge and a staffed manual recovery path. Test no more than a few compatible task families. A single cell is acceptable only as a bounded pilot, not as citywide critical infrastructure.

### Phase 2 — two-cell isolation test

Only if staggered demand and compatibility are observed, add a second cell with independent shutdown and test rerouting. Do not install a permanent network until shared benefits survive peak and failure evidence.

### Phase 3 — selective embedding

Professional urban-design, building, transport and landscape teams determine whether limited cell types belong in other existing buildings. Growth follows measured service demand; lack of demand triggers conversion or removal.

## Three genuinely different spatial consequences

| Area | Round-2 role | Physical consequence | What is explicitly absent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Zhongzhiyuan | controlled interoperability and heavy recovery | adaptable technical bay cluster on a service side; calibration/quarantine; segregated tools; controlled test court; technician access | no public robot spectacle on the heritage park; no claim that every platform shares every interface |
| AI Origin | public-task definition and accountability | small requirements clinic, accessible task-scripting room, public status/failure archive, human-supervision training and light checkout; connects public questions to controlled tests | no heavy battery/repair depot; no campus claim without authority |
| Dazhongsi | adoption, turnaround and service-interface test | managed short-stay edge, light recovery room, staffed AI-off help, delivery/visitor handover and operator feedback; heavy failures return to a technical cell | no replicated “station”; no assumption of exact station quadrants, land ownership or road geometry |

The system is therefore not `re-embodiment station × 3`. It is a controlled back end, a civic requirements interface and an operational adoption edge.
