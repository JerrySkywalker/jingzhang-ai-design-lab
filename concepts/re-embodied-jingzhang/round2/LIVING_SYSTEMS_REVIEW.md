## NON_NEGOTIABLE_SPATIAL_CHANGES

- Locate the first cells in existing hardened indoor/service space, not presumed green space.
- Draw people-first refuge, shade, drainage and AI-off circulation before equipment zones.
- Break the system into isolatable cells with manual recovery; reject a single central mega-hub.
- Separate heavy work, human-contact tools and public interfaces.
- Demonstrate ordinary-city conversion at 80% lower demand.

## EVIDENCE_GAPS_NOT_DESIGN_LICENSES

- existing tree/soil/habitat inventory;
- drainage, flood and utility constraints;
- microclimate and seasonal route conditions;
- exact building/service access, fire and ownership conditions;
- lawful public access, operating responsibility and maintenance labour;
- site-calibrated task demand and peak coincidence.
