# Candidate 01 Round-2 Unique Kernel

## Verdict

Candidate 01 cannot own `Agent`, `robot`, `digital twin`, `open platform`, `testbed`, `reconfiguration`, `minimum data`, `human handover` or `fallback`. Merged proposals already use these as central ideas or mature operating devices. Treating their combination as novelty would be vocabulary stacking.

The narrow residual kernel is:

> **CONCEPT:** derive the minimum physical support bundle from public-task requirements, then allocate only compatible sensing, compute, energy, tool, storage, recovery and maintenance resources across changing heterogeneous embodied platforms through spatially separated failure cells.

This is one joined proposition. If task inversion does not alter the resource bundle, if compatibility does not constrain sharing, or if the shared system does not alter buildings, service edges and phasing, the kernel collapses.

## C01_UNIQUE_KERNEL

Maximum retained differentiators:

1. **Task-to-physical-resource inversion.** Start with a public task, required state, tolerable uncertainty and time-to-live; derive a minimum sensing/compute/energy/tool/maintenance bundle. Do not start with a device catalogue.
2. **Bounded interoperability for heterogeneous platforms.** Share only resources that pass a compatibility and duty-of-care matrix. Human-contact tools, hazardous tools, platform-certified battery components and differently qualified supervision remain segregated.
3. **Urban failure-cell architecture.** Put universal building shells, service edges and recovery space into multiple isolatable cells; pool removable modules when demand is staggered while preventing a central-hub outage from disabling unrelated public tasks.

## Evidence from the minimal prototype

`experiments/c01-shared-embodied-infrastructure/results.json` is explicitly `SYNTHETIC`, `NOT_SITE_CALIBRATED` and `NOT_PERFORMANCE_EVIDENCE`. It demonstrates structural conditions only:

- minimum sensing can be derived without choosing every available sensor;
- sharing applies to a subset of compatible modules and bays, not to all resources;
- staggered peaks can reward pooling;
- coincident peaks can make dedicated provision lower-index;
- shared failure domains require spatial cells and manual recovery.

The experiment supports testability, not site feasibility.

## Survival condition

C01 survives Round 2 only as a major rewrite around this narrow kernel. A proposal that retains the earlier broad smart-city vocabulary, one branded “re-embodiment station” in each key area, or a central dispatch brain without spatial consequences fails this test.

C01_KILL_PRESSURE=CRITICAL

A narrow, falsifiable kernel remains, but the nearest open proposal already occupies common sockets, robot co-testing, interoperability, standard workshops, maintenance and disconnectable resources.
