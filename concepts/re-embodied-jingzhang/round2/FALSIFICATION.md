# Candidate 01 Round-2 Falsification

## Starting hypothesis

The candidate is valuable only if shared physical support for changing heterogeneous embodied systems alters the city and performs better than dedicated provision under identifiable conditions. Generic AI capability, broad scenario coverage and sophisticated orchestration do not count as survival evidence.

## Counterfactual A — remove all shared physical infrastructure

**Result: FAIL for the Round-1 spatial formulation.**

The inherited north-south heritage spine, cross-links, blue-green network, mixed functions and three official key areas remain recognizable after removing shared hubs. This shows that shared infrastructure was previously an overlay, not a generating spatial rule.

The candidate can be repaired only if Round 2 makes the following indispensable:

- a limited network of service edges and adaptable ground-floor shells;
- explicit separation of pedestrian space from charging, loading and recovery;
- multiple isolatable cells rather than one central hub;
- different heavy, civic and adoption interfaces in the three key areas;
- conversion rules that return every technical space to ordinary logistics, repair or public-service use.

If these do not appear in the next drawn plan and sections, C01 should be killed regardless of technical elegance.

## Counterfactual B — every robot type builds dedicated facilities

The synthetic experiment shows the real trade rather than assuming the answer. Dedicated provision duplicates sensor modules, energy ports, maintenance bays and staff capacity when task peaks are staggered, and tends to multiply private loading doors and reserved curb. Yet it bounds failures and avoids changeover, travel and interoperability costs.

When all task peaks coincide, dedicated provision wins the experiment's synthetic index. Therefore the urban rule is not “share everything”; it is:

1. share only demonstrably compatible resources with different peak timing;
2. segregate human-contact, hazardous, certified and duty-specific resources;
3. preserve dedicated safety capacity where correlated failure is unacceptable;
4. require service-level evidence before consolidating a facility.

## Counterfactual C — future platforms change completely

Reusable elements are limited to building and street capacity:

- clear-span ground-floor or service-bay shells;
- ordinary doors, turning and loading space sized through professional design;
- protected but replaceable utility spines;
- ventilated storage, manual workbenches and human welfare/supervision rooms;
- externally accessible safe-recovery points;
- public status/help interfaces independent of a vendor.

Proprietary battery packs, mounts, sensors, rails, firmware, radio protocols and robotic choreography are not assumed reusable. Permanent tracks, embedded device-specific markers and a single connector standard are rejected. If the concept depends on those, this counterfactual kills it.

## Counterfactual D — embodied-system demand is 80% below expectation

No independent land-use class or monument-sized depot is justified. The minimum pilot uses leased or existing indoor service space and removable modules. At low demand:

- bays revert to municipal/public-space maintenance, cycle repair, accessible-mobility support or ordinary last-mile handling;
- storage becomes general maintenance/event storage;
- energy interfaces are removed or used for compatible small mobility equipment only after safety review;
- public status interfaces become staffed help and service-information points;
- no technical object remains in the heritage promenade merely to preserve the brand.

Any space without a credible AI-off ordinary-city use is a prospective technical ruin and must not be built.

## Counterfactual E — automation stops

Pedestrian movement, shade, refuge, drainage, heritage interpretation, conventional delivery windows, public help, emergency access and human maintenance must continue. Automated lanes do not receive priority over the basic public route. The service cells may close their equipment rooms, but ordinary maintenance and recovery access remain usable.

This condition is partly occupied by Every Sense Jing-Zhang's `AI ON = AI OFF` proposition. C01 cannot claim it as distinctive; it is a minimum acceptance condition.

## Jing-Zhang specificity test

**Current result: CONCERN.** The task-inversion/shared-resource kernel could apply to many AI districts. Replacing “Jing-Zhang” with another innovation corridor leaves the systems logic largely intact.

What cannot be generic in a surviving version:

- the railway-heritage park is the continuous public address, not a robot lane or equipment trench;
- service cells respond to the belt's sequence of research/controlled validation, near-campus public problem definition and southern adoption/transport interface rather than repeat one kit three times;
- cross-corridor seams, existing buildings and back-of-house conditions determine where sharing is possible; no exact answer can be claimed until official geometry and survey evidence exist;
- cultural interpretation connects the history of interoperable public infrastructure and independent engineering to present-day repairability and public accountability, without pretending the historical railway endorses robotics.

If those site relationships cannot be demonstrated through professional mapping and field evidence, Jing-Zhang specificity remains too weak for advancement.

## Strongest kill argument

Even after stripping occupied terminology, C01 may be a systems-engineering thesis with a generic service-building typology. The current 11.4 km² framework does not yet prove that public tasks, resource compatibility and failure cells generate a better urban structure than an ordinary heritage-park-led mixed-use plan. That is the prosecution's strongest case.

## Provisional result

C01 is not cleared to advance unchanged. The prototype preserves a narrow testable kernel, but Counterfactual A fails the existing spatial formulation and the specificity test remains unresolved.
