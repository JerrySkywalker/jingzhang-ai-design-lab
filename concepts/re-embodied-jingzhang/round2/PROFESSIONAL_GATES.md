# Candidate 01 — Professional and Human Gates

Professional dependencies are not blockers to research. They define what the next phase must obtain and prevent the Agent or Owner from claiming expertise or facts they do not possess.

## CAN_BE_DONE_BY_AGENT_NOW

- maintain the collision inventory and trace claims to exact proposal evidence;
- run/review the synthetic task-resource model and sensitivity tests;
- test logical compatibility, non-shareability, failure domains and 80%-low-demand conversion;
- maintain scenario, evidence-gap and assumption registers;
- draft alternative spatial briefs and required drawing lists without asserting site fit.

## CAN_BE_DONE_BY_OWNER

Core contribution:

- lead task/state/uncertainty/TTL requirement elicitation;
- define shared-infrastructure architecture and interface boundaries;
- construct compatibility matrices and minimum-sensing logic;
- analyse normal, degraded, failed and human-takeover modes;
- design robust allocation/failure-cell rules and reproducible prototypes;
- reject unnecessary sensing or automation through evidence.

Supporting contribution:

- conduct bounded, non-intrusive structured observation of route/service tasks;
- help architects translate system zones into a requirements brief;
- implement versioned scenario/evidence tooling.

Weak fit:

- independently deciding statutory planning controls, building condition, public-realm dimensions, landscape ecology or community representation.

## REQUIRES_FIELD_OBSERVATION

- actual daily/service tasks and time-of-day peaks;
- present curb/loading/maintenance conflicts and ordinary recovery practices;
- locations where people already wait, rest, seek help or avoid routes;
- visible building service entrances and publicly accessible interfaces;
- observation protocol must avoid personal data and cannot establish ownership or population demand.

## REQUIRES_URBAN_DESIGN_PROFESSIONAL

- convert cell logic into the 11.4 km² spatial structure and sections;
- test three key-area massing, public/private interfaces and heritage-park relationships;
- establish whether the technical kernel genuinely generates urban form;
- coordinate land use, renewal, public realm, visual character and phasing.

## REQUIRES_TRANSPORT

- station/curb/service-edge feasibility and time windows;
- pedestrian/cycle separation, turning, loading, emergency access and recovery routes;
- event-peak and failed-platform operations;
- effects on existing road and transit access.

## REQUIRES_LANDSCAPE

- existing trees, soil, drainage, habitat and microclimate baseline;
- service-cell conflicts with rooting, infiltration, canopy, refuge and park maintenance;
- seasonal comfort and repair after events or equipment failure.

## REQUIRES_BUILDING_SURVEY

- actual use, access, structure, fire separation, ventilation, energy capacity and adaptable service space;
- retain/adapt/new-build choices by building, not by generic typology;
- worker welfare, storage and safe-recovery requirements.

## REQUIRES_OFFICIAL_DATA

- authoritative site/key-area geometry and statutory controls;
- roads, station interfaces, blue/green/protection lines and municipal constraints;
- applicable land-use, ownership/management and project boundaries;
- no provisional geometry may answer these questions.

## REQUIRES_COMMUNITY_PROCESS

- which public tasks are valuable, unwanted or harmful;
- acceptable trade-offs among convenience, space, labour, privacy and safety;
- participation and complaint/exit conditions;
- impacts on existing residents, workers, disabled users, older adults and service/maintenance workers.

## Advancement packet

C01 should not enter full professional design until the Owner's system brief is paired with: one urban-design lead, bounded field evidence, a building/service-space reconnaissance, a transport/service-edge review and a landscape constraints screen. This combination tests the kernel without assuming the Owner is the planner, architect, landscape architect or community representative.
