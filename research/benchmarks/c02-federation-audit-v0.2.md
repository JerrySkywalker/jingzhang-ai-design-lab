## C02_OCCUPIED_TERRITORY

- stay, belong, talent retention and family life;
- complete district and ordinary-day completeness;
- three living yards, civic courts or staffed rooms;
- three unequal innovation areas;
- campus-city and near-campus innovation community;
- corridor plus stations/service network;
- local urban units, five-minute webs and cross-corridor public routes;
- non-AI public services, human fallback and exit.

The name “three complete neighbourhoods” has no standalone differentiating value.

## C02_DISTINCT_KERNEL

Not yet proven. A conditional kernel would be:

> **CONCEPT:** an evidence-gated federation of unequal urban units in which every unit independently passes the same ordinary-day contract, while many-to-many exchanges and cross-unit fallback materially change land use, ground floor, mobility, public realm, labour and phasing.

To be distinct, it must prove all of the following together:

1. local ordinary-day chains do not depend on the continuous corridor for basic services;
2. units have different spatial/building/land/operating models;
3. exchange is many-to-many, not a north→middle→south production diagram;
4. local services survive failure of a shared service;
5. federation produces built and operating consequences beyond arrows.

No current site evidence proves these conditions. Common Ground, Stay Longer, Everyday and Local Urban Units occupy most of the constituent ideas.

## Official evidence for three

`FACT` from the official 2026-05-09 announcement and taskbook:

- three key design areas are required, approximately 192.1 ha, 104.3 ha and 72.0 ha;
- they are described from north to south;
- Zhongzhiyuan emphasizes full-stack autonomy, standards/safety governance and a garden innovation district;
- AI Origin emphasizes a near-campus district, transfer, residential life and campus–park links;
- Dazhongsi emphasizes AI-native business, an urban industrial district, station-city integration and four-quadrant walking;
- taskbook roles differ: full-stack/governance, world-class ecology and AI-native business.

The official announcement is at <https://ghzrzyw.beijing.gov.cn/zhengwuxinxi/tzgg/hd/202605/t20260509_4643047.html>. A Beijing science/technology authority report describes an approximately 9 km heritage park, north–middle–south areas, two wings and north-south/east-west coupling: <https://kw.beijing.gov.cn/xwdt/kcyx/xwdtcyfz/202604/t20260403_4573808.html>.

This supports three strategic/detail-design objects. It does not establish three independent daily catchments.

## Evidence against exactly three complete units

1. Official sources provide no population, facilities, capacity, hours, employment, travel or operating evidence for three independent communities.
2. Exact official overall/key-area polygons remain unavailable; repository geometries are provisional.
3. [Issue #846](https://github.com/open-city-ai/haidian/issues/846) reports the provisional overall polygon does not intersect an OSM-mapped heritage park and is approximately 412.5 m away at closest; this is a warning, not an official correction.
4. [Issue #1029](https://github.com/open-city-ai/haidian/issues/1029) reports the provisional Dazhongsi polygon centroid approximately 2.26 km from Dazhongsi station and containing Beijing North station; neither source is treated as authoritative, but catchment claims are blocked.
5. The official overall task emphasizes a 1–2 km corridor context, north-south continuity, east-west links and station integration, giving the corridor hypothesis a stronger evidence base than three catchments.
6. Mature proposals already translate the prescribed three areas into three courts, living yards, rooms, origins or stations.
7. Local Urban Units #1455 explicitly proposes nine cross-line units, keeping H4+ plausible.

## UNKNOWN

- real key-area boundaries, ownership, roads and buildings;
- number and overlap of daily service catchments;
- facility existence, eligibility, opening times and capacity;
- weekday/evening/weekend/weather chains for eight roles;
- whether the areas are complementary, overlapping or mutually dependent;
- fallback capacity when one local/shared facility fails;
- whether “three” is only a brief decomposition.

## WHAT_DATA_WOULD_DECIDE

1. official/cleared overall and key-area polygons plus transport, water, heritage and management layers;
2. cleared facility register for food, school/childcare, health, toilet, care, refuge, community, worker and night/weekend services;
3. privacy-preserving walking/wheelchair/transit audits across weekday, evening, weekend and rain/heat;
4. official/public aggregate user, employment, visitor and use baselines;
5. the same reproducible travel-time and service-chain test for H2, H3, H4+ and corridor hypotheses;
6. failure of one facility, digital outage, event displacement and cross-unit fallback;
7. urban-design/transport review of consequences for land, ground floor, public realm, movement, labour and phasing.

## Audit result

```text
C02_COLLISION_LEVEL=HIGH_DIRECT
C02_DISTINCT_KERNEL=UNPROVEN
C02_KILL_PRESSURE=CRITICAL
```

The strongest kill argument is simple: the official brief supplies three detail-design assignments, not three complete everyday cities. Without real service-chain and catchment proof, C02 is a one-to-one renaming of the taskbook and collides with more mature schemes.
