# Common-base Data Limitations

## Blocking precision gaps

1. `AUTHORITATIVE_DATA_MISSING` — exact official polygons for all three scales and three key areas remain unavailable.
2. `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` — the provisional key rectangles are fitted to sequence and approximate area, not anchored to named stations or roads.
3. `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` — the provisional overall-design polygon and the OSM-mapped Jing-Zhang heritage-park context were reported not to overlap in Issue #846.
4. `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` — PROV-KEY-003 is about 2.26 km from the contextual Dazhongsi station point and contains the contextual Beijing North Station point; it cannot support parcel/site-specific Dazhongsi design.

## OSM limits

Presence is not availability; absence is not non-existence. POIs do not establish opening hours, capacity, affordability, accessibility, quality or stable operation. Streets do not establish lawful road redlines, crossings, gradients, sidewalk continuity or current construction state. Building footprints do not establish use, ownership, height, structural condition, vacancy or reuse suitability. Green/water tags do not establish soil, canopy, habitat, public access, drainage or flood performance.

## Decision effect

- C01 may develop typological building/street/service interfaces, but not place permanent cells on parcels.
- C02 may compare H2/H3/H4+/Corridor+N as hypotheses, but cannot complete a formal catchment analysis or prove “exactly three”.
- All candidate maps must show provisional constraints with dashed/low-contrast styling.
- Professional and field gates must decide routes, barriers, building interfaces, logistics, climate, maintenance and actual service chains before downselect confidence can become high.
