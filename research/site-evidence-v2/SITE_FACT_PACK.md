# Site Fact Pack v2

Retrieval date: `2026-08-12`

## What the evidence changes

1. `FACT` Jing-Zhang is a layered working corridor: about 6 km of high-speed railway moved underground, Line 13 remains alongside it with changing vertical relations, and transverse roads create unequal crossings. It is not a cleared strip that can be organised by one new line.
2. `FACT` The heritage park is partly delivered. Phase 1 from Qinghua East Road to Zhichun Road is open (`2.5 km`, `16.8 ha`); later works and micro-renewal projects have separate scopes and opening statuses. A proposal must complete interfaces around existing assets rather than redraw the park as a future project.
3. `FACT` The three key areas have unequal real contexts: Qinghe is an operating regional multimodal gateway; Wudaokou–Qinghua East Road West is a campus/park/transit threshold with controlled institutional access and a future 13×15 interchange; Dazhongsi is an operating 12×13 interchange whose brief asks for four-quadrant walking, cycling and public-interface continuity.
4. `FACT` Public campus access is timed, reserved and gate-controlled. Permanent city routes and always-public innovation rooms cannot depend on continuous passage through university interiors.
5. `FACT` Current policy favours retain-and-improve urban renewal, mixed and adaptable use, public-interest facilities, accessible/all-age space and staged project packages. It does not prove that any named building is vacant, unsafe, low-efficiency, openable, or owned by a willing participant.
6. `FACT` Dazhongsi is documented as a heterogeneous renewal unit involving old communities, former industrial/low-efficiency fabric, traditional commerce and municipal infrastructure. This supports a typological renewal problem, not parcel demolition decisions.
7. `FACT` Qinghe and Xiaoyue River are active blue-green project corridors. Xiaoyue River's recorded project scope includes hard-bank and ecological repair, flood protection, maintenance paths and bridge works. A new concept must coordinate with these programmes rather than invent an unconstrained river section.
8. `FACT` The current district strategy links knowledge innovation, technology innovation and industrial cultivation, and also requires campus–park–neighbourhood integration, youth living and public services. Public interest and enterprise space are one implementation problem, not separate appendices.
9. `FACT` The published Jing-Zhang corridor control plan had passed municipal joint review by `2026-04-30`, but the available evidence does not establish final statutory approval or disclose usable parcel controls.
10. `FACT` Exact organizer polygons remain unavailable. Existing key-area rectangles are temporary constraints; the south rectangle is materially displaced from the contextual Dazhongsi station anchor. Site-specific design must therefore be typological/topological until official geometry arrives.

## Three unequal spatial thresholds

### North — Qinghe / Zhongzhiyuan context

- Existing regional gateway combines high-speed/suburban rail, metro, bus, taxi and bicycle interfaces.
- Official task emphasises Five-Ring external access and integrated building–green–water design.
- Qinghe/Xiaoyue projects and the Guangji Bridge heritage context constrain river/interface design.
- `UNKNOWN`: exact competition boundary, last-kilometre conflict locations, carrier availability, building adaptability, utility capacity and current water-project completion.

### Middle — AI Origin / Wudaokou–Qinghua East context

- Existing innovation carriers, public programmes and a delivered park phase mean the design is adjacency/renewal work, not creation from zero.
- Campus gates are controlled; the always-public layer must sit outside or immediately at verified interfaces.
- Wudaokou and Qinghua East Road West already provide transit access; the planned 13×15 interchange demands an adaptable before/after strategy.
- A separately approved seven-node public-space project must be coordinated rather than rebranded.
- `UNKNOWN`: exact gate permeability, building conditions, lease/tenure, ground-floor conversion rights and current interchange completion.

### South — Dazhongsi context

- Line 12 is operating and Dazhongsi is now a 12×13 interchange.
- Official tasks identify four-quadrant walking/cycling, station-to-key-site links, commercial public environment and green-space integration.
- Grade separation, the Xiaozu Tayuan context, heritage and mixed renewal fabrics rule out one generic crossing type.
- `UNKNOWN`: exact key-area polygon, parcel conditions, current surface crossing performance, ownership, station-city development rights and permissible construction.

## Design permissions and prohibitions

Evidence supports:

- unequal fabric and interface typologies;
- reversible, survey-gated retain/repair/adapt/infill sequences;
- public-side institutional interfaces;
- three different mobility thresholds;
- coordination with built/approved/in-delivery projects;
- typological AI space requirements with `NO-BUILD` outcomes.

Evidence does not support:

- precise parcel or redline plans;
- claims that campuses, compounds or labs can open permanently;
- building-specific demolition, vacancy, low-efficiency or structural claims;
- actual firm migration, floor-area demand, freight volumes or logistics conflicts;
- utility capacity, underground feasibility, hydraulic performance or exact heritage-control zones;
- confirmed investment, operator consent, implementation approval or government endorsement.
