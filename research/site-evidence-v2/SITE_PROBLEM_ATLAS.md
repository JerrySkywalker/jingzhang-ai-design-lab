|---|---|---|---|---|

## Required pre-design surveys

- public entrance, gate, opening-hour and accessible-route audit;
- current park/river project completion and connection audit;
- building use, structure, fire, heritage, access, tenant and consent audit;
- transit threshold and four-quadrant walking/cycling audit;
- servicing, loading, waste, repair, emergency and worker-interview audit;
- tree/root/soil, shade, drainage, noise/light and maintenance audit;
- exact official geometry and statutory-control replacement when released.

## Map status language

- `VERIFIED`: solid navy or green, only where public primary evidence supports the claim.
- `CONTEXTUAL`: dotted slate, mainly committed OSM or public issue cross-checks.
- `ASSUMED`: amber dashed, design-research hypothesis only.
- `UNKNOWN`: magenta hollow/question mark.
- `PROVISIONAL`: thin violet dashed outline with no fill.
