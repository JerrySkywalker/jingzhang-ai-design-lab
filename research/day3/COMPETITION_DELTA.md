## Delta decision

### C01

`DIRECT_KILLER_FOUND=false`

The narrow kernel still has a residual opening, but only as the full causal chain:

```text
public task
→ observable state / tolerated uncertainty / TTL
→ minimum resource bundle
→ four-way shareability decision
→ isolated allocation and degraded path
→ a bounded physical spatial consequence
```

The delta removes any remaining right to claim ordinary/validation dual tracks, failure siding, maintenance, charging, shared components, public accountability, reversible stations, controlled back-of-house or human fallback as original. Common Table is especially important: cleaning, isolation, tool storage, handoff and logistics frequently belong to ordinary regulated service space. C01 survives only where heterogeneous task requirements actually change which shared cell, specialized backend or no-build decision is selected.

### C02

`DIRECT_KILLER_FOUND=true` for the **exactly-three complete-neighbourhood identity**, not for the completeness review tool.

The delta supplies multiple more mature ordinary-day, dignity, care, maintenance and one-line/three-node schemes, while no new authoritative geometry or service-hours evidence supports three independent complete catchments. The competition evidence therefore reinforces the same-base result: kill H3 as a candidate identity; retain the ordinary-day completeness contract as a review gate.

## What changed after the start-head audit

1. No official rule or geometry change requires rerunning the common base.
2. C01's originality boundary became narrower and more defensible: interfaces alone are not unique; the decision chain is the only remaining claim.
3. C02's collision level increased. Its H3 identity cannot be defended by wording or maturity of presentation.
4. No end-head proposal demonstrated the entire C01 kernel as a cross-task, shared-versus-dedicated physical allocation method. This is a bounded negative finding, not proof of global novelty.
