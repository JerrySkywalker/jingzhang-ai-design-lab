# Fieldwork

Potential future fieldwork should focus on verifiable, non-personal observations that can correct desktop assumptions.

Candidate observation classes:

- walking continuity and effective width;
- barrier-free discontinuities;
- crossings and curb conflicts;
- public-space interfaces and dwell points;
- visibility / wayfinding problems;
- station-to-neighborhood connectivity;
- service entrances and human fallback availability;
- physical constraints relevant to low-speed robotics.

Do not collect identifiable personal trajectories or portray participant observation as a professional survey.
