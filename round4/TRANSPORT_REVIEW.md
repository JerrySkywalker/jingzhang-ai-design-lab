## SPATIAL_CHANGES_REQUIRED

Every section must show the pedestrian/wheelchair clear route, cycle relationship, accessible arrival/crossing, ordinary loading/waste/maintenance/emergency route, controlled handoff, safe holding/manual recovery, queue/dwell/booking/refusal rule, staff welfare/late exit, and normal/event/weather/maintenance/failure states.

Additional binding changes:

1. Public routes must be physically independent of the technical layer.
2. All new curb allocation is temporary and demand-gated; no robot curb, reservation or public charging queue.
3. Existing ordinary service space is tested before specialist construction.
4. A cross-area servicing comparison must expose deadheading, street crossings, timing and failure isolation.
5. Worker arrival, changing/rest, cleaning/waste, supervision, incident closure and late exit must be spatial programme.
6. Quiet/night limits apply to loading, fans, energy, light and recovery.
7. Phase 0 repairs ordinary route/curb/access/refuge/maintenance; Phase 1 uses one existing compliant reversible bay; Phase 2 adds a second domain only after evidence; Phase 3 retains, converts or removes.
