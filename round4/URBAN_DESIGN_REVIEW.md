## Finding

C01 has a potentially real spatial consequence only in one narrow case. Zhongzhiyuan may need a deeper public-front / controlled-middle / segregated-back sequence with independent failure domains and manual recovery. AI Origin is presently an ordinary civic room with an optional removable interface. Dazhongsi is ordinary metropolitan arrival, loading and temporary event management expressed as a typology.

The candidate is admissible only if production makes this asymmetry binding:



## HARD_FAILURES

No unconditional candidate killer was confirmed at conceptual level, but K1 and K3 are under immediate pressure. The unchanged production concept would be rejected.

- AI Origin and Dazhongsi may not be counted as new C01 spatial types when deletion leaves ordinary routes, frontage, depth and operations intact.
- Loading, storage, maintenance, staff welfare, cleaning, washdown, service rooms, public help and curbs are ordinary infrastructure unless a task packet proves an added spatial requirement.
- Zhongzhiyuan's second cell and specialist backend may not become permanent before demand, compatibility, building and safety evidence.
- Any copied cells, permanent technical backends, dedicated AI curbs or ordinary rooms renamed as innovation trigger rejection.

## SPATIAL_CHANGES_REQUIRED

1. Classify every element as ordinary, task-derived, conditional, temporary/mobile or no-build.
2. Draw same-scale ordinary-baseline and admitted-state pairs for each section, explicitly marking route, frontage, depth, BOH, curb, refuge, landscape and phasing changes.
3. Delete any AI room where an ordinary civic, service, storage, loading, maintenance or staff room works.
4. Restrict the spatial demonstrator to Zhongzhiyuan unless later evidence proves a gap elsewhere.
5. Make all specialised, contaminated, high-energy and hazardous space conditional on building/fire/energy/ventilation/acoustic/logistics/waste evidence.
6. Keep the phase gate binding:

   ```text
   ordinary repair
   -> one reversible cell
   -> observed demand/compatibility
   -> second independent cell
   -> selective embedding or removal
   ```

7. Remove claims that maintenance, fallback, human help, reversibility, minimum sensing, handoff or sharing are distinctive outside the complete chain.

## Three sections

### Zhongzhiyuan

`CONDITIONAL_ADMIT_WITH_CHANGES`. It is the only plausible spatial demonstrator. Survey ordinary workshops/service courts first; prove why one ordinary bay cannot meet the task; keep the second cell/backend/new slab conditional; verify independent routes, egress, fire separation, wash/waste, noise and energy; make the drainage/canopy edge a prior boundary.

### AI Origin

`ADMIT_WITH_CHANGES_NO_BUILD_DEFAULT`. Ordinary civic, learning, human/paper/phone, accessible walking, shade and refuge are valuable but not C01-specific. Integrate accountability into the ordinary counter; require real evening/weekend and non-digital service; delete the cell unless a physical gap is proved; prevent technical queue, equipment, noise and night logistics at the quiet edge.

### Dazhongsi

`TYPOLOGICAL_ADMIT_WITH_CHANGES`. Do not claim a station quadrant, curb, parcel, catchment or building. Active frontage, help, arrival, loading, worker access, event staging and holding are ordinary. No permanent AI curb/staging; close temporary overlays first; return heavy work to an approved backend; wait for authoritative geometry and professional evidence before placement.

## EVIDENCE_MISSING

- authoritative geometry, statutory/public-private and heritage controls;
- verified walking/wheeling/cycling routes, crossings, gradients, curb and transit interfaces;
- building use/access/ownership, structure, fire, utilities, ventilation, acoustics and heat rejection;
- existing loading, maintenance, storage, wash, waste and staff-welfare capacity;
- observed tasks, concurrency, TTL, compatibility, low/peak/failure demand and ordinary-space insufficiency;
- soil, roots, canopy, drainage, flood, habitat, quiet/night-light, shade/refuge and maintenance capacity;
- weekday/evening/weekend public access, non-digital completion, worker welfare and authorised community/operator evidence.

## WHAT_IS_ORDINARY_URBAN_SPACE

Civic/help, staffed/paper/phone service, learning/meeting, commercial frontage, loading/service courts, facilities management, staff welfare, storage/lockers/wash, electrical/IT rooms, conventional delivery/recovery, event staging, sidewalks, shade, refuge, drainage, canopy and regulated clean/dirty BOH are ordinary space. They are not C01-specific merely because an AI task may use them.

## WHAT_IS_TRULY_C01_SPECIFIC

C01-specificity is a decision rule, not a building type: task state/error/TTL/privacy and a non-AI baseline derive a minimum bundle; four shareability classes select ordinary/no-build/dedicated/distributed provision; sharing requires separate failure domains, reset rules and manual recovery; low demand, peaks, failure and weather may reverse the build decision; retained modules must be removable or have ordinary conversion.
