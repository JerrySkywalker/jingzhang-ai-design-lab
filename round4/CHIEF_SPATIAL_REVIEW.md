## DECISIVE_REASON

C01 demonstrates a potentially useful allocation method, but not a presently admissible C01-specific urban-design layer.

This is not a 3:1 vote. The four reviews' underlying evidence agrees:

- Zhongzhiyuan is the only section that might acquire a material consequence.
- AI Origin defaults to an ordinary civic room with no cell.
- Dazhongsi remains an unresolved arrival/loading typology with at most a temporary overlay.
- Most named frontage, handoff, loading, storage, maintenance, welfare, wash, isolation, recovery and service functions are ordinary city or regulated building operations.
- The need for two cells, specialist backend and extra separation has not been derived from observed tasks, ordinary-space insufficiency, building conditions or two physically proven failure domains.

Reviews A–C treat this as curable. Review D identifies the controlling problem: those changes delete C01-specific claims from B/C, reclassify most of A as ordinary BOH, and condition every material increment on future evidence. This dissolves the candidate identity and leaves a future test protocol. At the final candidate gate before production, a possible future consequence is not a present urban-design consequence.
