## Seven scratch hypotheses

### H1 — Thick institutional thresholds

- **一句话城市问题** — `ASSUMPTION` — the inherited public corridor may pass beside campuses, enterprises and communities without producing enough genuinely shared ground between their secure interiors and the ordinary city.
- **一句话城市设计判断** — Make a variable-depth sequence of public-to-shared-to-controlled thresholds, while leaving secure institutional cores closed.
- **可画的 11.4 km² primary geometry** — A continuous ordinary public field with several unequal lateral depth-bays; each bay is drawn as a section from street/park through public forecourt and shared room to controlled interior, not as three stations.
- **三重点区为何不同** — Zhongzhiyuan needs a productive/service threshold; AI Origin a campus-community learning and transfer threshold; Dazhongsi a compact metropolitan arrival and market threshold.
- **AI 扮演什么角色** — AI changes the controlled test, interpretation and service layer behind the ordinary threshold; the public route, shade, toilets, help and access remain usable with AI off.
- **最大撞题** — `Jingzhang Common Eaves` (`cygaber/jingzhang-common-eaves`), `Jingzhang Public Foyer` (`tianmengchu/jingzhang-public-foyer`), `Jingzhang Intelligence Commons V6` (`youngkermit8-coder/jingzhang-intelligence-commons`), `Jingzhang Cross-Streets` (`jianshi-codes/jingzhang-cross-streets`) and `Ground Truth` (`XuejiFang/jingzhang-ground-truth`).
- **最大 kill reason** — K6: Common Eaves already resolves the same core with P0–P3 depth, a common forecourt, three unequal eaves, complete-city chapters and phased delivery; renaming bays or changing their count would be derivative.

### H2 — Enterprise space ladder

- **一句话城市问题** — `ASSUMPTION` — the official research–acceleration–market ambition may lack a spatial middle between a small research team, controlled prototyping, first public use and a firm needing durable urban premises.
- **一句话城市设计判断** — Organise adaptable building and block types as a non-linear space ladder so teams can change scale without losing access to ordinary streets, services and public life.
- **可画的 11.4 km² primary geometry** — Three interlocking fields of shallow shared rooms, adaptable courtyard/workshop blocks and metropolitan mixed-use buildings, connected by a distributed service-and-public-space mesh rather than a branded line.
- **三重点区为何不同** — AI Origin supplies small shared rooms and university transfer; Zhongzhiyuan supplies controlled deeper-floor prototyping and servicing; Dazhongsi supplies vertical mixed-use, customer-facing and capital/service premises.
- **AI 扮演什么角色** — AI materially changes secure compute/data requirements, dry/clean prototyping, model evaluation and the mix/timing of shared space; the buildings remain usable ordinary innovation, work and mixed-use fabric with AI off.
- **最大撞题** — `Origin Belt` (`scottscottie/ai-origin-ecosystem`), `Jing-Zhang Civic Foundry` (`kingyfleo/jingzhang-civic-foundry`), `Jing-Zhang First Mile` (`93171-sys/jingzhang-first-mile`), `Jingzhang Common Table` (`shawnshi/jingzhang-common-table`) and `Jing-Zhang Intelligence Commons V6` (`youngkermit8-coder/jingzhang-intelligence-commons`).
- **最大 kill reason** — P2/K6: an enterprise growth/space ladder transfers almost unchanged to another research district, while Origin Belt already owns 0→1/1→10/10→N and Civic Foundry more maturely joins street sections, production, public use and adopt-or-exit delivery.

### H3 — Compute-to-city municipal exchange

- **一句话城市问题** — `ASSUMPTION` — AI compute may add heat, power, water, cooling and maintenance loads without a spatial contract tying those loads to verified municipal capacity and public benefit.
- **一句话城市设计判断** — Reserve conditional municipal exchange interfaces where verified compute loads could connect to compatible energy, heat, water and maintenance systems without occupying ordinary public ground.
- **可画的 11.4 km² primary geometry** — A distributed set of utility catchments and conditional exchange yards linked to existing service corridors, with public-benefit receivers shown separately from compute producers; no continuous AI duct is presumed.
- **三重点区为何不同** — Zhongzhiyuan would test production/compute loads; AI Origin would favour small shared-compute and teaching interfaces; Dazhongsi would require compact building-integrated exchange and strict service/public separation.
- **AI 扮演什么角色** — AI creates the distinctive load, scheduling and resource-accounting problem; AI off leaves an ordinary municipal resilience and public-space system rather than stranded showcase infrastructure.
- **最大撞题** — `Compute Belt` (`hongshuxifan321/jingzhang-compute-belt`), `Jingzhang Thermal Belt` (`LING71671/jingzhang-thermal-belt`), `The Warm Return` (`ztkuaikuai/jingzhang-ai-belt`), `The Warm Line` (`zxw1992/jingzhang-warm-line`) and `Jingzhang Open Compute Commons` (`miociallo0721/jingzhang-open-compute-commons`).
- **最大 kill reason** — K6/K8: compute–heat–public-return territory is already occupied by several complete schemes, while no current official evidence proves a usable heat source, load, receiver, route, capacity, ownership or operator; a city organised around the unverified interface would be speculative infrastructure.

### H4 — Time-sharing city

- **一句话城市问题** — `ASSUMPTION` — specialised research, learning and commercial space may sit unused in some hours while care, evening learning, cultural activity and night workers lack suitable places in others.
- **一句话城市设计判断** — Treat opening hours, handovers and recoverable shared use as a building-and-public-realm design variable before adding floor area.
- **可画的 11.4 km² primary geometry** — A field of day/evening/night building carriers with separate public, staff, service and quiet routes, plus small all-hours refuges rather than one event spine.
- **三重点区为何不同** — Zhongzhiyuan separates late technical shifts from public edges; AI Origin pairs daytime research with evening co-learning; Dazhongsi manages day/night commerce, arrival and resident quiet.
- **AI 扮演什么角色** — AI supports bounded scheduling, demand forecasting and handover records, while fixed timetables, staffed access and physical keys keep the city usable offline.
- **最大撞题** — `Jingzhang Chrono Commons` (`FA555/jingzhang-chrono-commons`), `Whose Hours?` (`Mr-Metatron/whose-hours-jingzhang`), `Jing-Zhang Human Hours` (`RootReturn0/jingzhang-human-hours`), `Jingzhang 168` (`budoyh/jingzhang-168`) and `All Shifts` (`marx-zhang/jingzhang-all-shifts`).
- **最大 kill reason** — P2/K6: the same time-sharing logic applies to almost any innovation district and is already expressed as several complete 24-hour/shift-based proposals.

### H5 — The serviced city

- **一句话城市问题** — `ASSUMPTION` — the visible innovation district may externalise deliveries, waste, repair, cleaning, shift change, worker welfare and emergency access into unsafe or landscape-damaging residual space.
- **一句话城市设计判断** — Draw the ordinary service city first: separated service courts and routes, worker entries/refuges, waste/repair space and event recovery must precede public spectacle.
- **可画的 11.4 km² primary geometry** — A distributed rear-and-side service network with hardened courts, cross-block service passages and small worker commons, explicitly separated from the public/blue-green hierarchy.
- **三重点区为何不同** — Zhongzhiyuan needs production and hazardous/service separation; AI Origin needs fine-grain campus/community deliveries and shared facilities; Dazhongsi needs compact timed loading, waste, late exit and event recovery.
- **AI 扮演什么角色** — AI changes some equipment maintenance, secure deliveries and operating schedules, but does not replace staff welfare, conventional logistics or emergency access.
- **最大撞题** — `All Shifts` (`marx-zhang/jingzhang-all-shifts`), `Night Shift` (`Edmounds/jingzhang-night-shift`), `Second Shift` (`mofada/jingzhang-second-shift`), `On Duty Jing-Zhang` (`007xf/jingzhang-on-duty`) and `Jingzhang Common Table` (`shawnshi/jingzhang-common-table`).
- **最大 kill reason** — P2/K6: this is an essential complete-city subsystem, but its total identity is portable and current shift/duty/production proposals already foreground the same hidden labour and operating problem.

### H6 — Fine-grain renewal field

- **一句话城市问题** — `ASSUMPTION` — a long innovation designation may erase unequal existing fabrics by replacing them with a generic park, superblock or landmark sequence.
- **一句话城市设计判断** — Retain, open, subdivide, infill or replace only after classifying each existing fabric and its public/service relationships; renewal should be a field of different block actions, not one corridor module.
- **可画的 11.4 km² primary geometry** — A patchwork of retained compounds, opened edges, subdivided superblocks, infill courts and metropolitan mixed-use blocks overlaid by a public-space and servicing hierarchy.
- **三重点区为何不同** — Zhongzhiyuan prioritises productive compounds and landscape; AI Origin fine-grain campus/community interfaces and reuse; Dazhongsi compact station-city blocks and vertical mixed use.
- **AI 扮演什么角色** — AI changes selected building programmes and helps compare renewal options, but professional/public decisions determine urban form and AI-off use remains complete.
- **最大撞题** — `Jing-Zhang Intelligence Commons V6` (`youngkermit8-coder/jingzhang-intelligence-commons`), `Fine Jing-Zhang` (`RanchoGoose/capillary-jingzhang`), `Jing-Zhang City Version` (`Sh7ne/jingzhang-index`), `Jing-Zhang Urban Renewal Yard` (`JAVA-LW/jingzhang-reassembly-yard`) and `Jingzhang Cross-Streets` (`jianshi-codes/jingzhang-cross-streets`).
- **最大 kill reason** — K4/K6: the strongest version is conventional form-first renewal with AI as an optional analysis layer, while current form-first and block/street proposals already occupy the spatial territory more concretely.

### H7 — Level-access reliability

- **一句话城市问题** — `ASSUMPTION` — a linear public-space promise can fail at each road, grade, gate, lift, toilet or sheltered waiting point, making the corridor nominally continuous but unusable for many people.
- **一句话城市设计判断** — Build a redundant, step-free route-and-refuge network whose weakest transition, not its landmark, determines admission.
- **可画的 11.4 km² primary geometry** — Two or more redundant accessible paths with level-change nodes, staffed alternatives, refuge rooms and failure detours mapped as a network rather than a single green line.
- **三重点区为何不同** — Zhongzhiyuan tests long garden-district distances and service crossings; AI Origin campus/community gates and shared facilities; Dazhongsi compact metropolitan grade, crowd and interchange conditions.
- **AI 扮演什么角色** — AI may predict outages and provide routing, but physical redundancy, signs and human help remain primary and work offline.
- **最大撞题** — `Jingzhang Level Access Line` (`zymk8353/jingzhang-level-access-line`), `Passage Before Barriers` (`zyaoii/jingzhang-passage-first`), `No-App Jingzhang` (`MaxwellYe0815/no-app-jingzhang`) and `The Slow Line` (`Restless-One/jingzhang-slow-line`).
- **最大 kill reason** — P3/K7: the proposition can strongly correct mobility and public realm but cannot naturally generate the full innovation ecosystem, land-use, buildings, blue-green, culture and implementation concept without bolted-on chapters.

### Deep read: H2 enterprise space ladder

`Origin Belt` already makes enterprise maturation itself the master structure: 0→1 at AI Origin, 1→10 validation at Zhongzhiyuan and 10→N clustering at Dazhongsi, with capital, IP, compute, scenario services, adaptable space and three-scale delivery. `Civic Foundry` is still closer to the desirable urban-design version: it begins with an existing public-space baseline, draws three urban fabrics and five street sections, converts public commissions into prototypes and use, and gates adoption or exit. H2 could add a more explicit building-size ladder, but that is a refinement of occupied territory, not a new mother premise.

### Deep read: H3 compute-to-city municipal exchange

`The Warm Return`, `The Warm Line`, `Jingzhang Thermal Belt` and `Compute Belt` already connect compute loads, green power, heat recovery and public receivers to the Jing-Zhang narrative. `Jingzhang Open Compute Commons` further gives the interfaces resource, maintenance, ordinary-service and exit contracts. H3's conditional exchange yards improve evidence discipline but do not create an unoccupied core. More importantly, current official material supplies no site heat/load/capacity/receiver evidence, so the premise would either invent an engineering basis or remain too conditional to organise a complete city.
