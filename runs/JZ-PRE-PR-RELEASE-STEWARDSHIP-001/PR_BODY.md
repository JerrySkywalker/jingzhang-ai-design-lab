## 京张续城 / Jing-Zhang In Place

This submission proposes an **in-place heterogeneous renewal field** for the
Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt. Its controlling spatial mechanism is
`STATUS → ACTION`: observed evidence states determine which retain, repair,
adapt, pause, or no-build action is permissible for each patch. It avoids a
mandatory single-spine narrative and assigns three unequal key areas distinct
roles in one evidence-governed renewal portfolio.

### Included package

- Complete Chinese and English proposal, report, static visual pages, five
  paired core figures, A3 booklets, and A0 boards.
- Professional design evidence for the three scales, land use, buildings,
  mobility, blue-green/public space, portfolios, phasing, metrics, sources,
  standards, and design-depth matrices.
- An ordinary-city baseline (`AI_OFF_CITY`) and three bounded AI task packets;
  AI does not replace authority, and NO-BUILD findings remain explicit.
- Provisional geometry is prominently disclosed: no polygon is asserted to be
  an official boundary, redline, statutory area, approval, or implementation.
