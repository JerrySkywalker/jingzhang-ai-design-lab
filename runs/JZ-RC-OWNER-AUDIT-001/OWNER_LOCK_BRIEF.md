# Owner Lock Brief — 京张续城 / Jing-Zhang In Place

Read time: under 10 minutes
Recommendation: `LOCK_AFTER_MAJOR_FIX`

## 1. Why C05 should be final winner

C05 has a durable urban-design judgment rather than a technology slogan: Jing-Zhang is an already functioning, heterogeneous city, so evidence status must select a conditional action before any intervention. This single rule coherently governs land use, access, buildings, public realm, mobility, service edges, phasing, AI tasks, stop conditions, and reuse. It also survives the absence of official polygons because its honesty is procedural, not cosmetic.

No fundamental contradiction was found. The three areas remain materially different, the ordinary city remains complete with AI off, and replacement/new build is never the default. No newly merged peer is a direct near duplicate.

## 2. Strongest remaining weakness

The intelligence is mostly in prose, registers, and structured evidence. The human-facing package does not yet convert that intelligence into an equally persuasive spatial image.

## 3. Strongest visual weakness

The figures and boards show abstract boxes, lines, and number cards instead of recognizable site structure, patch distribution, urban sections, or lived spatial sequences. A3/A0 layouts repeat those figures with excessive blank space and small board-distance content.

## 4. Strongest design weakness

The exact spatial consequence of the 17 patches is hard to see. A juror cannot quickly locate where OPEN_EDGE, SUBDIVIDE_RECONNECT, service separation, blue-green maintenance, or public/controlled thresholds produce different urban form.

## 5. Strongest AI weakness

AI restraint is excellent, but AI is almost too invisible as design. The package needs a legible spatial comparison showing the ordinary baseline, the bounded specialist state, human responsibility, and the exit/reuse condition for the three deep task packets.

## 6. Strongest competition risk

Several current peers also use evidence gates, rollback, human fallback, reversible prototypes, or retain-first logic. Without stronger spatial visualization, jurors may group C05 with this broader governance cluster and miss its distinctive no-spine STATUS × ACTION field.

This is a differentiation risk, not a direct-duplicate blocker.

## 7. Display-name lock

Lock `京张续城 / Jing-Zhang In Place`.

The name is concise, bilingual, and precisely matches the retain-first/in-place thesis. Do not rename it during the presentation pass.
