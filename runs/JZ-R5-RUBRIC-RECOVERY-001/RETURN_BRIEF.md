## 11.4 km² spatial structure

The controlling diagram is a no-spine **`STATUS × ACTION PATCHWORK`**.

Each urban patch is first labelled `BUILT/OPERATING`, `APPROVED/IN DELIVERY`, `CONTROLLED/LIMITED ACCESS`, `SURVEY-ONLY UNKNOWN`, or `PROVEN ADAPTABLE CANDIDATE`. Only then may it receive `RETAIN`, `REPAIR`, `OPEN EDGE`, `SUBDIVIDE/RECONNECT`, or `ADAPT/INFILL`. Replacement remains behind a separate evidence, consent and statutory gate.

Four unequal connection types overlay the field: ordinary public streets, grade-separated crossings, controlled institutional thresholds, and transit/water/heritage interfaces. Public space and servicing have separate hierarchies. The drawing must never collapse back into one rail spine, three renamed cores or a generic retain/open/infill legend.

## AI is neither a label nor the city protagonist

AI-off passes: the heritage/park, public routes, mixed-use renewal, blue-green systems and ordinary rooms remain a good city with physical signs, staff, keys, phone and paper fallbacks.

AI-matters passes conditionally through three bounded task packets:

1. physical model/equipment validation can require a reversible separated test envelope and servicing/safety state;
2. secure collaborative project work can temporarily divide an ordinary learning/work room into public and controlled zones;
3. adoption/compliance work can require a staffed demonstration/assurance side beside ordinary professional service.

Broad classes of tasks explicitly return `NO-BUILD`. If the formal design reduces these differences to tenant branding, dashboards or option scoring, Candidate 05 fails.
