# R5 Blockers and Non-Blockers

## Global blockers

None at admission.

Only a safety, authorization or repository-integrity issue may block the whole run.

## Known non-blockers

- exact official polygons and some professional surveys remain unavailable;
- individual public sources or APIs may fail;
- a premise or dummy validation step may fail;
- the current official proposal corpus may advance during the run.

Each is recorded locally and the independent lanes continue. Unknown official geometry must not be fabricated and, under the official rubric, does not itself reduce a content score.
