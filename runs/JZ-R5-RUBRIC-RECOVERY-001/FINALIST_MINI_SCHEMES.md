# Finalist A/B Comparable Mini-schemes

Status: `PROXY_JURY_INPUT`  
Naming: working descriptors only; neither scheme is a candidate or Owner winner.

Both schemes use the same evidence boundary: exact polygons, parcel controls, ownership, building condition, operator consent, capacity and engineering are unknown. Every physical action is a conceptual/typological suggestion gated by survey and statutory review.

## Finalist A — H6 Fine-grain renewal field

### First principle

Jing-Zhang is not an empty innovation line. It contains delivered and in-progress public works, controlled institutional edges, operating transit gateways, active innovation carriers, unequal fabrics and different heritage/water relationships. Urban design should make those unequal pieces work as one city without erasing their differences or redrawing projects already under way.

### 43.6 km²

Create an asset-and-interface framework rather than an enterprise arrow diagram. Six relationship classes are mapped with source/status labels:

1. university/research controlled edges;
2. current innovation carriers and public programmes;
3. enterprise/professional/capital interfaces;
4. regional and metro gateways;
5. heritage, park and water projects;
6. community, youth/talent and ordinary-life fabric.

Each relationship receives an interface action, evidence ceiling and responsible-role type. The two wings supply ordinary services and public/problem contexts without presumed partners. Compute/data are service dependencies, not assumed facilities. Cross-institution relationships occur through public-side rooms, scheduled events and verified agreements, never fictional open campuses.

### 11.4 km² primary spatial diagram

The controlling drawing is a `STATUS × ACTION PATCHWORK`, not a spine:

- status: `BUILT/OPERATING`, `APPROVED/IN DELIVERY`, `CONTROLLED ACCESS`, `SURVEY-ONLY UNKNOWN`, `VERIFIED ADAPTABLE`;
- action: `RETAIN`, `REPAIR`, `OPEN EDGE`, `SUBDIVIDE/RECONNECT`, `ADAPT/INFILL`; replacement remains behind a separate proof/statutory gate;
- connections: ordinary public street, grade-separated crossing, controlled institutional threshold, transit/water/heritage interface;
- public hierarchy: regional river/park routes, area forecourts/courts and doorstep spaces;
- servicing: separately audited from public/blue-green movement.

No status implies an action automatically. The design value is the combination of an evidence state, fabric relation and unequal section.

### Three key areas

#### Zhongzhiyuan — water–compound–regional-arrival mosaic

- position/structure: Qinghe gateway, Five-Ring relationship, compounds and water/green interfaces form several linked fields rather than a ceremonial entrance;
- land/buildings: retain first; only surveyed adaptable deep/large shells receive controlled validation fit-outs; no assumed factory/lab vacancy;
- public realm: river/park interfaces, public observation and worker/visitor amenities remain outside secure test space;
- mobility/service: regional arrival and last-kilometre routes are distinguished from equipment/service access;
- AI: bounded model/robot/equipment validation can require separation, safety, servicing and reversible building fit-outs;
- ordinary life: food, rest, toilets, shade/rain refuge and public water/park access work with AI off;
- implementation/risk: coordinate announced water projects and heritage; audit structure, fire, power, title, flows and current works before a carrier is selected.

#### AI Origin — public-side campus threshold field

- position/structure: delivered park, current/future transit, controlled gates, street-side carriers and neighbourhoods form fine-grain public-side patches;
- land/buildings: small courts and selectively adaptable street/park-side floors support transfer, learning, project rooms and community/talent services; no blanket wall removal;
- public realm: permanent city paths stay outside gates and connect to already approved public-space nodes;
- mobility/service: Wudaokou works now; Qinghua East Road West receives an adaptable pre/post-interchange public/cycle threshold;
- AI: controlled data/project rooms sit beside ordinary learning/community rooms; secure work changes access, acoustic, power/data and booking states but not the public route;
- ordinary life: residents can use learning, care, rest and community rooms without enrollment or AI;
- implementation/risk: current opening, building and tenure rights require field/institutional agreements; do not claim a new innovation centre.

#### Dazhongsi — grade-separated station-city renewal field

- position/structure: operating 12×13 interchange, four quadrants, underpass/bridge conditions, heritage context and mixed urban fronts generate several unequal sections;
- land/buildings: prioritise accessible ground-floor/public interfaces and small adaptive reuse; enterprise/customer/professional-service rooms enter only verified carriers;
- public realm: connect station exits, bicycle interfaces, ordinary streets and small courts without treating the provisional rectangle as a parcel plan;
- mobility/service: distinguish interchange movement, local access, service/loading and grade-separated crossings;
- AI: staffed compliance/adoption rooms and bounded urban-use demonstrations alter selected programme/operating states, while ordinary station-city routes are unaffected when AI is off;
- ordinary life: step-free route, toilets/rest, food/care, quiet residential interfaces and non-digital help are admission conditions;
- implementation/risk: align with the documented renewal unit; never transfer its area-level condition labels to individual buildings.

### Industry ecosystem

The scheme spatialises a plural existing-city ecosystem: secure verification in eligible north carriers; small transfer/learning/project interfaces in the middle; customer, professional and capital-facing premises in verified southern carriers; public services, youth/talent life, mobility, water/park and culture are shared foundations. It does not assert partners, firm migration or a sequential north-to-south enterprise conveyor.

### Land and building renewal

A survey matrix records current use, structure, fire, heritage, access, tenant/business impact, title/consent, service capacity and adaptability. Until verified, each building is only `candidate retain`, `candidate adapt` or `survey required`. Infill repairs a demonstrated spatial/programme gap. Replacement follows legal planning and public procedures and is never the default.

### Mobility

Three different threshold systems: Qinghe regional intermodal/last kilometre; Wudaokou plus present/future Qinghua East public-side campus access; Dazhongsi four-quadrant interchange and grade separation. The permanent network never relies on campus interiors. Exact crossing/exit designs wait for official geometry and professional traffic/accessibility work.

### Blue-green and public realm

Preserve delivered park assets, dovetail announced Qinghe/Xiaoyue works, retain flood and maintenance functions, and connect regional routes to courts/doorsteps. Soil, root zone, canopy, shade/refuge, drainage, noise/light, seasonal use and maintenance are gates, not branding.

### AI role and double test

AI is selective and spatially material:

1. north validation changes safe separation, service access, fit-out and operating envelopes in a verified carrier;
2. middle secure project/data work changes the controlled half of otherwise ordinary learning/work/community rooms;
3. south adoption/compliance/urban-use programmes change selected room mix, staffing and timetables, not the base interchange.

AI-assisted comparison may track sources, confidence and option consequences, but the professional/public decision maker assigns the renewal action. Task-to-Space can return `NO NEW AI SPACE REQUIRED`.

`AI_OFF_CITY=PASS`: a coherent heritage/park, campus-edge, transit, mixed-use and blue-green renewal city remains.  
`AI_MATTERS=PROVISIONAL_PASS`: the controlled validation, secure project interfaces and selected fit-outs/operating states disappear or materially change. If jury treats these as optional tenants rather than a planning mechanism, H6 fails promotion.

### Ordinary-day city

Every patch tests anonymous passage, affordability/continuity for existing users, food/care, toilets/rest, shade/rain refuge, staffed/manual entry, evening/weekend use, facility-down and digital-down conditions. Failures change access, room mix, route or phase—not only a risk paragraph.

### Implementation

- G0: reconcile built/approved projects; field, access, tenant/business and building surveys;
- G1: no-regret interfaces and three consented/reversible 1:1 pilots;
- G2: selected adaptive reuse after independent fire, heritage, accessibility and service review;
- G3: infill only against measured programme/public-space need;
- replacement: separate statutory and public-interest decision.

A project-interface register prevents duplication of park, water, station and public-space works.

### Culture/brand direction

Working directions only: descriptive `The Existing City, Renewed`; cultural `京张续城`; international `Jing-Zhang In Place`. Railway heritage is evidence of successive working layers, not an AI-track metaphor. Name remains unlocked.

### Nearest competitors and kill argument

Closest: `Jingzhang Intelligence Commons V6`, `Capillary Jingzhang`, `Cross-Streets`, `City Version`, `Reassembly Yard`. Differentiation depends on a no-spine status–action mosaic and explicit current-project interfaces. Strongest kill: it may collapse into conventional retain/open/infill urban renewal with AI as optional analysis or optional tenancy.

## Finalist B — H2 Enterprise space ladder

### First principle

Jing-Zhang's research-to-market ambition needs a portfolio of urban premises, not a north-to-south corporate conveyor. Teams should be able to change size, control, equipment and public exposure without being expelled from ordinary streets and daily services.

### 43.6 km²

Map a distributed premises portfolio across research/university supply, prototyping/enterprise use, capital/professional services, talent housing/daily services, compute/data access and real-use demand. The regional role is a district where teams can move among room, bay, court, block and stack types while keeping public-city access. No actual firm movement, partnership, quantity or public compute resource is asserted.

### 11.4 km² primary spatial diagram

Three interlocking fabric fields, not a branded line:

- north: deeper garden-production blocks and service courts;
- middle: fine-grain transfer/learning rooms and courts;
- south: metropolitan stacked mixed-use buildings;
- cross systems: a public/slow mesh, distinct servicing mesh and continuous blue-green ordinary ground;
- variable: size, depth, control/public exposure and fit-out, not `0→1→10→N` arrows.

Every field contains more than one ladder rung; its weighting and urban fabric differ.

### Three key areas

- Zhongzhiyuan: adaptable deeper-floor prototype/validation courts, separated service edges, public observation and worker amenity, only after carrier surveys.
- AI Origin: small-team rooms, shared project/test rooms, transfer courts and ordinary community/talent support outside controlled campuses.
- Dazhongsi: typological stacked customer/capital/professional-service/urban-use premises with active ground and compact servicing; not a station-precise plan.

### Industry ecosystem

The premise explicitly covers the spatial support for research transfer, controlled validation, enterprise growth, professional/capital/customer interfaces, talent/daily life, compute/data and public problem contexts. The portfolio is non-linear: a team may remain small, move sideways, split or return to ordinary space.

### Land and building renewal

Survey first; retain useful shells; use reversible fit-out and subdivide/merge only with structure/fire/access/title evidence; infill after a documented gap. No vacancy, rent, lease or enterprise demand is treated as fact. Affordable workspace/public obligations need future operator and policy confirmation.

### Mobility, blue-green and public realm

Connect premises to the same three unequal transit thresholds and ordinary walking/cycling network. Booked/timed servicing does not replace emergency access. Shade, drainage, public refuge and habitat/maintenance are independent common gates.

### AI role and double test

AI changes secure compute/data rooms, model evaluation, equipment interfaces and clean/dry or public/controlled separation only where a task and carrier justify it. Bounded scheduling may improve shared use, with manual allocation and offline access.

`AI_OFF_CITY=PASS`: adaptable mixed innovation fabric remains useful.  
`AI_MATTERS=PROVISIONAL_PASS`: some controlled premises, equipment/service states and operating envelopes change. Ordinary rooms yield `NO-BUILD` where sufficient.

### Ordinary-day city

Food, care, toilets, affordable work/community rooms, rest, housing/talent support, worker access and AI-off/manual operation are integrated into every field rather than a separate complete-neighbourhood brand.

### Implementation

- G0: building, entrance, tenure, service and demand survey;
- G1: one reversible retrofit in each fabric;
- G2: shared fit-out/service standards and a portfolio-operator proposal;
- G3: expand only on measured occupancy, transition, affordability and service outcomes.

### Culture/brand direction

Working direction: `room to grow / growth without displacement`. Naming is deferred because `growth` language intensifies collision.

### Nearest competitors and kill argument

Closest: `Jingzhang Growth Mesh`, `Origin Belt`, `Civic Foundry`, `First Mile`, `Intelligence Commons`, `New Track`. The different primary diagram is the physical, measured type/floorplate/tenure/service matrix; if the story becomes an enterprise journey, validation–incubation–first-order chain, or one stage per area, it is derivative. Strongest kill: Growth Mesh now integrates much of H2's adaptable space, three roles, AI scenarios and delivery more completely, making H2 a sharper subsystem rather than a clearly superior total proposal.

3. Does H2's physical typology diagram remain a complete proposition after Growth Mesh, or is it dominated?
4. Which finalist better converts verified site evidence into spatial design without fabricated parcel facts?
5. Is either finalist significantly superior enough to justify Candidate 05, rather than merely clearing `70/100`?
