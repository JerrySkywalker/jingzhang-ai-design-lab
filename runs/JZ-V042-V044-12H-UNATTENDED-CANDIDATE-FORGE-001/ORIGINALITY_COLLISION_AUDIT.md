## 1. Risk of Competitor Convergence

- **Generic "AI District / Smart Corridor"**: Over-relying on sensor meshes, digital twins, and autonomous vehicle loops without physical site friction.

- **"KPI-Heavy Planning Decks"**: Accumulating ungrounded metrics without explaining who verifies them or what happens when systems fail.
