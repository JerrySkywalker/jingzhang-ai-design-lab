# Current official rubric

Source: `open-city-ai/haidian` `upstream/main` at admission. This packet omits all historical-score comparisons.

| Dimension | Weight | Review focus |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `brief_alignment` | 20 | Brief, three scopes, key areas, and task coverage. |
| `originality` | 10 | Distinctive concepts, mechanisms, and scenarios. |
| `ai_planning_innovation` | 15 | Necessary AI contribution to spatial planning. |
| `implementation_feasibility` | 20 | Phasing, actors, metrics, and verifiable boundaries. |
| `public_interest_inclusion` | 10 | Residents, access, equity, and ordinary-city service. |
| `risk_compliance` | 10 | Data, rights, privacy, and policy boundaries. |
| `expression_completeness` | 15 | Coherent narrative, drawings, visual evidence, and deliverables. |

Scale: 0 absent or invalid; 1 seriously deficient; 2 weak; 3 adequate; 4 strong; 5 exceptional. Calibrate dimensions independently, cite visible evidence, and do not multiply-punish a single defect. Missing organizer-owned geometry is a gap, not a score reduction by itself.
