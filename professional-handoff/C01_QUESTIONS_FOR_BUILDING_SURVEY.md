# Questions for Building Survey

1. Which existing ground floors plausibly have service access, floor loading, ventilation, acoustic separation and fire separation suitable for adaptation?
2. Where are loading, maintenance, storage, energy, cleaning and staff welfare already provided?
3. Which proposed AI-specific rooms can be absorbed into ordinary facilities management space?
4. What evidence is required before reusing a room for battery/energy, washdown, quarantine or safe recovery?
5. Can public accountability counters be reached independently of controlled operational areas?
6. Are back-of-house routes compatible with accessible public entrances and existing residential/learning uses?
7. What reversible fit-out would prevent a low-adoption future from leaving stranded technical space?
8. What building condition or code issue would immediately kill each section?

The current materials contain no building-condition survey, measured drawings, structural assessment, utility capacity or code compliance conclusion.
