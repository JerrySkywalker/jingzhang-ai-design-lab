# Minimum Data Request List

## Tier 1 — controls the next admission decision

1. Official overall and three key-area polygons, coordinate reference system, version and release authority.
2. Public pedestrian/cycling network with legal crossings, barriers, accessible entrances and major grade/vertical-access conditions.
3. Existing public-service inventory with verified type, opening hours, public eligibility and non-digital access.
4. Building ground-floor use/access, loading/service entrances and basic reuse-condition survey for the three section study areas.
5. Transit entrance/transfer and curb/loading operating information sufficient for typological—not capacity—review.

## Tier 2 — controls professional feasibility

- statutory planning controls and verified land/building status;
- utility capacity and constraints relevant to energy, washdown, ventilation and edge compute;
- drainage/flood, soil, canopy, habitat and protected landscape constraints;
- fire/life-safety, battery/energy, acoustic and hazardous-isolation requirements;
- freight/service timing and facilities-management arrangements;
- heritage inventory, protection scope and reuse constraints.

## Tier 3 — controls later calibration

- aggregated, privacy-preserving service demand by time/state;
- event and disruption operations;
- maintenance/failure logs where legally publishable;
- validated innovation ecosystem relationships and facility access conditions;
- community priorities obtained through an authorized process.

## Acceptance rules

For every dataset record source, date, licence/permission, spatial/temporal coverage, responsible publisher, uncertainty and permitted use. Do not substitute commercial map screenshots, inferred ownership, scraped personal information or undisclosed operational data.
