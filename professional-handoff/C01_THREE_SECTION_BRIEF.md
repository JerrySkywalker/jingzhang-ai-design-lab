# C01 Three-Section Brief

## Review instruction

Review the three sections as different urban interfaces, not three branded stations. Reject the concept if the same service module can be copied unchanged into all three.

## A — Zhongzhiyuan productive interface

Question: can public R&D/testing coexist with controlled handoff, repair, technical logistics and a protected landscape/drainage edge?

The section separates public frontage and ordinary walking from controlled transfer, two isolatable service cells, maintenance/storage/energy and a specialist backend. The intended AI-specific consequence is not a display pavilion; it is safe separation and selective sharing among heterogeneous tasks. Test whether existing/adapted buildings and conventional loading space can carry most of this program.

## B — AI Origin civic/learning interface

Question: can optional AI service enter daily civic and learning life without equipment and logistics occupying the foreground?

The section puts the staffed civic room, accessible walking, shade/refuge and quiet learning/residential edge first. It permits only a light local service cell; heavy maintenance, storage and recovery are remote. Test whether even that cell is needed, and whether evening/weekend staffing and non-digital completion are credible.

## C — Dazhongsi metropolitan-adoption typology

Question: can temporary embodied service and enterprise exchange coexist with arrival, public help, curb logistics, events and an AI-off state?

The section uses short dwell, a bookable curb/service edge, a small recovery room and human help. It is deliberately typological because the canonical geometry is unresolved. Reject any parcel, station-corner or exact catchment interpretation until official geometry and transport evidence arrive.

## Common stress states

For each section, mark the exact spatial change under:

- AI off;
- 80% lower robot adoption;
- coincident peak demand;
- one shared cell unavailable;
- rain/heat;
- event state;
- scheduled maintenance.

An answer that changes only operating text, not routes, frontage, back-of-house, refuge, storage, curb or phasing, does not pass spatial admission.
