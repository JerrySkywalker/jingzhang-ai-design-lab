# Questions for Urban Design

1. Do the three sections create genuinely different frontage, depth, access and public-space relationships?
2. Which proposed rooms are conventional mixed-use/service program, and which—if any—justify a distinct planning or design type?
3. Can the productive backend be inserted through adaptive reuse without producing a sealed technical campus?
4. Does the AI Origin interface preserve an ordinary civic foreground at night, weekends and during digital failure?
5. Does the metropolitan typology manage arrival and event peaks without making the public realm a queue or logistics apron?
6. What minimum plan/section dimensions must be tested before spatial admission can be called professionally credible?
7. Which heritage relationships materially shape alignment, reuse, thresholds or phasing, rather than graphics and naming?
8. What is the strongest urban-design reason to reject C01 even if the technical model is internally consistent?

Return any recommended plan/section revisions as relationships and required dimensions to verify, not invented site measurements.
