# Bounded Field-Observation Protocol

Status: `PROTOCOL_ONLY` — not executed during Round 3.

## Purpose

Collect the minimum non-personal observations needed to test route continuity, ground-floor/service interfaces, ordinary public use and the H2/H3/H4+/Corridor+N hypotheses. This is structured observation, not formal survey, traffic count, accessibility certification or community consultation.

## Before the visit

1. Print the common-base warning map and a blank observation sheet; do not treat provisional boundaries as field redlines.
2. Define public routes and observation windows in advance, including weekday/evening or weekend contrast where lawful and safe.
3. Do not enter controlled property, obstruct routes, contact minors, or record private interiors.

## Observation fields

| Topic | Record | Do not infer |
|---|---|---|
| route continuity | visible public connection, barrier, grade/steps, crossing, detour, surface, refuge | legal easement, universal accessibility certification |
| ground-floor interface | active/blank frontage, public entrance, staffed counter, visible service door | tenancy, ownership, internal use |
| service edge | loading bay/curb activity, conflict point, approximate observation time | formal curb designation or demand rate |
| shade/refuge | shade source, seating/shelter, rain protection, public availability at observation | seasonal performance from one visit |
| ordinary public use | anonymous activity categories and whether space supports passage/stay/help | identity, intent, demographics or individual trajectories |
| service hours | posted public hours and timestamped observation | actual reliability beyond the observed period |
| maintenance | visible repair/cleaning/storage evidence and public obstruction | responsible entity or maintenance quality history |
| transit threshold | entrance context, transfer barrier, waiting/refuge and public-help interface | ridership, catchment or operational capacity |

## Privacy and conduct limits

- no facial recognition or biometric processing;
- no individual tracking or repeated-person linkage;
- no collection of names, contact details, licence plates or device identifiers;
- no covert or unauthorized interview/audio recording;
- no photographing people as the subject of observation;
- no claim of formal surveying, measured accessibility compliance or representative community input;
- no publication of sensitive access, security or private operational details.

## Evidence form

Each record should contain a public-location description, date/time window, weather, observation category, short factual note, optional non-identifying diagram/photo, uncertainty and which decision it affects. Separate `OBSERVED` from `INTERPRETATION`.

## Stop conditions

Stop if access is restricted, observation would compromise privacy/safety, staff or residents object, or the task would require measurement, interviewing or data permissions beyond this protocol.
