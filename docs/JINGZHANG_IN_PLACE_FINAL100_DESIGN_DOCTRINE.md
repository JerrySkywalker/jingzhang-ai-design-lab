## 2. Owner-approved humanistic core

### Project name

**京张续城 / Jing-Zhang In Place**

### Public slogan

**让未来生长，让城市相续。**

### Humanistic thesis

**技术属于时代，城市属于世代。**

### City philosophy

**城市不是技术的容器，而是代际生活的连续体。**

The proposal must no longer present itself first as an AI compliance/audit system. It is an urban-design proposition about how a city can continue across technological generations while still accepting technological change.

The key value judgment is:

> **不是每一次技术进步，都值得城市付出永久的空间代价。**

The existing AI admission machinery remains valid, but becomes the professional mechanism beneath this larger city philosophy.

## 3. Three continuities

### 续时 — continuity through technological cycles

Models and devices change in months or years; streets, trees, buildings, public space and communities persist for decades or generations. The city must not be rebuilt around the lifespan of one model generation.

### 续生 — continuity of ordinary life

Ordinary life must not depend on participation in AI. A resident without a smartphone, refusing algorithmic mediation, requiring human help or accessibility support must still possess the city completely. Human-facing language should explain `AI_OFF_CITY` as:

> **生活不以技术参与为前提。**

### 续忆 — continuity of urban memory

Urban renewal is not a reset to zero. Jing-Zhang railway heritage, Zhongguancun innovation history and contemporary AI are successive technological layers entering one city. New technology should become another layer in urban history, not erase prior urban life.

## 4. Three temporal layers

### 长久城市 / Enduring City

Time scale: decades to a century.

- streets and ordinary paths
- Jing-Zhang heritage
- public space and green space
- accessibility
- everyday commerce
- community relationships
- human service

This layer never depends on AI.

### 可适应城市 / Adaptable City

Time scale: years to decades.

- convertible rooms
- modular elements
- plug-in interfaces
- adjustable boundaries
- reusable public facilities

This layer accepts change without becoming model-specific.

### 暂居智能 / Temporary Intelligence

Time scale: months to years.

- AI devices
- bounded test surfaces
- temporary interfaces
- model services
- data/service adapters

It must be removable, stoppable, replaceable and resettable. The implicit relationship is:

> **AI 是城市的住客，不是城市的主人。**

## 5. Preserve the professional grammar

`heterogeneous existing city -> STATUS × ACTION -> ORDINARY_SPACE_SUFFICIENT -> AI spatial admission -> 12→3→9 -> minimum reversible spatial delta -> human authority -> STOP/RESET -> ordinary city`

Also freeze:

- nine NO-BUILD / ordinary-carrier results
- G0–G4
- 15 renewal project families

The final revision must not obtain points by adding scenarios, personas, project counts, regional promises, invented partnerships, fake site precision or more audit tables.

## 6. Reframe 12→3 for human jurors

The machine mechanism remains the ordinary-space sufficiency test. The human-facing explanation is:

- if software can solve it, do not build a new space;
- if human service can solve it, do not force an intelligent interface;
- if a reversible element is enough, do not make a permanent intervention;
- if evidence is insufficient, leave the future decision open.

Thus `12→3` is a planning ethic, not a scoring algorithm.

## 7. Three key areas as a city narrative

Internal IDs remain S01/S04/S07, but the first human-facing names are:

### 众智园 — 验证

**让新技术先学会克制。**

A bounded physical task must prove that ordinary space is insufficient before it receives a reversible spatial difference.

### AI 原点 — 共处

**让人与新技术共同学习。**

Technology enters public daily life where people can understand, question, adjust and refuse it. S04 remains a light NTS typology rather than a second P0.

### 大钟寺 — 裁决

**让技术最终接受城市生活的判断。**

A staffed review/appeal relationship decides whether a service continues, changes, pauses or leaves. S07 remains a light NTS typology rather than a third P0.

Human narrative chain: **验证 → 共处 → 裁决**.

## 8. One S01 P0 only

S01 is the sole participant-authored P0 reference candidate because it has the strongest irreducibly physical need, inspectable human stop/reset edge, reusable component logic and professional handoff value. S04 can collapse to an ordinary room plus portable tools; S07 can collapse to an ordinary staffed desk. Keeping them light strengthens rather than weakens `ORDINARY_SPACE_SUFFICIENT`.

### P0 purpose

Test whether one supervised physical AI equipment/model-observation session can operate inside a removable fit-out while the ordinary route and ordinary training/work use remain intact.

Success does **not** prove site suitability, product safety/performance, public deployment, legal compliance, approved occupancy, budget, scalability or implementation approval.

### Reference envelope

Authoritative form is parametric:

`E = R_existing + T_verified + B_professional + H_authority + O_observer + M_reset`

A `12 m × 9 m` zero-coordinate rectangle may be used only as a `DESIGN_ASSUMPTION` for participant layout, component quantities and jury communication. It is not surveyed, approved, official or field-verified.

Task footprint, safety/setback, egress, accessibility clearance, height, floor load, anchorage and power stay null until professional input.

### Spatial zones

Professional IDs may remain Z0–Z6, but drawings should visually organize them through the three temporal layers.

- Z0 ordinary clear route — untouched; no queue/storage/test intrusion
- Z1 arrival/handoff
- Z2 removable test zone
- Z3 human authority/operator
- Z4 booked observer zone
- Z5 maintenance/reset access
- Z6 variable professional safety/setback buffer with no invented code clearance

### Capacity and roles

Participant operational assumption only:

- one active task/device
- five session role positions
- four booked observer positions
- design cap nine
- no walk-in queue in the ordinary route
- `field_observed_capacity=null`
- `approved_capacity=null`

Five role classes:

1. test operator
2. separate human safety authority
3. public/service handoff
4. maintenance/reset role
5. independent observer/reviewer

They are roles per session, not five FTEs, named organizations or employment commitments.

### Components

Use a bounded removable/reusable component schedule covering:

- STATUS/reset frame and controlled entry
- removable control perimeter
- reusable floor/interface protection
- power/isolation interface
- physical stop/control kit
- operator/safety console and physical log
- handoff/observer elements
- reset/storage inventory

Each component needs function, states, installation dependency, inspection point and fail-closed stop/removal consequence.

### Operation and physical reset

`pre-open -> ordinary-baseline verification -> install/inspect -> conditional activation + stop rehearsal -> one controlled task -> fail-closed trigger -> human stop/isolation/takeover -> reverse removal -> surface/route restoration -> independent ordinary-baseline verification`

Never claim a reset duration. Damage, missing components or uncertain isolation means `QUARANTINED` and P0 remains stopped.

### Alternatives

Keep real alternatives visible:

1. NO-BUILD: simulation/code review/ordinary rooms/manual review only
2. lower-tech reversible: markings/manual cordon/human observation
3. selected removable P0
4. alternate linear side-bay arrangement

Alternatives 1 or 2 win unless a verified physical task defeats lower interventions while preserving the ordinary route.

### ROM/OPEX

Use only source-bounded sensitivity:

`CAPEX_ROM = Σ(quantity × dated public unit-rate low/base/high) + installation/inspection allowance + explicit uncertainty reserve`

`OPEX_ROM = session role-hours + setup/reset labor + inspection/certification + storage/transport + utilities/consumables + replacement reserve`

If credible rates are unavailable, publish a relative sensitivity index instead of fabricating CNY totals.

Always expose:

- `market_quotation_count=0`
- `approved_budget=null`
- `funding_commitment=null`

### External HOLD gates

All remain `HOLD_EXTERNAL`; `P0_GO=false` until every applicable gate closes:

- site rights/access
- professional design acceptance
- structure/load/anchorage
- fire/life safety/egress
- power/isolation/equipment interface
- accessibility/ordinary-route continuity
- privacy/data/legal
- operations/competence/emergency/reset
- insurance
- procurement/vendor scope
- manufacturer requirements
