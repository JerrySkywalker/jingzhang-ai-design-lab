# Round-3 Red Team

Date: 2026-08-12

The six prosecutors use only Round-3 evidence, canonical official constraints and the bounded competition audit. They do not assume that future fieldwork, design skill or official data will rescue a candidate.

## 1. URBAN_DESIGN_PROSECUTOR

### C01 attack

The three sections are different diagrams, but not yet a 11.4 km² spatial structure or professionally credible urban sections. They have no verified block depth, cross-section dimension, building condition, access right or statutory control. Much of the program—loading, storage, cleaning, maintenance, staff room and help counter—is ordinary urban back-of-house. Calling the selection logic “AI-native” does not itself create urban form. If an urban designer can absorb every proposed room into conventional mixed-use service space without changing frontage, route, massing or phasing, C01's spatial admission collapses.

**Strongest kill test:** a professional plan/section study finds no recurrent spatial consequence beyond normal service/loading provision.

**Evidence-limited response:** the model already deletes a universal station, chooses no-build/dedicated provision in most stress profiles and produces three unequal interfaces. That is enough for conceptual admission, not professional admission.

### C02 attack

The candidate mistook three officially named focus projects for three complete urban catchments. The shared base cannot establish service completeness, hours or route permeability; the same evidence fits H2, H4+ and Corridor+N. Rebranding Corridor+N as “three neighbourhoods” would be a new hypothesis hidden under the old name.

**Verdict:** exactly-three identity remains killed.

## 2. TRANSPORT_PROSECUTOR

### C01 attack

Distributed cells and specialized backends may reduce duplicated equipment while increasing deadheading, crossings, curb dwell and recovery movements. The synthetic model has no street network, travel time, curb capacity, freight window or emergency-clearance calibration. “Bookable service edge” could simply privatize scarce curb. A cell failure may shift load into pedestrian space or across a barrier the diagram does not represent.

**Strongest kill test:** required movement and recovery cannot be accommodated without degrading accessible walking, cycling, transit access or ordinary servicing.

**Current evidence:** C01 specifies prohibited pedestrian-priority functions and separate routes, but transport feasibility is untested.

### C02 attack

No network catchment or observed crossing audit supports H3. POI dots cannot establish a daily-life unit across institutional edges, rail infrastructure or major roads.

**Verdict:** H3 not supported; Corridor+N remains a question, not a replacement candidate.

## 3. PUBLIC_REALM_PROSECUTOR

### C01 attack

Visible accountability can turn into screen/display clutter; controlled handoff can become a queue; event staging and technical logistics can consume the same shaded, accessible ground needed by ordinary users. Energy, washdown, night lighting, noise and security may harden the park edge. The diagrams say ordinary path/refuge first, but no observation shows those spaces exist or can carry the added footprint.

**Strongest kill test:** under heat/rain/event or AI-off conditions, technical space reduces shade, refuge, soil/drainage continuity or free public access.

**Current evidence:** Living Systems and ordinary-day gates forced drainage, shade, refuge, staff and route changes; this is a credible design constraint, not proof of site success.

### C02 attack

C02 is stronger on ordinary public life, but the strength is generic and heavily occupied by Civic Support Line, City Completeness, Everyday Jing-Zhang and MEND. It offers no evidence that three complete units are the best public-realm structure.

**Verdict:** keep the review contract, not the candidate identity.

## 4. AI_SYSTEMS_PROSECUTOR

### C01 attack

The expanded model is intentionally synthetic and small. Its costs, compatibility classes and demands are authored assumptions. Dedicated provision wins five of seven profiles; distributed sharing wins only staggered and weekday patterns. A skeptic can conclude that “shared substrate” is the exception and should never organize an entire urban-design concept. The supposedly unique kernel may be an engineering procurement rule whose correct spatial result is often “use ordinary space” or “do not build.”

**Strongest kill test:** realistic task/resource inputs repeatedly select dedicated or no-build provision, leaving no stable spatial layer across the three areas.

**Current evidence:** this does not kill C01 yet, because a bounded rule that prevents overbuilding is itself useful and it materially differentiates the three sections. It does kill the former universal-station concept.

### C02 attack

Removing AI leaves a reasonable complete-city agenda, but the candidate did not then prove why it remains a world-class AI innovation belt. Federation was institutional prose, not an AI production/testing/adoption spatial system.

**Verdict:** insufficient AI/industry distinctiveness reinforces kill.

## 5. COMPETITION_PROSECUTOR

### C01 attack

Nearly every ingredient is occupied: Agent/OS, robotics, sensing, digital twin, fallback, human takeover, reversible pilot, charging, maintenance, repair, public interfaces, validation tracks, service cells and shared components. Common Table now demonstrates concrete isolation, cleaning, tooling and logistics interfaces in one domain. C01's residual distinction is a conjunction of familiar elements, not a guaranteed original urban proposition.

**Strongest kill test:** a high-quality peer demonstrates the complete task-to-minimum-resource, four-way sharing, isolated allocation and physical-section chain—or professional review finds that the conjunction does not create distinct urban form.

**Current evidence:** the start and end delta audits found no proposal completing that exact chain. This is a bounded repository finding, not global novelty proof.

### C02 attack

The territory is directly occupied by complete-neighbourhood, everyday, civic-support, care/repair and exact-three-unit proposals, several more mature in spatial/evidence terms. Naming differences do not protect it.

**Verdict:** collision is independently sufficient to reject C02 unless H3 has exceptional site evidence; it does not.

## 6. IMPLEMENTATION_PROSECUTOR

### C01 attack

Safe isolation, energy, cleaning, operator supervision and specialist recovery introduce code, insurance, responsibility, staffing and lifecycle burdens. A shared cell also creates correlated failure and governance concentration. No operator, utility capacity, building survey or cost calibration exists. Starting with permanent infrastructure would create stranded technical space in the low-adoption future.

**Strongest kill test:** the smallest reversible pilot cannot reuse ordinary space and demonstrate public value without permanent construction or unresolved safety/operations dependency.

**Current evidence:** the correct phase-1 action is task audit plus temporary/leased cells and existing-space adaptation. Any proposal for a permanent corridor network before demand verification contradicts Round-3 evidence.

### C02 attack

Completeness depends on operating hours, eligibility, staffing, maintenance and redundancy—not merely nearby POIs. None is established, and obtaining it for three alleged units requires field/official/community evidence. There is no implementation case for privileging H3.

**Verdict:** kill exactly-three; retain service-chain audit for any later concept.

## Red-team synthesis

```text
C01_KILL_PRESSURE=HIGH_BUT_NOT_TERMINAL
C02_EXACTLY_THREE_KILL_PRESSURE=TERMINAL
```

C01 survives only as a city-first proposal whose technical subsystem is conditional, distributed and often absent. It does **not** survive as a universal station network, robotics corridor or claim that its individual components are novel. Its next gate is professional/field spatial admission.

C02 does not survive as an independent candidate. Its ordinary-day completeness contract and Corridor+N hypothesis are retained as review tools. This is not permission to rename the killed candidate.
