# Round-2 Red Team

## Rules

Four roles were simulated sequentially after independent read-only collision reviews. Prosecutors use the strongest non-strawman case. Defenders may cite only evidence already in this branch; a future survey, partner, standard or drawing is not a rebuttal.

## C01_PROSECUTOR

> 即使我是 Candidate 01 作者，我认为最强的淘汰理由是：去掉技术词后，它还没有证明自己生成了不同的城市。

1. Fifteen close proposals occupy agents, city OS, spines, public testbeds, commons, interoperability, maintenance, charging, rollback, minimum data and human takeover. Open PR #1805 approaches even the residual shared-physical kernel.
2. Counterfactual A fails: remove shared physical infrastructure and the inherited heritage spine, cross-links, mixed functions and three areas remain legible.
3. The prototype is synthetic. Its compatibility, demand timing, penalties and failure cells are chosen for logic testing; they provide no evidence of real cross-vendor interfaces, spatial demand, maintenance labour, safety or cost.
4. Shared capacity wins only under staggered peaks in the synthetic model. Coincident peaks make dedicated provision lower-index; pooling creates correlated failure and routing/changeover burden.
5. The technical kernel is generic. A service-cell system could sit in many innovation districts; current Jing-Zhang specificity is an unproved combination of the prescribed corridor and three prescribed areas.
6. A permanent physical network risks sealed soil, occupied curb, lost shade, equipment ruin and a technology-led public realm before demand or controls exist.

**Prosecution request:** `KILL`, unless the jury accepts a major rewrite with a short real-interface/spatial proof gate before full design.

## C01_DEFENDER

The defense cannot claim site performance, but it can show a surviving falsifiable proposition:

1. The collision audit does not find an implemented or evidenced task-to-minimum-physical-resource system with explicit non-shareability, capacity, failure cells and urban consequences. It finds severe partial occupation.
2. The prototype did not rig a universal sharing win: it returns B for staggered demand and A for coincident peaks, segregates human-contact/hazardous resources and pauses automated delivery on compute failure. This is evidence of falsifiability and design discipline.
3. Round 2 materially changes the scheme: no independent robot land use; existing/hardened ground floors first; front/middle/back building sections; managed service edges; multiple isolated cells; ordinary-city conversion at 80% low demand.
4. The three areas are no longer station copies: controlled technical back end, civic requirements/accountability front end and operational adoption edge.
5. The Owner can make a core contribution to requirement inversion, compatibility, degraded modes and resource allocation without pretending to be an urban designer.

**Defense limit:** none of these proves site fit. The defensible disposition is no stronger than `ADVANCE_WITH_MAJOR_REWRITE`, conditional on professional spatial translation and real interface/demand evidence.

## C02_PROSECUTOR

> 即使我是 Candidate 02 作者，我认为最强的淘汰理由是：“三邻”不是场地发现，而是把任务书的三个重点区改了名字。

1. Official sources require three different detailed-design objects but provide no three-catchment population, facility, hours, capacity, travel or operating evidence.
2. Provisional geometry has material park/Dazhongsi warnings, so current catchment drawing would be false precision.
3. Twelve close proposals occupy belong/stay, complete districts, three living yards/civic rooms/courts, campus-city, ordinary-day services and local units. Some are more mature and one proposes nine cross-line units.
4. Genericity fails: civic centre + short web + mixed use + local completeness applies to any three AI parks.
5. AI/system distinctiveness fails: the good city survives AI removal, but no specific research-test-production-adoption system then distinguishes it from ordinary complete-community planning.
6. Federation is unproved. The synthetic contract shows that exporting ordinary services to a shared remote hub fails local completeness; arrows do not establish access, hours, capacity or fallback.
7. A professional team could spend substantial effort illustrating three circles that later evidence forces into H2, H4+ or corridor-plus-N.

**Prosecution request:** `KILL` the exactly-three premise now; do not fund full professional design before the bounded hypothesis test.

## C02_DEFENDER

The defense has a narrower case:

1. Three areas are not invented: official sources give different sizes, locations and tasks. Treating them as unequal strategic projects is grounded.
2. Ordinary-day completeness directly addresses a weakness in technology-led schemes and already produces distinct stress briefs for workday enclave, campus access and event/commercial/TOD pressure.
3. The contract is explicit and reproducible: eight roles, seven states, local/external separation and no averaging away failure. It catches both a workday enclave and a federation-dependent unit.
4. C02 has high visual and professional-design potential because daily chains, ground floors, routes, refuge, worker space and events can become drawings.
5. The concept can abandon exactly three as a predetermined catchment count while retaining an evidence-gated polycentric federation around three official strategic projects.

**Defense limit:** the last move is a major identity rewrite. It defends `corridor + three projects + N units`, not the current “three neighbourhoods” thesis. Existing evidence supports at most `HOLD` for a short comparative test.
