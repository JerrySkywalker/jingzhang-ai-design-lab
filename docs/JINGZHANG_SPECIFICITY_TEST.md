# Jing-Zhang Specificity Test — Round 3

## Method

Move each candidate to “any Chinese AI district.” Identify what survives unchanged, what depends on Jing-Zhang’s heritage/public structure, Zhongguancun innovation culture and the three unequal official projects, and what remains blocked by site evidence. Mentioning railway names, drawing a line or using railway vocabulary is not site specificity.

## Candidate 01

### What transfers unchanged

- public-task requirement inversion;
- minimum sensing from uncertainty/TTL/privacy;
- four shareability classes;
- dedicated/shared/no-build comparison;
- failure cells, degraded modes and human recovery;
- ordinary-day and Living Systems gates.

These are portable methods and cannot by themselves make a Jing-Zhang concept.

### What is currently Jing-Zhang-derived

1. **Public continuity before technical circulation.** The railway heritage/public-space line becomes a protected north–south ordinary-city and living-systems structure; embodied logistics is deliberately excluded from it.
2. **Three unequal interface sections.** Official roles plus corridor conditions lead to Zhongzhiyuan productive backend, AI Origin civic/learning front and Dazhongsi metropolitan adoption typology—not three stations.
3. **Railway culture as maintenance responsibility.** Heritage changes the visible accountability/maintenance/recovery logic and the phased “repair ordinary ground before adding service” method, not just logo/name.
4. **Zhongguancun culture as knowledge translation.** Open-source contributions, task packets, public failure evidence and research-to-city translation change the public ground-floor programme.
5. **Geometry uncertainty changes design.** Dazhongsi is kept typological rather than falsely site-resolved; the heritage-park mismatch prevents the provisional polygon from dominating the plan.

### What remains generic or weak

- the exact public spine and transverse seams are not professionally drawn from authoritative geometry;
- actual buildings, station thresholds, campus/community edges, service routes and blue-green conditions are unverified;
- the name “Re-Embodied” remains technical and portable;
- the kernel affects a bounded layer, not the entire urban form.

### Judgment

```text
C01_SITE_SPECIFICITY=PARTLY_PORTABLE
```

C01 is no longer generic at the conceptual-structure level, but cannot be `SITE_SPECIFIC` until official/field/professional evidence determines routes, sections, reuse and living-systems relationships.
