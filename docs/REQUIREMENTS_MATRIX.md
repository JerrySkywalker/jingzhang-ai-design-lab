# Official Requirements Matrix — Working Snapshot

**Snapshot date:** 2026-08-11

**Canonical source:** `open-city-ai/haidian` latest `main`. This file is a working interpretation only; the official repository remains authoritative and must be re-read before material design decisions and before formal submission.

## A. Three spatial scales

| Level | Approx. scope | Required design role |
|---|---:|---|
| Coordinated research area | 43.6 km² | AI innovation ecosystem, industry/future-city research, regional collaboration, three areas/two wings, cultural and strategic framework |
| Overall design area | 11.4 km² | Complete urban renewal + urban design at regulatory-plan-oriented depth, without fabricating statutory certainty |
| Key detailed-design areas | 368.4 ha total | Detailed urban-design subplans for Zhongzhiyuan, Beijing AI Origin Community, and Dazhongsi |

Current exact official polygons are not assumed available in this lab. Provisional geometry may support concept development but cannot become an official redline or approval basis.

## B. Complete urban-design coverage

Every serious candidate must eventually demonstrate a coherent response to all of the following:

- existing-condition diagnosis and data gaps;
- three-level scope framework;
- overall spatial structure;
- innovation ecosystem and industrial/function layout;
- land-use strategy;
- building massing/character and renewal logic;
- retain / renovate / demolish / new-build logic at a conceptual level when official conditions are absent;
- roads, rail integration, walkability, cycling and parking strategy;
- municipal and new infrastructure strategy;
- blue-green system and public-space network;
- detailed design for all three key areas;
- renewal project list and phasing;
- metrics, recalculation path, assumptions and risks.

## C. Six Agent tasks

1. Overall belt concept, naming, visual identity/Logo direction, three positions, five functions, three-areas/two-wings collaboration and overall spatial concept.
2. Full-stack AI innovation system and world-class AI ecosystem, including 5–8 relevant global cases and mechanisms for land, space, capital, talent, compute, data and scenarios.
3. AI+ scenarios and AI-active-city design: at least 10 scenario cards, at least 3 industrial test/validation scenarios, at least 5 user personas, each tied to space, operations, data, privacy, human review and risk.
4. AI public space, intelligent-native businesses and at least 3 AI pilgrimage/recognition landmarks, plus public-space component logic.
5. Integrated Jing-Zhang railway heritage, Zhongguancun innovation culture and new AI culture narrative, wayfinding/signage and international communication.
6. Global AI innovation activity system and long-term operations: annual event architecture, developer community, scenario opening, public experience, international communication and conversion pathways.

## D. Required formal artifact families later

The final official submission is expected to include, among other files:

- bilingual `proposal.md` and translation;
- structured source, assumption, metric, compliance, standards and design-depth files;
- GeoJSON for site/key areas/land use/buildings/roads/green/public space/constraints/phasing;
- core figures;
- A3 booklet and A0 boards in both languages where required;
- offline HTML proposal and visual experience;
- self-check / manifest evidence.

These do **not** belong in this lab merely as template copies. They are generated later in the official fork.

## E. Prohibited certainty

Without authoritative data, do not state as final facts:

- approved FAR, building height or development intensity;
- exact parcel demolition decisions;
- road redlines, rail alignments, bridge/tunnel engineering;
- municipal capacity or underground-space feasibility;
- ownership, financing, approval or guaranteed implementation conclusions.

The correct response is complete design **plus explicit uncertainty and a recalculation/deepening path**.

## F. Evaluation lens

Candidate reviews in this lab should consider at least:

- goal fit;
- functional fit to three positions / five functions / three areas + two wings;
- brand identity;
- regional collaboration;
- planning innovation;
- industry support;
- tangible AI scenarios;
- spatial specificity;
- transferability to professional teams;
- expression completeness;
- public/legal/licensing compliance;
- international communication;
- long-term operational value.
