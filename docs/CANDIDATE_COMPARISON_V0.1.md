## 5. Anti-convergence checks

### Test A — remove AI

- **Candidate 01:** loses part of its core re-embodiment and adaptive-infrastructure proposition; remaining heritage/public-realm design must still work, but the concept is materially changed.
- **Candidate 02:** remains a federation of complete neighbourhoods. This is intended.
- **Candidate 03:** remains a living-system mosaic. This is required.

**Result:** different AI dependencies.

### Test B — remove the railway spine as sole organiser

- **Candidate 01:** core structure is substantially weakened.
- **Candidate 02:** three local catchments remain primary; the park is a shared common.
- **Candidate 03:** the mosaic remains non-linear, while losing an important heritage seed corridor.

**Result:** different spatial dependencies.

### Test C — swap the three key-area designs

- Candidate 01's engine roles follow a research/test/service metabolism.
- Candidate 02's neighbourhood types follow different ordinary-day and economic contexts.
- Candidate 03's matrices follow different productive, learning and metropolitan living-system conflicts.

None should be swappable without breaking its logic. This remains a design hypothesis until authoritative geometry and field evidence.

### Test D — identify the first project

- **01:** a bounded re-embodiment interface/station and its operational evidence.
- **02:** ordinary-day facility/access repair and one accountable neighbourhood civic interface.
- **03:** protect/repair verified living assets and public comfort before any AI procurement.

**Result:** different implementation priorities.

### Test E — identify the emblematic section

- **01:** inherited rail/fabric + public interface + sensing/service/test layer.
- **02:** mixed neighbourhood block + civic centre + local daily-life web.
- **03:** building edge + soil/water/canopy + accessible route/refuge + maintenance access.

**Result:** different architectural sections.

### Test F — Owner technical advantage

- **01:** can influence the central competitive proposition.
- **02:** can influence federation reliability but should not define visual/spatial identity.
- **03:** is mostly supporting; domain professionals lead living-system design.

**Result:** the new candidates were not reverse-engineered around the Owner's résumé.

### Test G — convergence failure triggers

- Kill Candidate 02 if it becomes Candidate 01's three cores with more amenities.
- Kill Candidate 03 if it becomes Candidate 01's spine with more planting.
- Kill Candidate 03 if it becomes Candidate 02's three neighbourhoods with identical green commons.
- Re-run the generation step if any two candidates share the same primary diagram, first project and emblematic section.

## Anti-convergence conclusion

**ANTI_CONVERGENCE_PASS = true at architecture-exploration v0.1.**

The three candidates have different first questions, organising geometries, public-space ontologies, renewal sequences, AI dependencies and Owner contribution patterns. This conclusion does not imply competition distinctiveness: Candidates 02 and 03 each have high external collision risk.

## 7. Questions for the chief architect

The most consequential disagreements to examine are:

1. **Primary geometry:** should a continuous north–south spine, three polycentric neighbourhoods or a non-linear living-system mosaic lead the 11.4 km² plan?
2. **Heritage role:** is the railway an operating backbone, a shared common between places or a seed corridor inside a broader system?
3. **AI visibility:** should AI be constitutive and visible, quiet/optional in ordinary institutions, or deliberately subordinate and removable?
4. **Innovation versus everyday/ecological completeness:** which condition must be secured first, and what trade-off is acceptable?
5. **Competition risk versus Owner fit:** is it better to carry a technically distinctive high-fit concept, a familiar urban-life concept, or a lower-fit ecological concept in crowded fields?
