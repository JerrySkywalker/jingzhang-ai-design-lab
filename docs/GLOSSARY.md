# Glossary

## Complete urban design
A proposal that coherently covers strategy, spatial structure, land use, buildings/renewal, mobility, infrastructure, landscape/public space, key areas, AI scenarios, culture, phasing and operations at the levels required by the brief.

## Candidate concept
A complete-design hypothesis under evaluation. Detail does not equal selection.

## Re-embodiment / 再具身
In Candidate 01, a four-part idea: heritage infrastructure receives new civic functions; existing urban fabric is reconfigured rather than simply replaced; AI acquires physical/public-service embodiments; governance becomes an observe–adapt–reconfigure loop.

## Embodied-AI infrastructure
Shared physical/digital systems that allow robots and other physical AI services to operate, recharge, maintain, exchange tools/modules, receive tasks and fall back safely.

## Re-Embodiment Station / 再具身站
A candidate infrastructure/landmark type for service, charging, maintenance, module/tool exchange, staging and human-supervised recovery of heterogeneous embodied agents. It is a concept, not an approved facility type.

## Minimum-sufficient sensing / Know-Enough
A systems-engineering principle: derive the minimum information needed to meet a defined public-service/safety requirement instead of maximizing sensing by default.

## Robust operation
Designing services for normal, degraded and failed states, including explicit triggers for pause, human takeover and recovery.

## Provisional geometry
Geometry permitted for temporary concept generation/visualization when official boundaries are missing; it cannot be described as an official redline or precise statutory basis.

## Horizontal completeness / vertical depth
The T-shaped strategy: cover the whole city-design brief horizontally while developing distinctive technical depth vertically.
