## Why C01 is not `ADVANCE`

The conceptual spatial gate is necessary but insufficient. The diagrams do not prove building reuse, route/curb feasibility, landscape compatibility, code/safety, demand or operation. The city-first rewrite also needs professional synthesis so the system kernel remains a conditional subsystem rather than the visual organizer of the whole city.

## Why C02 is not `HOLD`

Round 3 was the promised one-shot same-evidence test. H3 required positive evidence because the candidate's identity says **exactly three**. The shared base could not distinguish three daily-life catchments, while direct competitors already occupy the language and method. Holding the identity would protect it from its kill criterion. The ordinary-day gate is explicitly preserved, so killing C02 does not discard its strongest contribution.
