# ADR-0003: Round-3 Owner Downselect and Round-4 Admission

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision date:** 2026-08-12
- **Authority:** Human Owner instruction, Round-3 convergence
- **Scope:** design-lab candidate direction; not a final submission selection
- **Evidence head:** `c0b4cda9ba59e1d465a0c48517b9fd5603f6a784`

## Context

Round 3 placed C01 and C02 on one contextual spatial evidence base. C01 passed a conceptual urban-program spatial-admission test but did not receive professional/site admission. C02's one-shot unit-hypothesis test did not support exactly three complete neighbourhoods; its ordinary-day completeness contract remained useful across candidates. Candidate 03 had already stopped as a standalone direction while its Living Systems Gate remained useful.

The Owner accepts these falsification results and authorizes convergence before Round 4. This decision narrows the design budget; it does not declare a competition winner or authorize a formal fork.

## Candidate consequences

### Candidate 01

C01 is the only surviving provisional candidate and enters Round 4 as `ACTIVE / PROFESSIONAL ADMISSION`. Its conceptual spatial admission does not substitute for professional urban-design, transport, landscape or building-interface judgment. Round 4 may still kill C01.

“Re-Embodied Jingzhang / 再具身京张” is not locked as the total public brand. Re-embodiment remains, for now, a technical subsystem describing task-requirement inversion, bounded shared physical support, isolated allocation and degraded/recovery operation. The total city concept and public-facing name must follow the city design rather than force it.

### Candidate 02

C02 is killed as an independent candidate. Its `exactly three complete neighbourhoods` identity may not be silently restored by renaming Corridor+N or by treating the three official focus areas as three proven catchments.

The `Ordinary-Day Completeness Gate` becomes a candidate-neutral review method. It must continue to test different users, time states, non-digital completion, failures and external service dependencies in C01 and any future candidate.

### Candidate 03

C03 remains killed as a standalone candidate. Its original files remain design memory. The `Living Systems Gate` becomes a candidate-neutral review lens for soil, water, drainage, canopy, shade, seasonal comfort, habitat, refuge, maintenance, recovery and technology footprint.

### Candidate 04

Candidate 04 is not triggered. It is recommended only if C01 fails the next professional spatial-admission gate. Round 4 must not generate Candidate 04 pre-emptively or use it to avoid a clear judgment on C01.

## Formal-geometry correction

```text
OFFICIAL_GEOMETRY_MISSING
!= ABSOLUTE_FORK_BLOCKER
!= ABSOLUTE_FORMAL_SUBMISSION_BLOCKER
```

The official intake contract permits provisional geometry when its status and precision limits are disclosed. If authoritative geometry arrives later, all dependent geometry, metrics, figures and claims must be recomputed and rechecked. Missing authoritative polygons therefore create precision, recalculation and design-risk obligations; they do not by themselves prohibit a formal fork or formal intake.

The current `NOT_READY_TO_FORK` decision is controlled by two gates:

1. `OWNER_FINAL_DIRECTION` — the Owner has not locked C01 as the submission direction or final public concept;
2. `C01_SPATIAL_ADMISSION` — C01 has not passed Round-4 professional spatial admission.

## Consequences

- Round-4 effort concentrates on a falsifiable professional spatial-admission package for C01.
- C02 and C03 receive no further independent candidate-development budget.
- Their review gates must remain active and may force spatial changes or kill C01.
- C01 is not a final winner, and no formal fork or submission is authorized by this ADR.
- R2/R3 evidence and killed-candidate files remain in history; this convergence does not rewrite or squash them.

## Supersession condition

Only a later explicit Owner decision may: pass or kill C01 after professional spatial admission, trigger Candidate 04, lock the total concept/brand, or authorize a formal fork.
