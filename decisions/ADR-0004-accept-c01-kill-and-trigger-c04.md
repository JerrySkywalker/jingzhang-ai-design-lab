# ADR-0004: Accept C01 Kill and Authorize Candidate 04

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision date:** 2026-08-12
- **Authority:** Human Owner instruction, Round-4 convergence
- **Scope:** design-lab candidate direction; not a final submission selection
- **Evidence head:** `58544299434d2a81bfaf6e4986ac8610596a3916`

## Context

Round 4 returned a proxy-panel recommendation to reject C01 at the professional spatial-admission gate. The controlling finding was not that its task-to-space chain lacked analytical value. It was that the current candidate did not demonstrate a C01-specific urban-design layer: deleting the proposed layer left two key areas spatially unchanged and left the remaining area as ordinary workshop, servicing and back-of-house space unless future evidence proved otherwise. The changes needed to address K1 and K3 would dissolve the standalone candidate rather than revise it for production.

The Owner accepts that recommendation. This decision closes C01 as a standalone candidate, retires its total-design identity and authorizes a separate one-shot Candidate-04 generation Goal. It does not generate Candidate 04, select a winner or authorize a formal fork.

## Retired candidate identities

- C01 — Re-Embodied Jingzhang / 再具身京张;
- C02 — Three Neighbourhoods Jingzhang / 京张三邻;
- C03 — Habitat Mosaic / 京张生境拼图 as a standalone candidate.

C01 may not be revived by renaming it, deleting two cells while retaining its candidate claim, or repackaging ordinary civic, servicing or back-of-house provision as a distinctive spatial layer. A future evidence-backed bounded pilot may be tested under a fresh authorization, but cannot retroactively pass or restore C01.

## Reusable candidate-neutral methods

- `Task-to-Space Requirement Method` — test whether a public task, tolerated error, TTL, privacy ceiling, non-AI baseline and ordinary-space sufficiency produce a minimum resource bundle and a bounded shared, dedicated or no-build spatial consequence;
- `Ordinary-Day Completeness Gate` — test people, daily chains, time states, non-digital completion, failure and external dependencies;
- `Living Systems Gate` — test soil, water, drainage, canopy, shade, seasonal comfort, habitat, refuge, maintenance, recovery and technology footprint;
- evidence labels and falsification discipline.

These methods may review C04. They do not determine C04's concept, name, spatial structure, key-area roles or technical spine. Their survival does not make C04 a continuation or repair of C01, C02 or C03.

## Candidate-04 authorization boundary

The next Goal is `JZ-C04-ONE-SHOT-001`. It must begin from the complete urban-design problem and generate one constrained candidate in one shot. This convergence records the authorization only; no Candidate-04 concept, draft or formal-submission material is created here.

## Evidence and uncertainty

The Owner decision is authoritative for candidate status. The Round-4 proxy evidence remains bounded by provisional geometry, open contextual data, unscaled sections and non-licensed review; it does not prove that a future task-to-space pilot is impossible. That limitation does not reopen C01 or reduce the force of the Owner decision.

## Consequences

- There is no surviving or final candidate after this convergence.
- C04 generation is authorized only for the next one-shot constrained Goal.
- No formal fork, submission scaffold or official-repository mutation is authorized.
- Round-2, Round-3 and Round-4 branches, evidence and rejected-candidate files remain in history without rebase, squash or rewrite.
