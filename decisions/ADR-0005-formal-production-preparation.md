## Binding production conditions

### B1 — Status and action must become spatial evidence

`STATUS × ACTION` must control actual patches, interfaces, sections, evidence states, triggers, actors, stop/go tests and exit/reuse paths. A generic list of retain/repair/open/adapt verbs is insufficient.

### B2 — Three key areas remain genuinely unequal

Zhongzhiyuan's water–compound–regional-arrival mosaic, AI Origin's public-side campus/park/transit threshold field and Dazhongsi's grade-separated station-city renewal field must keep different fabrics, sections, constraints and delivery logic. They may not become one module or innovation lifecycle repeated three times.

### B3 — AI planning remains spatially material

AI planning innovation must remain at or above the R5 specialist floor. Bounded validation, secure project interfaces and adoption/assurance operations must visibly change selected spatial configurations, service envelopes or operating states while the AI-off city remains complete.

### B4 — No-build findings remain valid

Task-to-Space is a reviewer. Where ordinary space is sufficient, the formal package must state `NO-BUILD` and may not manufacture permanent AI-specific buildings to raise an AI score.

### B5 — Provisional geometry remains provisional

The current geometry is a temporary, rough constraint. It may support generation, visualization and content review only with full disclosure. It may not become an official redline, statutory control, parcel/building conclusion or engineering commitment; official polygons trigger complete recomputation.
